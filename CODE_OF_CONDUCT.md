# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Keep discussion on the project, and credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful.
To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" link on the comment, issue or pull request. To reach
the maintainer privately, send a report through the repository's Security tab ("Report a vulnerability")
and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.
