# Legatum art review — 2026-11-16

Author review of `astra/art-pass-2`, based on `claude/calendar` at `4246569c5894`. **Provisional: lead review still required.** No push, no master merge, no rule/AI/UI changes. The only app-source edit routes the new Aztec raster crest in `app/src/data.ts`.

**Coverage:** 151 existing paintings: 54 unit miniatures, 12 portrait masters (including archived Japan), all 59 unlock paintings (35 buildings, 13 effects, 11 improvements), 20 late city paintings, and the three existing era paintings flagged/referenced by the earlier audit, plus three existing World War hamlet construction frames. Three existing SVGs were additionally reviewed. Missing asset families are listed separately, not counted as opened paintings.

**Initial findings on the 151 paintings:** 28 wrong, 30 poor, 7 minor, 86 judged fine. Counts are highest-severity findings per piece, not a claim that each piece has only one defect. The three SVGs add 1 wrong and 2 poor. Twelve missing-asset findings/families are separate below; eight now supplied, four wall families remain.

**Delivered:** 46 paintings (15 units, 3 portraits, 12 cities, 4 unlock/improvement paintings, 2 era scenes, 1 crest, 9 construction frames) and 6 SVGs. Fourteen of fifteen initial wrong figure/portrait findings received a correction; the barbarian archer is blocked. Some corrected pieces retain explicitly listed poor details. All thirteen other initial wrong existing paintings (Stock Exchange and twelve city copies/recolours/period mismatches) were replaced with their own paintings. All ten previous repaint jobs received work; job 3 remains partial because the four late-era wall families are not done. This is not an all-faults-cleared claim.

- [Phone index](art-pass-2/index.png); [group 1](art-pass-2/contact-01.png), [group 2](art-pass-2/contact-02.png), [group 3](art-pass-2/contact-03.png), [group 4](art-pass-2/contact-04.png), [group 5](art-pass-2/contact-05.png), [group 6](art-pass-2/contact-06.png), [group 7](art-pass-2/contact-07.png). Each changed piece also has `art-pass-2/pairs/<slug>.png`: old/new at 256 px, then new at native 48 px beside three labelled neighbours.
- [All units at 48 px](art-pass-2/all-units-48.png), all portraits at 48 px (archived outside the repository), icons at 16/24/48/128 (archived outside the repository). New art remains readable as subject silhouettes; short-pike and group-body scale limitations remain below.
- `art-pass-2/inventory.json` enumerates every figure master opened. Original full paintings and their quadrant crops were inspected: hands and held objects; entire weapon; shield/arm; feet/footwear; headgear/worn kit; anatomy; period; base, stance, camera/light; edge transparency; stray text. `inspection/` preserves the initial crop boards and unlock/city grids. `after-crops/` preserves accepted-generation detail boards. Each generated output, including rejected versions, was inspected before a choice was made; retained artifacts are the selected outputs, not all attempts.
- Portrait feet lie outside their compositions and are **not** asserted correct. Occluded shield grips, fingers, mechanisms and reins are explicitly not authenticated. Spearman and Drone Swarm only have a 256 px source; enlarged pixels do not prove detail. No clear accidental text/watermarks or extra limbs/fingers were found in retained figure art. Deliberate lambda symbols, decoration and intentional occlusion are not faults.
- Camera/light are broadly consistent across the miniature set; pose, height and base differences are called out individually. The new standing bodies are judged against the same set, not against a silhouette-score target. No miniature was mirrored or reposed to evade distinctness.
- Same Codex built-in image generation as the previous pass; no other paid image service. Exact requests in `art-pass-2/generation-*.json` and adjacent `*.art-pass-2.prompt.txt`. Standard `unit-art.py` and `wonder-art.py` pipelines rebuild figure/unlock derivatives. All twelve changed city paintings use the documented 512 px canvas, 476 px footprint width and y=455 ground edge; All three hamlet stage families share their respective parent’s exact transform. New era JPEG/PNG/portrait sizes and crest sizes follow the existing asset contracts.
