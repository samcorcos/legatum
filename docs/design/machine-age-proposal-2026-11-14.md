# New era between Industrial and Modern: proposal (2026-11-14)

Status: proposed to the user, awaiting his answer. His rulings so far are in `legatum-design` ("Open: the Industrial to Modern jump").

**Thesis.** The era is the pause before manoeuvre returns: defence is strong, armour is slow, aircraft scout and harass. Reaching it first gives an edge, not a walkover. Modern keeps Tanks, Fighters and Bombers at full strength.

**Name.** Machine Age (lead's pick). Alternatives: Electric Age, Imperial Era, Age of Steel, Great War.

**Technologies (five moved down from Modern).**
| Tech | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Replaceable Parts | Trench Infantry; Factories +2 Production (as today) |
| Steel | Howitzer (indirect fire); Eiffel Tower (as today) |
| Combustion | Reveals Oil; Landship; Oil Wells +1 Gold (as today) |
| Flight | Biplane; hangars |
| Electricity | Stock Exchange (third Gold building after Market and Bank) |

**Modern's five new technologies.** Mass Mobilisation (Infantry), Rocketry (Artillery), Armoured Warfare (Tank), Air Power (Fighter, Bomber), Electronics (Research Lab, moved from Electricity). New Modern wonder: Hoover Dam.

**Units.**
| Class | Industrial | Machine Age | Modern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Melee | Rifleman 61 | Trench Infantry 74 | Infantry 89 |
| Ranged | Field Cannon 50/65 | Howitzer 62/80, indirect fire | Artillery 77/98 |
| Mounted | Cavalry 79, 4 moves | Landship 86, 3 moves | Tank 94, 5 moves |
| Air | none | Biplane: strike 60, range 3, guards within 2, half damage to city HP, lifts fog within 4 of its city | Fighter 103 range 5; Bomber 114 range 8, +36 vs city HP |

- Biplane upgrades to a Fighter or a Bomber, the player's choice, once Air Power is researched (the one branching upgrade in the game).
- Oil is the resource of both eras (revealed by Combustion). No new resource.
- Howitzers hit what any unit or Biplane can see; spotting is the Biplane's main job. No new rule.
- Open: Trench Infantry with a larger Fortify bonus as the era's signature (verify how Fortify defends today first).
- Open: take the rules-31 +30% Modern cost back off, since the era now does that job.

**Knock-ons.** `ERA_COUNT` 8, rules version bump (old saves will not load), era-scaled factor about 1.39, Landship excluded from the Outrider rule, faction lines (Byzantium, Aztecs, Mongols) checked, era objective pool, World Ages cards, takeover painting, city art tier.

**Increments.** 1: playable era with tinted placeholder art (about a day, wall-clock; the release check is the bottleneck). 2: art, takeover, objective pool, Hoover Dam. 3: Hard's use of the era, tuned from his games.
