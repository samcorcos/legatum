Shared atlas specification

Transparent PNG, portrait 1024x1536, precise 3-column by 4-row grid for a premium historical
strategy map. Columns left-to-right are woodland, rolling hills and mountains; each row is a visibly
different variant. One centered, self-contained feature cluster per cell, with transparent margins.
Woodland cells use 3–5 separated readable trees with visible trunks, a mix of broadleaf and conifers;
the fourth woodland variant is sparse tundra conifers and frost scrub. Hills use 2–3 broad low rounded
mounds. Mountains use 2–3 bold asymmetric connected peaks with ivory snow caps and cool shadow faces.
Keep each cluster under 55% of its cell, leaving at least 40% of the implied hex as open ground.
Feature silhouettes and colors must be instantly distinct. No grid lines, ground plate, hex tile,
full scene, text, symbols, glossy gradients or detached cast shadow. Preserve genuine transparency.

Reference: docs/design/terrain-art/reference/civ6-reference-user.webp

S1 · Painted toy

Apply restrained soft dimensional painted landforms, believable and refined, never cute or cartoon.
Use muted sage and deep pine for trees, warm ochre and olive for hills, ivory snow with slate/umber
rock shadows for mountains. Soft painted volume with subtle edge definition. No black outline, glossy
gradient, miniature landscape or detailed scenery.

S2 · Flat illustrated

Apply a refined modern illustrated board-game style: clean flat pigment areas, crisp elegant silhouettes
with one muted shade tone per object and understated fine ink edges. Use muted sage-green canopies,
dark pine undersides, sage and ochre hills, and ivory snow with cool slate/umber mountain shadow.
Simple solid shapes, controlled ink edge, one shade plane. No texture or cartoon.

S3 · Watercolour sprite

Apply very sparse organic watercolor pigment shapes with a delicate light sepia ink contour, elegant
parchment-compatible earth colors, atmospheric but crisp at small game-map scale. Use muted greens for
woodland, ochre and sage for hills, ivory snow and subdued blue-grey/umber shadow washes for mountains.
Keep watercolor controlled within each shape with slight organic edge variation. No sprawling paint,
heavy outline, dense fine detail, glow or gradient.
