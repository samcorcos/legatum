# Germany identity: Fable's research and candidates (2026-11-05)

Research highlights (sources in the session transcript): Free Imperial Cities and the Hanse; guilds, Meister and the Mittelstand; Moltke's railways (1870 mobilisation in ~20 days); the Dreyse needle gun and Krupp cast steel; the Ruhr as coal plus iron plus rail. Game precedents: Civ 6 Germany (Hansa: a placement puzzle, top tier), Civ 5 Germany (a random early bonus and a Panzer-era payoff: called weak and incoherent, the trap Legatum is in), Civ 7 Prussia (railways as identity), Humankind Germans (Coking Works, "one-note"), AoE2 Teutons ("slow and strong is a boring identity": the −1 move), TI4 Clan of Saar (production as a rule break about where you produce).

Candidates, Fable's ranking:
1. **Hellweg / railway army:** +2 moves starting on a road (+3 after Railways), cheaper Roadworks; −1 move off roads until Industrialization.
2. **Krupp Works:** replaces the Forge at +6 Production (approved by the designer 2026-11-05 as the Forge-slot Ruhr Foundry).
3. **Stands alone:** Germany cannot sign Alliances (Bismarck's "nightmare of coalitions"). Rivals can ally against it; it cannot answer in kind.
4. **Zeughaus:** half-price upgrades, upkeep 2 Gold a unit.
5. **Made in Germany:** buildings and units cost 20% less Production, but Germany can never buy anything with Gold.
6. Kontor (Markets earn Gold per nearby foreign city at Peace). 7. Gutenberg (Library +1 Science per Workshop-family building). 8. Wirtschaftswunder (losing a city gives the rest +50% Production for 10 turns).

Fable would ship 1 + 2 and hold 3 as the drawback in reserve. Risk flagged: the AI must value Roadworks early or the census calls the railway rule dead for the wrong reason.
