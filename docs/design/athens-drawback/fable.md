# Athens drawback: Fable's candidates (2026-11-05)

Ranking (full reasoning in the session transcript; summary here):

1. **Greece Captive.** Capturing an Athenian city that has a Lyceum teaches the captor one technology Athens knows (in the captor's current era; nothing when the captor is ahead). History: Sulla shipped Aristotle's library to Rome; "Graecia capta ferum victorem cepit". The Swiss-bounty pattern: the bigger Athens's lead, the juicier each city. Uses the capture hook and the free-tech picker. Risk: only bites when rivals actually capture Athenian cities. Census check (4,096 games, 562226396): Athens loses at least one city in 62% of seats, 1.44 a seat, so it would fire.
2. **Share the Learning reshaped.** The broadcast gives every rival one free technology per era Athens has entered (today a flat 3, fired at a median T164 when they barely matter). Firing late hands rivals a whole era and costs Athens the Most Advanced crown. Ship beside a standing drawback, not instead.
3. **Laurion.** Each Lyceum uses 1 Copper supply. Strong story (Laurion silver, Decelea 413 BC), but map luck, and Copper supplies nothing today.
4. **Ravaging of Attica.** An enemy military unit on an Athenian tile denies its yields (extends barbarian tile denial). Right lever, but Hard has no raiding play yet; overlaps the Germany "Ruhr occupation" proposal.
5. The Assembly Votes (Athenian declarations take effect next turn, target told). 6. The Plague (no growth with an enemy adjacent). 7. The Grain Fleet (no growth past 6 without a Market). 8. Ostracism (don't ship).

Recommendation: Greece Captive plus the reshaped goal.
