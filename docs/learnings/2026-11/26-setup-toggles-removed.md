## 2026-11-26: Game setup drops the turn limit, barbarians and AI alliances settings

- **Only the app changed.** New setups always send `turn_limit: null` (turn 500, most VP wins), `barbarians: true` and no `ai_alliances`. The core `GameSetup` fields, the hosted room format (`StagingConfig`) and the sim flags stay, so saves, tapes and in-flight rooms made with a custom last turn, barbarians off or AI alliances load and play as they were, and no `RULES_VERSION` bump is needed. Proxy benchmarks keep barbarians off for their calibration.
- **Teams are the only starting partnerships.** The Co-op preset no longer pairs the AI empires into Alliances; to have AI empires stand together, put them on one team. The Diplomacy note that reads `PlayerView.ai_alliances` stays for old saves.
- **Gotcha: a collapsed section hides inputs from Playwright.** The map seed moved under a closed "Advanced" `<details>`, so `getByLabel('Map seed').fill` times out. e2e specs use `e2e/mapSeed.ts::setMapSeed`, which opens Advanced the way a player does. Advanced opens by itself only when a seed you typed is invalid (`onChange && !seedOk`): a guest's read-only "Hidden" seed would otherwise force it open.
