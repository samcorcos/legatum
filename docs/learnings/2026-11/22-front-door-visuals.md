## 2026-11-22: The README's diagrams merged with no reviewer looking at them
- **Why nobody caught it.** `tools/gates/check.py` counts `README.md` and everything under `docs/` as docs, so a branch that changed only the README, `docs/media/` or the link card needed no reviewer at all, and the art reviewer's rubric only covered game art. Sam called the "What the AI can see and do" diagram "claude slop". The front door is now its own kind (`FRONT`: `README.md`, `docs/media/`, `app/public/og.jpg`) and needs both the docs and the art reviewer; legatum-art "Front-door media looks like the game" lists what to reject.
- **What the generic look is, so it can be named in a review:** eyebrow or kicker labels in small letter-spaced capitals, a feature strip of filler under the art, boxes-and-arrows cards, pill chips and numbered circles, cards of uneven height holding one line. The banner, link card and trailer end card all carried the kicker and the strip, made by `banner.mjs` and `captions.mjs`; the templates are what to fix, not the pictures.
- **Replacing a trailer's end card needs no re-recording.** The masters are not kept (the clips were recorded in a cloud container). Re-render the card with `captions.mjs`, keep the video up to the start of the old card's 0.6 s dissolve, hold its last frame for 0.6 s, `xfade` into the new card, and copy the audio. Both xfade inputs need the same frame rate and time base (`fps=N,settb=1/N`), or ffmpeg fails with "First input link main timebase do not match".
- **The end card's painted background had silently gone flat.** `captions.mjs` pointed at the full-size key art master, which is not in git, so a fresh checkout rendered the card on plain dark brown. It now falls back to the committed 1600 px copy.
