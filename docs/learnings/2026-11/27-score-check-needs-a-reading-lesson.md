# A score sheet is not a lesson (2026-11-27)

A first-time player with the advisor on reached the first Age score check and still did not know how to win. The
sheet showed every way to score and how to get the next point, but nothing said what a VP is, that each square is one,
or how the game ends; the only lesson on winning was a one-line card on turn 8 ("hold your target for 10 turns").

Fix: the advisor's "How to win" lesson moved to turn 3 and names the target, the hold, the turn-500 ending and the four
ways to score; and with the advisor on, the first two score checks carry a short note on reading the sheet.
Lesson: a dense summary screen needs a one-time "how to read this" for a new player, shown where the screen is, not
earlier somewhere else.
