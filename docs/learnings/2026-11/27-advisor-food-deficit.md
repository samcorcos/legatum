# Food deficit advice must respect the population floor

The city panel's net Food is gross Food minus two per population. A negative value draws down stored Food; when that reserve runs out, a city above population 1 loses one population. Population 1 is the floor, so a warning that says every starving city "will shrink" is false there.

Cities work their owned tiles automatically. Advice to "work a Food tile" suggests a control the player does not have. The advisor should point to an available Food building, project or improvement in the selected city's production list, and use different wording at population 1.
