# 2026-11-24: animated units in 3D (Codex lanes, Fable, Blender on the Mac)

- Codex lanes run in a macOS seatbelt sandbox: no Metal (Eevee aborts), no Chromium launch, and often no `index.lock` writes (the runner commits afterwards). Lanes model and check with Cycles CPU; the lead runs GPU renders and browser captures outside the sandbox. Claude/Fable agents on the Mac run outside it, so they can render and look at their own output: that tight loop made the biggest jumps in quality.
- Blender crashes ("quit unexpectedly" dialogs on the Mac's screen) came from Cycles converting a near-zero-scaled volume mesh to an OpenVDB grid (ScaleMap throws, SIGABRT). Hide effects with keyed visibility, never with scale 0. Every Blender call goes through one slot gate: background only (`-b --factory-startup`), a few CPU slots, one GPU render machine-wide via a mkdir lock, up to 2 retries after a crash.
- Duck feet on the first walk came from home-made retargeting (rest poses and bone rolls not matched, then foot rotations patched by hand). Use a real retargeter (Rokoko or Blender's own) on CMU or CC0 clips with heel/toe locks, and check feet from above and in front every round.
- The look that finally matched the painting: project the painting from its own camera, render with Eevee emission (no second lighting pass), paint unseen parts per part with colour ramps fitted to the painting's luminance, and reduce renders the way the painting was reduced (display-space Lanczos). Cycles plus denoise plus a grade was worse.
- Measure each round: silhouette IoU against the painting's alpha, and colour ΔE on 2 px-blurred images at zero offset, per region. Raw per-pixel ΔE is dominated by texture misregistration and cannot reach target even on a near-match; an offset search adds about 5 ΔE of edge error.
- Appending to queue files with a glob races the runner (a task already in `running/` is recreated in `pending/` without headers). Edit pending files by name, after checking they still exist. A moved task file keeps its queue-time mtime; judge a finished round by its output's mtime.
- BSD `find -mmin` takes only integers; `-mmin +0.5` errors and matched nothing, which silently blocked pushes for hours.
- GPU passes run one at a time (15-20 min) starved 24 lanes; run them every few rounds or only on the final round. A dirty GPU worktree blocked every later checkout: stash first, or render in the lane worktree that holds the branch.
- GitHub rejects pushes whose author is a private email; use the noreply address.
- Codex budget: 24 lanes burned roughly 30-46 points of the weekly meter per hour.
