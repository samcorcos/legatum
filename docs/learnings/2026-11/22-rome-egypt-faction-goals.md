## 2026-11-22: Rome's Colonia Victrix and Egypt's Royal Tomb (rules 54, `claude/project-thread-qamoox`)
- **A site "beside a rival's border" does not exist next to a young city.** No city may be founded within 3 tiles of another and a new city owns one ring, so the nearest rival-owned tile to any legal site is 3 away until that city claims its second ring. `COLONIA_BORDER_REACH` is 2, so the first engine test found no qualifying site on the standard test map; the fixture now grows the rival's land to ring 2. In play this means the Colonia needs a grown neighbour, which fits a goal that also needs a level-3 veteran.
- **Egypt's action could not be called "the levy".** `PlayerPublic::levy` already carries Byzantium's Thin levies (`LevyView`), so the first patch declared the field twice. The state is `tomb_work` / `tomb_turns` / `tomb_city`, the order `Action::Tomb { build }`, and the player-facing words "Build / Pause the Royal Tomb". Grep the view for a word before naming a rule after it.
- **The tomb's yield cut is the last line of the yield builder** (`economy.rs`, after the Nile and the end-of-tree Science conversion), so nothing added later can leak Production back in. It leaves a Food deficit alone, so a city under the work can still starve. Checked through `resolve_turn`: population and stored progress stand still for three turns.
- **A patch script that writes each file as it goes is not atomic.** One failed assertion left half the edits applied; check `git diff --stat` before re-running rather than assuming nothing landed.
- **Hard Rome never reaches level 3, so it never founds a Colonia (160-game all-Hard smoke, seed 9000).** Without a reserve every level-1 veteran became a city and no Roman unit reached 90 XP; keeping the best veteran back gave 2 seats in 160 a level-3 unit, and neither founded. Whenever a level-3 unit existed, a legal Colonia site existed (0% no-site turns), so sites are not the bottleneck: Rome's wars are (about half its seats are eliminated, 1.5 cities on average). The designer wins easily as Rome, so this is AI skill; the goal is not eased for it.
- **Why the level-3 veteran still did not found (trace of smoke games 95 and 118, `colonia` decision-trace rows).** Three AI mistakes and one core rule. (1) The AI's site search dropped every site flagged as border risk, and every site within 2 tiles of a rival's land carries that flag, so the AI never saw a Colonia site; it now searches with the risky sites included. (2) Founding needs full HP and the war passes moved the veteran every turn, so it sat at 86 to 96 HP and never healed; a wounded level-3 veteran with a site, free to leave and out of enemy reach now rests for the Colonia. (3) A veteran under the occupation lock was claimed as founder and its walk order dropped every turn; it is no longer a candidate. What is left is the lock itself: a veteran usually earns level 3 by taking a city and is then held there until Peace (35 of 55 level-3 seat-turns in the paired run). That needs the capture passes to spare the would-be founder, or Rome to seek Peace: a hill-climb job, not part of this change.
- **Keeping the founder out of the lock (round 3, same 320 seeds on the merged tree).** A guard at `Planner::unit_order` (`goal_colonia.rs::colonia_lock_guard`) catches any move by the would-be founder that ends in a city with `CityView::occupation_lock`: another free melee unit within one turn takes the move, else an enemy capture waits one turn (never two) when a unit is within 2 turns, else the founder captures. Guarding at the one place every pass's order goes through beats patching each capture pass; the guard must not hand away the founder's own Colonia walk, and a later pass must not send the founder in behind its substitute (both were bugs found in the run). Level-3 seat-turns with every veteran locked fell from 51 of 56 to 30 of 130; Colonias founded 0 to 1 (held 57 turns, and Rome won that game); Rome's captures per seat 1.93 to 1.76 (paired difference 0.17, interval 0.18 either way) and seats with a level-2 unit 35 to 25, so it is not free. Hard's Peace is decided by the plan (`wants_peace_why` returns "plan fights this enemy"), not by `peace_terms`, so making Rome seek Peace for a locked founder is a plan-level change and was left out. The master merges changed the seed-9000 games themselves (games 95 and 118 no longer produce a level-2 Roman), so a before and after needs a baseline on the same tree.
- **Paired run, goals on against `--rule-off rome_goal,egypt_goal` (320 all-Hard games per arm, seeds 9000 to 9319; `docs/sim-results/rome-egypt-goals-2026-11-22.md`).** Egypt 20.0% against 19.4% (paired 95% interval about 3.4 points either way), Rome 1.6% against 0.9% (1.5): neither goal moves win share measurably. Tomb: started in 173 of 320 seats (median turn 188), raised in 112 (turn 209), Egypt holds 84 at the end and a rival 24. Colonia: 5 seats reached level 3, 2 founded (turns 221 and 224), both held. Four games were won by 2 VP or less by an empire holding its own goal city, all Egypt, and Egypt also won all four with the goals off. The best to worst ratio (12.8 on, 21.0 off) is Rome's AI weakness, not the goals.
- **The tomb's length is not a balance lever.** At 10, 15 and 20 turns (160 games each) Egypt won 23.1%, 21.2% and 22.5%; the work starts in the same 81 seats, a longer count raises fewer tombs (35%, 32%, 26% of seats) and fewer of them fall to a rival (11, 8, 4). It stays 15.
- **Egypt's tomb under the first AI:** started in 51% of seats around turn 189, standing in 32% around turn 204, held at the end in 25%, captured by a rival in about 6%. Egypt's win share was 21 to 22% with the goal against 20.8% in a 48-game old-AI baseline: not visibly up, verdict needs the paired run.
- **The work takes only each turn's yield, not stored progress,** so a half-built item waits and resumes; the AI needs no "finish the Walls first" rule, only a garrison before it starts.
- **"Goal reached" telemetry counts a captor.** `faction_goal_turns` counts a player holding another faction's held goal as reaching its own (Rome showed "reached 2" without founding); this has always applied to the Hall, Diwan and Heir.
- **`legatum-sim` unit tests take over 58 minutes in debug on a shared 4-core container and about 4 in release.** Use `--release` for that suite. A debug `target/` for the workspace grew to 48 GB and filled the disk for two worktrees.
- **Held goals need no new scoring code.** `scoring::check_held_goal` already emits `FactionGoalCompleted` / `FactionGoalLost` when `goals::held_by` changes, so a new held goal is a `held_by` arm, a `reached` arm and a holder function.
