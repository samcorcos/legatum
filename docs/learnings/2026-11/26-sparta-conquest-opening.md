# Hard Sparta could not find its neighbour, so it never rushed

A recorded Hard game in which a Sparta player declared on the Celts on turn 12 with two Hoplites and a Scout, and eliminated three one-city rivals by turn 48, was replayed with the Sparta seat as Hard (`crates/sim/examples/offense_ladder.rs --tape`, 32 dice salts). Hard Sparta's first declaration came around turn 29 and it took a capital by turn 50 in 28% of runs. On random maps (300 games) it declared by turn 30 in only 32% of games.

The cause was not the assault but the search. Hard Sparta's Scout circled within about nine hexes of home and missed the Celtic capital nine hexes away; Sparta knew of no rival city until turn 25, so its campaign code had nothing to aim at and the capital built an Archer and a Settler instead of Hoplites. The Roman opening (march, finish, second war) already solves the commitment half; Sparta now plays it (`sparta_rome_opening`), declares with two melee units because Phalanx plus Level 1 makes a pair strong enough (`sparta_opening_ready`), and while no rival city is known it trains Hoplites and sends up to two spare ones to explore within ten hexes (`sparta_opening_search`). On the recorded map the first declaration moved to about turn 18 and the first capture to about turn 22; in 300 paired full games capitals taken by turn 50 went from 15% to 74%, VP share from 0.18 to 0.31 and wins from 22% to 37%.

Also seen in the recording: every eliminated rival (Celts, Mongols, Athens) was still on one city and one or two Spearmen when attacked, and the Mongols' Settler stood unfounded for ten turns. That is a defence and expansion problem, not an offence one.

Partial web recordings (`first_turn` > 1, `initial_state` as a JSON string) replay with `crates/sim/examples/partial_dump.rs RECORD.json OUTDIR`; `game_dump` replays from the setup and cannot read them.

All numbers above were measured on rules 62. City upkeep (rules 63, `26-city-upkeep-catch-up.md`) charges wide empires, and Sparta fell from 14.0% to 9.3% all-AI win rate under it; a Sparta that conquers early will pay it sooner. Re-measured on rules 63, 64, 66 and 67 in `27-sparta-opening-under-city-upkeep.md`: the opening through turn 50 is unchanged, and on rules 67 wins go from 9.8% to 16.3% (paired +6.5 ± 3.6 points, 600 games) instead of 22% to 37%.
