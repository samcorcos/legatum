# A faction-sheet stat must be able to wrap its comparison (2026-11-25)

India's three-card faction sheet at 1440x900 gives each card's text column 156px, and the War Elephant's "50 strength (Knight: 42)" with its arrow needs 166px. `.fs-sheet .pc-stat` was `white-space: nowrap` with no flex wrap, so the arrow spilled past the card and `app/e2e/piece-compare.spec.ts` failed in the nightly. The stat now wraps, and the standard item's number and its arrow sit in one `.pc-diff` group so the arrow never lands on a line by itself. The stat hangs a wrapped comparison under the value (a 19px left padding that the icon pulls back with a negative margin), so it lines up with the number instead of falling back under the icon. Any longer stat label or comparison on a three-card sheet is the case to check.
