## 2026-11-27: Measure delegated economics and pause after a lesson

- Economic automation needs marginal payoff, not just a high score for a building category. The actual-core experiment exposed an Egyptian growth loop: valuing extra Nile build capacity as permanently useful encouraged population 19 with only 338 cumulative city Gold. Valuing its fallback at the real Gold-project conversion rate corrected that case. See [experiment and limitations](../../sim-results/2026-11-27-city-economy.md); these controlled cases do not establish faction balance or global optimality.
- Teaching a convenience is not finished when its card disappears. In the 1000×600 browser tour, learning Automate immediately exposed a different lesson over the same control. Optional discovery lessons pause for the rest of the turn after learning or acknowledging one; core build, move, research and end-turn teaching remains available. Advisor positioning must keep the active control clear instead of silencing core guidance. Test the real control after using it, not only the card's initial visibility.
