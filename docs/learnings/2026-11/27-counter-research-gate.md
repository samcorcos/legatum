# A cheaper counter unlock did not close the late gap

Hard can prioritize the cheapest legal technology that unlocks a matching counter to a visible outclassing rival unit. The strict era gate remains: if no counter is available now, the candidate advances through the cheapest remaining current-era technology. It changes research order, not Science income or training.

In a 300-pair six-seat duel on public base `82a0e544` (seed 7201), the candidate won 45 games versus 47 at baseline: −0.67 percentage points, paired 95% bootstrap CI [−3.00, +1.67]. VP share fell from 0.1683 to 0.1658 (−0.0025). In 32 paired stand-in branches from T150, leader-bloc captures rose from 3.75 to 4.34 and rival cities remaining fell from 14.34 to 13.41. The gene stays default 0. Research order alone cannot fix low Science output or fielded units that remain several eras old.
