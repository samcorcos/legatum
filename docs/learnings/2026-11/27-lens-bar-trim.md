# Map lens bar trim

Map lens usage is not captured in game telemetry. The controls kept in the bar are the ones observed in use: Default, Settle and Yields. The bar now places the two exclusive lenses beside the Yields overlay in one row.

The move preview and Default reach wash convey movement. Fog and the ruin marks already expose discovery. Tile hover, combat previews and map units convey known threats. Resource icons remain on every map, and border lines remain visible without an overlay. Settle keeps its site scale, ranked recommendations and Colonia marks. Yields continues to stack over either lens and to remember its setting.
