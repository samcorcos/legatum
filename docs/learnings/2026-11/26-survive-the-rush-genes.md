# Surviving an early rush: what a Hard seat can and cannot buy (2026-11-26)

Context: in real uploaded games, 58% of Hard empires still had only their starting unit at turn 20, and humans who took an AI city by turn 60 almost always faced a smaller army. Five genes were written to test the obvious fixes (`params.rs`; `production.rs`, `capitals.rs`, `runaway.rs`). They were measured with `crates/sim/examples/rush_ladder.rs`: one seat plays the arm against five built-in Hard seats on paired seeds (6 players, factions drawn from all eleven, seat = seed mod 6). `--mode rush` makes the nearest rival a scripted rusher (`legatum_wasm::rusher`, war from turn 12, storm at 3 units) and plays to turn 100. `--mode duel` plays a full game. `--tape` plays a recorded game's map and lobby with every seat Hard.

- **Why Hard has one unit at turn 20:** the city starts a Spearman around turn 7. The modernisation reconsider (`modernization_value` > 0 re-chooses a combat unit under way every turn) then swaps it for a Settler at turn 10. Under attack it flips between Walls, Archer and Spearman, so nothing finishes. Banked progress is kept, but each item finishes later.
- **Shipped: `keep_unit_build` 2.** A combat unit under way may be replaced only by Walls or, once it holds Production, by a later era's unit. Rusher test (300 seeds): capital lost by T100 70.0% to 66.0% [-7.7, -0.3 points], cities lost 1.59 to 1.31, army at T20 1.86 to 2.24. Full game (400 seeds): win 14.8% to 15.0%, VP share flat, alive 69.0% to 70.5%, cities at T40 -0.15 [-0.23, -0.07]. On three recorded Rome maps (32 dice salts, every seat Hard), Hard Rome's cities at T100 moved 6.3/3.9/5.1 to 5.6/3.1/5.7, within noise.
- **Level 1 is a trap.** Blocking the switch to Walls as well cut rusher capital losses much more (70% to 57%), but in full games the seat was alive less often (-5.5 points). Walls matter later even if a unit matters more against a turn-12 rush.
- **Rejected: `war_footing` 1** (any enemy unit within 8 hexes stops non-military builds). Rusher -6 points, but full-game win 14.8% to 7.0% and VP share -4.5 points: it fires during the seat's own wars. Level 2 (only in a war declared on us, threat above local defence) is harmless and does nothing measurable. Both stay off.
- **Rejected: `early_army` 3** (2 units from T3, 3 from T12). Rusher -6 points, but in full games it costs cities (T40 -0.21, T100 -0.17) and capitals are lost slightly more often (+6 points). An always-on floor buys units at the cost of Settlers. This repeats the 2026-11-22 hill-climb result for always-on defence floors.
- **Rejected: `capital_keep`** (hold the last unit in the capital, recall one when it is empty, with an enemy within 6). It adds nothing on top of the others (54.0% to 56.7%). The existing `capital_guard` never fires in this test.
- **Inconclusive: `capital_coalition`** (a Hard neighbour at Peace declares on an empire holding another's original capital that captured a city in the last 15 turns). With every seat on, more empires declare on the rusher (0.72 to 0.89 per game), but the victim's capital falls first in a turn-12 rush (no change). Its target is a late runaway conqueror, which this test does not measure. Off.
- **Test caveat:** `--tape` without fresh dice repeated one game 32 times. `rush_ladder` now reseeds `dice_seed` per salt, as `hill_suite` does.
