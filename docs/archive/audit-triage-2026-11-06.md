# Audit triage, 2026-11-06

## Art briefs (ranked by on-screen and trailer impact)

All unit briefs follow the units README prompt convention and the miniature rules in `legatum-art`. Each is a refined hand-painted miniature in painterly realism with muted parchment-compatible pigments and soft diffuse studio light. It is one complete centred subject on a round neutral pale-stone base, with a foot, hoof or track planted on it. The background is transparent with generous margins (generated on flat magenta and keyed with `tools/art/unit-art.py`). There is no owner colour, text, scenery, national insignia or thin antennas. The source is 1254 px; the 256/64/48, card and panel derivatives are rebuilt from it, and each is checked in greyscale at 45 px against same-era units. These also complete the held review-plan items A7 and A8, which never ran (`done/review-art-unit-sources.md`, `done/review-art-keshig-base.md`).

1. **Spearman, full-resolution master.** It is the first military unit every player builds and opens every war in the trailer, yet its source is 256 px, so every larger use is upscaled. Repaint the existing Ancient Greek foot soldier at 1254 px and keep the subject and pose so no derivative changes silhouette. He stands upright, spear held vertical in his right hand with the point well inside the canvas. The round bronze shield on his left arm carries a restrained sun boss. He wears a Corinthian helmet with a red crest, a linen corselet and greaves, and both feet are planted on a full-size pale-stone base. Keep the warm bronze, red cloth and linen palette, the weight of a real hoplite and a calm ready stance. Match the Archer and Pikeman masters in camera height (slightly above, three-quarter front), light direction and base size, with no extra figures or ground clutter.
2. **Tank, complete high-resolution master.** The current 256 px source is cut by a vertical edge through the rear hull and base, and its coarse dark surface differs from the cleaner set. Paint a complete modern main battle tank in three-quarter view, turret slightly traversed toward the viewer, with a long cannon. The full rear hull, both tracks and the whole circular pale-stone base must sit inside generous margins. Use muted olive and earth tones with worn paint, track mud and crisp panel lines. Match Autonomous Armour's camera, lighting and base alignment, but keep a distinct silhouette: a heavier, lower hull with a classic turret. No antennas, national markings, numbers or scenery.
3. **Keshig, grounded on its base.** The Mongols' signature unit currently floats above a separate stone disc with a visible gap, which breaks the miniature rule ("a figure floating beside a small disc fails"). Edit the existing 1254 px master and keep the rider: he draws a recurved bow forward, in a blue deel with ochre trim and a fur-trimmed hat. Keep the compact bay steppe pony at full gallop and the low, fast silhouette. Repose the gallop so one hoof clearly strikes an integrated pale-stone base with a little trampled steppe earth. Carry the motion in the extended legs, mane and tail rather than by lifting the whole horse. Match the Horseman's and Cuirassier's base size, camera and lighting, keep every limb and the whole bow inside the canvas, and keep real transparency. Also correct the units README, which still says the rider turns back (the Parthian shot): the painting draws forward.
4. **Drone Swarm, full-resolution master.** It is the Future-era ranged unit and appears in late-game footage, but its source is 256 px, its three drones hover with no visible tie to the base, and a stray clipped fragment sits at the left edge. Paint three compact realistic quadcopter combat drones in a tight rising echelon above one display stand: a single slim dark-metal post on the pale-stone base, as the aircraft miniatures stand. Use dark gunmetal with olive accents, one small sensor ball under each, and motion-blurred rotor discs drawn as soft translucent ellipses, not thin lines. The whole group must sit inside generous margins with nothing clipped. Avoid insect or sci-fi styling and glowing effects. Keep it distinct from the Hunter-Killer and the aircraft at 45 px.
5. **Moment takeover check (no new painting).** The three 16:9 takeover paintings exist (`moment-takeover-founded/captured/greatwork`, 1672×941). Before wiring (next-batch item 1), compose each under the real card at 1000×600 and 1440×900. If the card hides the subject, regenerate only that painting with the subject moved to the free third. Keep the same scenes and palette, and the founding and Great Work scenes' negative space.
6. **Germany Industry project, Gold** (only if question 10 keeps it). Show productive commerce: manufactured goods weighed and crated on a loading dock beside a ledger, a brass balance and a modest stack of coins. Give it a broad, low silhouette, warm brass and muted umber, and a soft ground blend. Use the same isometric miniature camera and painterly materials as the Workshop and Market unlock art, with ongoing activity, since the project never finishes. No heap of gold, currency symbols, readable text or insignia.
7. **Germany Industry project, Science** (only if question 10 keeps it). Show a compact industrial research bench: a precision lathe, an optical instrument and an open drawing folio with blank technical plates. Give it a taller, asymmetric silhouette so it reads apart from the Gold project at 64 px. Use muted steel, parchment and restrained blue accents in the same light and ground blend. No glowing machinery, readable equations or generic flask icon.
