# Swiss Reserve valuation (2026-11-06)

## Diagnosis

The current Reserve is **5,000 Gold**, not 1,000; the 1,000 threshold belonged to older playtests. The rule is live: it gives 2 VP only while the treasury remains at or above 5,000 after upkeep. A captured Swiss city transfers a share of the treasury to the captor and can remove those points. The rule and its capture bounty stay unchanged.

Sources: the 4,096-game Hard AI-only control at `~/LegatumRuns/cloud/ablate-athens-control-1105-225238` and its paired `--rule-off swiss_goal` arm at `~/LegatumRuns/cloud/ablate-switzerland-swiss_goal-1105-232443`, seeds 9311003 onward, 3,610 paired Swiss seats. Recompute the figures with [diagnose.py](diagnose.py). Both runs use engine `24b39619-dirty`. Their Swiss seat win counts are 388/3,610 (10.75%) with the goal and 434/3,610 (12.02%) without: 225 control-only wins and 271 rule-off-only wins. The paired rule-off advantage is +1.27 pp, 95% interval [+0.06, +2.49] from the [ablation report](../../faction-ablation/switzerland-2026-11-05/README.md).

`victory.rs::saving_for_goal` starts the full 5,000 Gold reserve once current net income * the expected remaining turns reaches the target and there are at least two cities. It does not wait for the Gold to be available, for a near-term score, or for 2 VP to matter in the race. `scripted.rs::ordinary_gold_reserve` then refuses routine purchases below 5,000. `victory.rs::plan_victory` independently protects that threshold. At War, `hoard_at_war=2` can release the unfinished reserve only while the routed plan fights an active war; a door rescue and the decisive Bomber path also have exceptions. Production orders continue, and `swiss_market_priority=300` values Markets even more while the goal is pending.

The old income/horizon condition would be true for 7.4% of goal-on Swiss seats observed at T50, 61.5% at T100, and 72.3% at T150 (among seats still alive at each snapshot). These are snapshots of the income condition, not the first saving turn: the artifacts have no per-turn reserve trace, and a held goal also qualifies independently. The paired control games end at median T183 (mean T185), far short of the 300-turn horizon used by the old saving condition; a Reserve first reached around T151 has little time to decide a ten-turn VP hold.

The census records completed items and total Gold spent, but not each declined purchase or its turn. The code identifies the blocked paths; the table shows their aggregate consequences. It cannot attribute the 0.78 fewer military units entirely to purchases versus Production choices.

In the 271 paired seats that win only with the rule off, the Swiss player spent 6,183 Gold on average versus 5,442 with the goal, and finished with 9.79 versus 6.57 cities; completed military units were 37.34 versus 36.76, Workshops 6.67 versus 6.14, and Walls 7.16 versus 6.65. These numbers describe outcomes selected for a rule-off win: extra cities may cause the higher spend and construction rather than result from them. The large treasury difference (peak 1,293 versus 3,942) identifies the Gold kept out of play.

The treasury reached 5,000 at some point in 833/3,610 goal-on seats (23.1%); 263 of these ended below 5,000. Of those 263, 200 also lost a city, consistent with capture bounty exposure, but the artifacts do not assign each drop to bounty versus spending or income. Among the 388 goal-on Swiss wins, 188 finished with at least 5,000; in 115 of those, final VP was below 12, so removing 2 VP from that final position would put the seat below the 10 VP target. Only 59 of those 115 were control-only wins in the paired rule-off game. Meanwhile, 95 of the 271 rule-off-only winners reached 5,000 in their control game: even receiving the VP did not recover the opportunity cost in those pairs. The 115 and 59 are **potentially decisive final-score counts**, not causal counts: scores and holds could change if the goal never existed. The simulator's `faction_goal_turns` field includes goals captured from other factions; its T151 median and 25% figure in the ablation report are not an exact Swiss Reserve attainment clock. The treasury threshold itself is the reliable count here.

## Candidate and gates

The prototype gene `swiss_hoard_value` defaulted to 0. At 1, the unfinished Reserve's delayed 2 VP was discounted by turns to reach 5,000 and by its relevance to the current VP leader, then compared with a currently buyable option's immediate investment value. If the Reserve won, the AI saved; otherwise Gold followed the ordinary purchase reserve. A Reserve already held remained protected. Levels 2 and 3 also kept 1,000 or 2,000 Gold of interest-earning working capital, released for a routed active war. The gene-off path reproduced the old behavior.

The first level-1 pilot (before the `saving_wins` and Great Work paths were routed through the value comparison) reduced the hoard but lost more games: 388/3,610 to 352/3,610 on seed 9311003, paired −1.00 pp [−2.05, +0.17]; 374/3,616 to 360/3,616 on held-out seed 8241001, −0.39 pp [−1.49, +0.72]. Its T150 treasury fell from about 1,800–1,900 to 770 Gold and net income from 58–60 to 50. Removing the 5,000 reserve without valuing interest capital gives up too much compounding. Raw pilot comparisons: [9311003](pilot-9311003.json), [8241001](pilot-8241001.json).

The corrected levels 2 and 3 also lost on seed 9311003: level 2, 388 to 329 wins in 3,610 Swiss seats, paired −1.63 pp [−2.77, −0.50]; level 3, 388 to 299, −2.47 pp [−3.60, −1.33]. Level 2's T150 treasury averaged 873 Gold (control 1,822); level 3's averaged 1,059. Keeping interest capital alone does not repair the purchase choices that `goal_pending()` still vetoes. Raw comparisons: [level 2](level-2-9311003.json), [level 3](level-3-9311003.json). Both levels remain off.

The separate `swiss_market_priority=0` diagnostic kept the old reserve and removed only the extra 300 production score for Markets. It also lost: 388 to 344 wins in 3,610 seats, paired −1.22 pp [−2.24, −0.17]; Markets completed fell from 4.35 to 4.14 and treasury peak from 2,940 to 2,870 Gold. That bonus is useful, not the cause of the backwards ablation ([paired report](market-0-9311003.json)).

Level 4 additionally prices the readiness defender, planned Great Work purchase and Market production bonus through the goal-value comparison. It also lost on the corrected eight-Hard, 4,096-game seed 9311003 census: 388 to 332 wins in 3,610 Swiss seats, paired −1.55 pp [−2.66, −0.44] ([paired report](level-4-9311003.json)). The unchanged gene-off binary reproduces the original control exactly, winner for winner, on all 4,096 seeds ([determinism comparison](default-regression-9311003.json)). Level 4 remains off.

The level-2 human-seat gate used `tools/sim/quality_gate.py::compare` on the same binary, 256 reseeded salts across eight maps (2,048 games per arm, seed 310001). Control human wins were 472; rivals-only was 461 (AI strength +0.54 pp, simultaneous interval [−0.67,+1.74]) and seat-only was 468 (AI strength −0.20 pp [−0.91,+0.51]). Both were noninferior on that gate, but level 2 still fails the AI-only Swiss win-share test. The gate cannot rescue a losing faction candidate ([report](gate-2.json)).

The next candidate, `swiss_hoard_defense`, changes only the readiness purchase veto: a visible threatened Swiss city may buy its defender before banking the 5,000 Gold. The existing readiness forecast still prices the quote, upkeep, local threat and held-goal floor. This tests whether defending a city and avoiding the capture bounty is worth more than delaying the unfinished 2 VP, while leaving the useful interest-saving behavior in place.

This broad readiness exception is not a reliable win. On seed 9311003 it fell from 388 to 352 Swiss wins, paired −1.00 pp [−1.88, −0.14] ([report](defense-9311003.json)); on held-out seed 8241001 it rose from 374 to 397, +0.64 pp [−0.25, +1.52] ([report](defense-8241001.json)). The human-seat gate on the same binary found seat-only AI strength +0.05 pp [−0.64,+0.73], noninferior; rivals-only −0.39 pp [−1.51,+0.73], inconclusive against the −1.5 pp floor ([report](gate-defense.json)). The split AI-only result and inconclusive rivals gate do not justify a Swiss builtin default.

The narrow `swiss_hoard_land_buy` candidate extends the existing `decisive_buy` siege counterfactual to Swiss land units. It releases unfinished Reserve Gold only when the bought unit's added capture odds times live VP exceed the delay cost of reaching 5,000 Gold by at least the configured margin. The held 2 VP, deficit runway and upkeep check remain. It was mixed: 388 to 369 wins on seed 9311003, paired −0.53 pp [−1.30,+0.30], and 374 to 379 on held-out 8241001, +0.14 pp [−0.64,+0.91]. The primary gene-off binary reproduces the original control exactly across 4,096 games ([primary](land-9311003.json), [held-out](land-8241001.json), [gene-off comparison](default-regression-land-9311003.json)). Its human-seat gate found seat-only AI strength −0.24 pp [−0.85,+0.37], noninferior, and rivals-only −0.73 pp [−1.73,+0.26], inconclusive ([report](gate-land.json)). The land gene stays off.

Since removing `swiss_market_priority` also hurt, we screened higher Market production priority (450 and 600 against the current 300). At 450, Swiss wins were 388 to 389 on seed 9311003, paired +0.03 pp [−0.83,+0.89]; at 600 they fell to 370, −0.50 pp [−1.55,+0.55]. Higher priority completed slightly more Markets but did not establish a win ([450](market-450-9311003.json), [600](market-600-9311003.json)). These variants remain off.

Gene `swiss_hoard_any_war` releases an unfinished, nonwinning Reserve as the ordinary purchase reserve when Switzerland is actually at War, including when the routed plan says Peace. The existing `hoard_at_war=2` releases it only when the routed plan targets a live war. The held goal and a hoard that wins remain protected. This tests whether a plan label is blocking useful war spending.

The failed `swiss_hoard_value`, `swiss_hoard_defense`, and `swiss_hoard_land_buy` prototype implementations were removed from the final AI source after measurement. Their parameter files and paired reports here preserve the experimental settings; the branch's intermediate commits preserve the implementation used to build those binaries.

## Selected change

The actual-war release improved Swiss wins on both eight-Hard, 4,096-game censuses. Seed 9311003: 388 to 404 wins in 3,610 paired Swiss seats, +0.44 pp [−0.66,+1.52]. Held-out seed 8241001: 374 to 422 in 3,616 seats, +1.33 pp [+0.25,+2.35]. The gene-off binary reproduced the original seed-9311003 control in every one of 4,096 games ([primary](any-war-9311003.json), [held-out](any-war-8241001.json), [gene-off comparison](default-regression-any-war-9311003.json)). All three runs had zero panics.

On the primary census, the gene raised Swiss Gold spent from 3,831 to 3,969 per seat, total completed Production value from 9,674 to 9,935, and final cities from 5.0 to 5.1. T150 Gold fell from 1,822 to 1,405 and Swiss treasuries that ever crossed 5,000 fell from 833 to 578. Military units completed changed little (29.52 to 29.27); the Gold is buying earlier timing and a mix of purchases rather than simply a larger final army. The held-out direction was similar: spent Gold 3,827 to 3,962, completed Production 9,695 to 10,019, cities 5.0 to 5.2, and T150 Gold 1,943 to 1,488. This is consistent with better returns from releasing Gold during war, though the census does not log individual buy vetoes.

The 256-salt, eight-map human-seat gate (2,048 games per arm, seed 310001) passed with `tools/sim/quality_gate.py::compare`. Control human wins were 472. Rivals-only was also 472 (AI strength 0.00 pp, simultaneous interval [−1.15,+1.15], noninferior); seat-only was 480 (AI strength +0.39 pp [−0.30,+1.08], noninferior) ([gate report](gate-any-war.json)). Gene `swiss_hoard_any_war` retains default 0; the Swiss builtin sets it to 1. No faction rule changed. The primary AI-only interval still includes zero, so its estimated gain alone is uncertain; the held-out gain and noninferior gate support the builtin.
