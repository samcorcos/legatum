# AI research backlog

This file holds method-level ideas for making the Hard AI at least as good as the user (the north star). Gene tweaks don't belong here. The daily AI research director (scheduled task `legatum-ai-research`) owns it: it adds ideas, ranks them by expected gain per cost, runs the cheap ones, and moves results to `docs/sim-results/hard-vs-human/journal.md`. Each morning it sends the user the top three bets, plus anything that needs a decision.

Status values: idea, testing, adopted, rejected (with the reason).
