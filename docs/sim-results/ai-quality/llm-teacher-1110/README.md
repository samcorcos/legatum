# LLM teacher experiment: results (run 2026-11-07, Luna plain only)

Design: `docs/design/ai-strategy/fable-decision-models-2026-11-07.md` §3 and §5. Branch `claude/llm-teacher`.

## What was run

- **Designer decisions:** Luna via Codex only (`claude -p` is not logged in, so Haiku and Sonnet did not run). Plain variant only: his playtest notes were not sent to OpenAI (`--notes-models haiku`; no `notes` call exists in `calls.jsonl`).
- **Smoke:** Luna answered `NO_TOOLS`, so it cannot read files. Haiku: "OAuth session expired".
- **Referee:** the round-4 committed-executor logs (`operation-search-1107/round4-executor`, copied to `~/LegatumRuns/llm-teacher-1110/`). Window selection at 16 samples, confirmation of each window's best at 64 samples. The India T148 to T152 rows carry no operation list (strategist-only windows); `score` now skips referee rows without `operations`.
- **Windows:** 22 (Sparta T18 to T30, Swiss T30 to T40, India T13 to T22 and T148 to T152). Chooser and proposer ran on the 18 with a candidate list; the strategist ran on all 22. 3 repeats, option order shuffled per repeat.
- **Cost:** 174 Luna calls in this run (54 chooser, 54 proposer, 66 strategist), 3 to 6 s each, about 722k tokens in all (chooser 234k, proposer 268k, strategist 220k), 3 minutes wall time with 4 workers, plus 18 smoke-test calls earlier. $0 extra under the ChatGPT subscription. Raw calls: `calls.jsonl`.

Honest limits: the referee is thin. In round 4 only 3 of 18 windows have a confirmed winner, the confirmed gains on most windows straddle zero, and Hard's plan is often one of the options, so regret against it is noisy. Consistency at 22% is the cleanest signal: with shuffled order, Luna picks a different top option most of the time, which a referee cannot rescue.

## What would settle the open rows

1. **Proposer:** add an extra-operations input to `operation_search` and price the 45 proposals at 64 samples, starting with the 24 distinct ones. Success is two confirmed at +10 pp that the generator lacked.
2. **Strategist:** needs the designer's approval to send the notes to OpenAI, or the Haiku route working from a normal terminal, plus the takeover-turn windows.

## What exists

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| State renderer (Rust, integer only) | `crates/sim/examples/teacher_render.rs` |
| Harness (render, smoke, run, score) | `tools/llm-teacher/teacher.py`, tests in `tools/llm-teacher/tests/` |
| Static prompt parts | `tools/llm-teacher/rules.md` (the rules the model needs), `tools/llm-teacher/notes.md` (his reasoning, 20 long lines condensed from `docs/playtests/human-reasoning.md`) |
| The three tapes' inputs | `tools/llm-teacher/games.1107.json` (tape, hash-verified states and operation-search log per game; the paths are `~/LegatumRuns/llm-teacher-1110/...`, expanded by the harness, and are not in the repo) |

**Renderer.** `snap` takes `&PlayerView` only; the true state is dropped the moment the view is built, so a fogged garrison or an unseen city cannot reach a prompt. It names places ("2 tiles south-west of Karakorum, on grassland"; compass from integer hex-to-plane geometry), lists the seat's cities, grouped units, rivals' public numbers and known cities with distances to your cities, visible foreign units, wars and offers, and a view-diff events digest (cities appearing or changing hands, hp lost, Walls finished, items finished, units gained or lost, new foreign units, relation changes, VP and city-count moves, offers). The candidate list comes from the operation-search log, named exactly as the log names them, each with one line on what the executors do. The designer's recorded orders for the window (`tape`) go in the output for scoring and are never in a prompt (a unit test checks the prompt has none of the referee's numbers or the tape digest).

**Harness.**
- Routes: Haiku via `claude -p --model haiku` (no tools, no session persistence, no user settings, empty scratch dir, cleaned environment); Luna via `codex exec -s read-only -m gpt-6-luna` in an empty scratch dir with the shell, apps, plugins, computer use and browser features disabled.
- Roles: chooser (pick and rank the shuffled candidates), proposer (up to three operations off the list, in the generator's grammar, out-of-grammar ideas counted and dropped), strategist (posture, attack and settle-away targets, Peace and Alliance stance).
- Variants: `plain` and `notes`, 3 repeats, option order shuffled per (window, repeat) so both models and variants see the same order at a repeat.
- Every call is appended to `calls.jsonl`: id, window, role, variant, model, repeat, prompt hash, option order, full response, parsed result, latency, tokens where the CLI reports them, error. Runs resume (recorded calls are skipped).
- **Privacy flag:** `--notes-models` (default `haiku`). The `notes` variant is never built for a model outside it, so Luna runs `plain` only. To send the notes to Luna after the lead decides: `--notes-models haiku,luna`.
- Scoring (`score --referee LOG.jsonl ...`): agreement with the search's confirmed best (only windows whose confirmed gain has its interval above zero, and all windows), paired gain of the pick against Hard's plan (the log's gain on the same determinized samples, so common random numbers), regret to the best, agreement with his tape (heuristic label from his orders: declared or marched on a city, founded, accepted Peace, Walls; `other` is excluded), consistency across repeats and mean rank correlation, proposer yield (in-grammar, dropped, distinct new, already priced by the search and confirmed at +10 pp with the interval clear of zero), a strategist table, latency, and the section 5.5 verdict (a chooser verdict needs at least 5 clear windows).

## Known gaps

- Proposals the search has not priced cannot be confirmed yet: `score` writes `proposals-for-search.json`, and `operation_search` needs an extra-operations input to run them (not added; that branch is not mine to edit).
- The strategist windows are the same 22 review windows, not the takeover turns T2/T20/T40/T60/T80 of the training tapes; matching to his stated reasons (10 of 15) is by hand.
- Units of Sparta render as `Soldier` (the engine's kind; the Hoplite is a Spartan Soldier), and the faction kit text covers Sparta, Switzerland and India only.
