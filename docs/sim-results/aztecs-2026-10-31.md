# Aztecs functionality, 2026-10-31

Implements the designer's 2026-10-10 trial decision and 2026-10-31 instruction to start. This is a functionality check, not balance evidence.

Flower Wars rewards surviving melee participation against empire military units, excluding Scouts and barbarians, once per victim per Aztec owner. Nearest city is measured from the victim; lower city ID breaks ties. Garrison overflow and defensive kills count. Normal population territory claims happen immediately. No city means no reward. Hired Companies do not carry this economic rule to another faction.

No horses blocks Horseman, Cataphract, Knight, Cuirassier and Cavalry through the common availability check. Tanks and Autonomous Armour are machines and remain available, as the design document specifies.

The New Fire reuses the Swarm rule and action, offering 30 population for 2 VP once; the AI timing and post-rite rival valuation removed as dormant on October 24 are restored. Population map popups identify Flower Wars. The New Fire uses a torch glyph, with no blood imagery.

## Validation

The 64-game smoke completed on AWS spot (`aztecs-functional-1031-115112`, seeds 8310000–8310063): **0 panics, 0 stalls at 1200 turns, 32 invalid orders**. Flower Wars granted **273 population**; the AI completed **The New Fire 20 times**. All games reached a normal finish (turns 168–337). This establishes that both faction mechanics fire in actual AI games, not balance.

The 32 raw validation failures were 13 blocked spawn tiles and 19 invisible air-strike targets. They remain an AI follow-up; this run does not establish whether they predate the Aztecs. The report separately records 4,686 simultaneous-resolution failures (changed diplomacy, lost visibility, levy limits and similar). No invalid-order reason names the horse restriction or New Fire. See [the complete per-game results](aztecs-2026-10-31.json).
