## Read

This first deck does **not** meet its calibration target. Only four of twelve drawn Hold variants have both fewer than 15% of non-seeking entrants meeting them within 25 turns and at least 40% of seeking entrants meeting them. Medieval Walls is too passive (28.1% even with seeking off); late Bank and University cards are too hard to pursue. More consequentially, without objective seeking the deck still awards the Classical point to 93.4% of entrants over the game, versus 61.4% in the era control. The point is too often incidental, despite the 25-turn Hold window looking good in Classical.

The deck shortens the median game by 26 turns and shifts the winning VP budget toward cards (median five versus four) and away from wonders (two versus four). It does lower the winner's median rival-city captures from six to three, and 37.9% of winners capture at most two cities versus 16.0% in control. The capture gate (removed on the train; see the note at the top) produced zero capture-free winners. Wars declared fall from 31.64 to 26.57 per game; the turn-100 VP leader wins slightly more often (38.3% versus 34.4%), and lead changes stay at nine. The human-seat benchmark moves from 25.4% to 23.2% wins. These are paired-simulation observations, not evidence that human games feel more or less strategic.

The Spend prices are 0.93–1.25 times median entry Gold income multiplied by median time in the era, a deliberately rough gross-income proxy that ignores upkeep and purchases. Winner-source medians are calculated separately and need not add to a single winner's exact final score. The experimental capture gate followed the task's explicit rule; the designer then chose point design over a hard victory check, so the gate is gone and the arm needs a rerun to show the ungated zero-capture-winner rate.

Draw variance is limited in this skateboard: the seed chooses one of two Hold cards per era; that era's Spend and Fight cards are fixed so every reveal has exactly one Fight and at most one Spend.

**Recommendation:** retain `objectives=era` as default and keep this branch as the measured skateboard. The next deck iteration should cut incidental early points and replace the Hold variants that miss calibration before a winner is chosen.
