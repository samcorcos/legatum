## 5. Which option is it, and what would close the gap

**Answer: both.** Your option 1 is true: your start is a good one. It is the roomiest site on the map by design of the India rule, and Hard gets 25% from it on your map. Your option 2 is what made it "practically guaranteed". In two turns the AIs handed you two Settlers and a capital that no AI hands to another AI more than about 2% of the time, and that took Hard-in-your-seat from 8% to 56%. AI India in AI-only games wins 12% of six-seat games (even share 17%), so the faction is not over-tuned.

**Recommendations (AI, in order):**

1. **Price a surprise war on an exposed Settler by what the rival has done, not a flat prior.** `civilians.rs::peace_settler_capture_chance` with `peace_settler_capture` and `settler_safety`, both off in Hard since the 2026-10-24 gate, which found opponents softer by about 2 pp with them on. Raise the chance to near-certain for a rival that:
   - has military units or a Scout within reach of the route, and
   - has declared a war from Peace and taken a Settler or city in the public event log (India did both at T16–T17, before the Celts moved).

   Keep the low prior for everyone else, which addresses the gate's softness. Germany also needs the escort-or-stay rule to keep one unit home when the capital itself is in a rival's reach (`hard_defense.rs::garrison_can_escort`).
   - **Measured on your game:** with both genes on in every AI from T1 (or from T10), your recorded orders no longer take the German Settler or Leipzig in 48 of 48 runs. Germany is alive at T37 48/48, and you have 3 cities and 48 Production instead of 4 and 59. A neutral gene change leaves your game identical. The Celtic Settler is still taken, so the "has sniped" read is the missing piece.
   - **On the Hard-in-your-seat win rate:** from T1 it drops from 25% to 8% (12 → 4 of 48), and from T10 from 27% to 17%.
   - **Three gates:**
     - (a) A decisive-turn fixture `india_t13_settler`: Germany's Settler goes home or waits at T13–T14, and the Celtic Settler stays home at T17.
     - (b) The new Scenario Lab cut below, with Hard in the India seat as the baseline, plus a scenario where a non-sniping neighbour should not slow expansion.
     - (c) Re-gate on the human-seat benchmark, rivals-only. The 10-24 gate predates `settler_stalk` (11-02), which made the benchmark's Hard seat stalk Settlers, so the old "softer" result may not hold.
2. **Spend early windfall Gold on tempo.** AI Germany's 140 Gold went into a Pasture and a Mine at T2–T4 while it had one troop. Hard in your seat would have spent your windfall on a Settler and a Quarry. `production.rs::plan_buy` should value a second troop when the first Settler is due and a rival army is visible. Gates: the Germany T13 fixture above; benchmark rivals-only.
3. **No start-balance change.** The India land rule is the faction's identity. The floor worked. Food-poor, Production-rich sites like yours remain the human's favourite (as in your 8.9.23 note), but the start explains about 17 Production of your lead and none of the 48-point swing. A design question for you only if you want India's "widest land" to also count rival distance: your nearest rival was 8 hexes away, and that rival was the one you ate.

**Rubber bands:** not material by T37. Decadence is off since 10-31 and no crown upkeep applied. Diffusion was not measured for 37 turns.
