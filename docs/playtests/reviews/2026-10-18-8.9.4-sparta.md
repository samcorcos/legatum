## Coach's verdict

You're right that it was easy, and it wasn't only the AI. It was both, and the first victim was a rules problem.
- **Athens did what the design asks, and still died.**
  - It had Walls from turn 9 (it got Masonry from a ruin on turn 3).
  - It saw your first Hoplite two hexes away on turn 11, and your four on turn 16.
  - It fell on turn 20, three turns into the siege, to four or five Hoplites.

  Next to another Hoplite, yours fight at 27. A walled two-population city defends at 24, and an Archer's shot does only 16 to 20 to a Hoplite in a phalanx. In the what-ifs, Athens was forced to build Walls, three Archers and a Spearman from turn 5, against a scripted Hoplite storm cruder than you. It still fell by turn 40 in 27 of 30 runs. With the phalanx switched off, it fell in 14 of 30.
- **The Mongols and AI Sparta had time, and wasted it:**
  - The Mongols built a Settler for ten turns (T23–T32, 102 Production) while at war with you, as your army walked up to Karakorum.
  - AI Sparta finished its Walls (the 8.9.4 `walls_under_way` change worked), but its army spent turns 14–28 wandering between two campaign targets: six plan flips, never a declaration. Therapne fell in two turns with one defender at home and three of its four units 3–9 hexes away.
  - Nobody touched your capital, though AI Sparta had three units within five hexes of it for six turns while you held it with one Hoplite.
- **The 8.9.4 fixes were aimed at the wrong moment for this map.** They help defenders keep Walls and help attackers storm. Here every rival was one city, 7–10 hexes away, so each of your captures ended an empire. The game was decided by turn 29, with two of three rivals gone. The rubber bands never had time to engage: decadence was 0% until T30 and 6% at most.

**Pillar 10, in plain words:** against your rush on this map, the counter the design names (scout, then Walls and archers) doesn't hold for the nearest one-city neighbour. Walls fall in three turns to phalanx Hoplites that ignore wounds and heal on kills. Spearmen held better than Archers. The farther neighbours could have held with better AI play. So, ranked:
1. A rule question for you: Walls are too weak against phalanx Hoplites in the first 25 turns. The decided siege rework, where Walls halve uncovered damage, may fix it on its own.
2. AI work: defence that scales with the army at the door, and AIs that keep their army home or hit your capital.

## 3. The small map, four players: is the rush unstoppable here?

**The map:**
- Athens and Karakorum started 7 hexes from you, and Therapne 10. Seven is the generator's minimum on every map size (`mapgen.rs::START_MIN_DISTANCE`); 10 is its preferred spacing.
- On the 40x26 map, two of your three rivals sat at the minimum.
- Every AI still had one city at T20 (the Mongols founded their second on T21), so every capture could end an empire.

**The what-ifs.** I branched your game at the release engine, 30 runs a branch, about $0.20 of box time in all. Your seat was played three ways:
- **Your own recorded orders (the tape).** This is exact, but it can't adapt, and once a defender stands on a tile your orders used, the orders misfire. The dice are your game's, so the 30 runs are one sample.
- **Hard Sparta in your seat, forced into your wars, never accepting peace.**
- **A scripted storm** (diagnostic only, never landed): Hard runs the economy, the capital trains Spearmen nonstop, and every unit marches on the nearest city you're at war with and attacks once three stand within two hexes. It's cruder than you. It leaves the capital empty, so the AIs retake it often, and it loses 15–24 units by T100.

The defenders were forced, from a given turn, to build Walls (where the faction can), then Archers until 2 or 3 stand within two hexes, plus a Spearman ("strong"), or 3 Spearmen ("spears").

What it says:
1. **Athens, the nearest one-city neighbour, can't be saved by its own defence.** It falls whatever it builds; the phalanx is the difference between "always" and "half the time" against a full defence.
2. **The farther neighbours can hold, and Spearmen beat Archers against Hoplites.**
   - Karakorum's fall rate halves with three Spearmen (23/30 to 11/30). Two Archers did nothing (24/30).
   - Arithmetic: an Archer defends at 8 plus terrain and takes 32–39 a hit from a Hoplite even fortified in a forest; it shoots for 16–20. A Spearman defends at 20, plus 6 fortified.
   - The AI's defence ladder answers every threat with a shooter (`threat.rs::plan_threat_defense`, `RANGED_LADDER`).
3. **Timing matters more than the build.** Walls and archers from T5 broke your exact plan. From T15, when the AIs actually saw your army, it was too late for Athens and Therapne. That's your rule: "when you see Sparta next to you... build walls and archers as soon as possible". The AIs saw Sparta at T4 (AI Sparta) and T11 (Athens) and didn't react until T16–T20.
4. **Counterattacks work.** From your T15 position, the storm lost its empty capital in 30 of 30 runs. You kept one Hoplite home, and AI Sparta's three units nearby never attacked. When the AI defenders were forced to build defensively instead, they stopped counterattacking, and the storm swept 22/30. Turtling alone is the weaker answer to an all-in rush.
5. **Hard Sparta can't run your rush, even from your position.** From T25, with five Hoplites at Therapne's walls and one defender inside, it opened the campaign, called it `insufficient_army` two turns later, and walked to Sarai. It took 0 cities by T40 in 30/30 runs; you took Therapne in two turns. AI defence is still being tuned against attackers much weaker than you (pattern below).

## 4. The verdict: execution gap, rules gap, or both?

**Both.**
- **Rules (the nearest neighbour):**
  - A pop-2 walled city at T16–T20 can't hold against four or five phalanx Hoplites. The phalanx lifts a Hoplite (22) above the walled city (24 → you attack at 27).
  - Wound immunity keeps damaged Hoplites at full strength.
  - Kill heals refill them.
  - The split counterattack means a city hurts each of four attackers by only 7–9.
  - Walls add strength but don't slow the HP race: 200 HP against about 30 a hit is seven hits, two turns for four Hoplites.
  - Pillar 10 needs a counter for the opening. For Athens here there was none in the rules.
- **AI (the other two):**
  - Mongols: a Settler under siege.
  - AI Sparta: an army that flip-flopped away from home, and a Granary with its capital at 53 HP.
  - Everyone: Archers instead of Spearmen against a melee rush, no counterattack on a one-Hoplite capital, and no reaction to "Sparta next door" until the army arrived.

**Cheating-adjacent ideas, rejected:**
- Free Walls or units for Hard.
- Defenders reading your production queue.
- "Always target the human".

  - three Hoplites on a city at once;
  - one Hoplite at home;

- **A rules hole:** see section 4.
- **Map luck:**

## 5a. Rubber bands or AI decisions?

The rubber bands did nothing in this game, and couldn't have:
- **Decadence:** 0% until T30, 1% at T30–34, 6% at T38.
- **Crown upkeep:** none that mattered; your oldest crown, Strongest Army, passed 30 turns at T34.
- **Tech diffusion:** the AIs knew 2–5 techs when they died.

**Verdict: about 0% from the bands, 100% from the opening's rules and AI decisions.** Pillar 11 ("nobody should know the winner by turn 100") failed in the first 30 turns, before any band applies. That is fine by design only if the opening itself has a counter, which is section 4's point.

## 5b. What would most likely have beaten you

Ranked by how much they cut your sweep, each marked learnable or not:

1. **Defend from first contact, not from first army.** Every AI walling and arming from T5 stopped your exact orders cold (sweep 100% → 0%, an upper bound). From T15 it still cut you to two captures. Learnable: AI Sparta saw you on T4, Athens on T11, and a Spartan neighbour is public. This is the design's "faction-aware threat reading" rule, not yet strong enough in the AI.
2. **Spearmen, not Archers, against Hoplites.** Karakorum fell 11/30 instead of 23–24/30. Learnable: the forecast already knows a Hoplite's strength.
3. **Counterattack the thin capital.** The storm lost its capital 30/30 when it left it empty. AI Sparta had three units within five hexes of Amyclae (one Hoplite inside) on T21–27 and never struck. Learnable.
4. **Phalanx off against cities** (a rule change, not an AI fix). Athens with full defence fell 14/30 instead of 27/30, and Karakorum 1/30 instead of 11/30.
5. **Keep the army home** (AI Sparta). Three of its four units were away when you hit Therapne. Learnable: plan stability.

## 6. Recommendations, ranked by expected effect on how often you win on Hard

Measure every one with:
- these branches: the runner and configs are in `~/LegatumRuns/coach-tools/coach-894/`. Use the storm seat and the tape seat, and Athens/Karakorum fall rates;
- the human-seat benchmark (seat-only and opponents-only);
- the war-scenario suite.

Also add a new war scenario from this game, "Hold Athens" at T10 (below), with the defender needing to keep Athens to T35.

1. **Rule question for you: make Walls the counter in the first 25 turns.**
   - The decided siege rework ("Walls halve all uncovered damage to a city's HP") would halve your 31–42 hits on Athens. That roughly doubles the siege to five or six turns, enough for an Archer or two to finish wounded attackers.
   - My suggestion: land it, then rerun this game's storm branches before touching Sparta.
   - If Athens still falls, a story-fit drawback (pillar 8, adding a weakness rather than cutting a strength): **"the Spartans were poor at sieges"**, so the phalanx doesn't apply when attacking a city. That fits history: their siege of Plataea took two years, and of Ithome about ten, with Athenian help asked for. With it off against cities, a full defence held Athens half the time.
2. **AI: defence that scales with the army at the door** (`crates/ai/src/threat.rs::exposure_to`, `plan_threat_defense`; `production.rs::choose_item`).
   - Today, exposure is 0, 1 or 2 whatever the size of the army. Defence is one shooter, or Walls, and then `if c.walls { continue }`, so Athens went back to a Granary and a Settler. A shooter within two hexes counts as covered, so the Mongols kept their Settler.
   - Run the AI's own storm forecast (it already prices the phalanx, wound immunity and kill heals) from the defender's side. When the visible army can take the city within N turns: put all production into the best defender against that attacker (Spearmen against a melee rush), stop Settlers and Granaries, and buy at the door.
   - Expected: Karakorum 23 → about 11/30 (the spears branch), and Therapne holds.
3. **AI: read the rush faction at first contact** (`threat.rs::watched_exposure`, `production.rs` opening).
   - A rush faction's capital or units seen within about 10 hexes should mean Walls plus two Spearmen before the next Settler. It's public information, and it's the user's own rule.
   - Expected: the T5 branch rather than the T15 one.
4. **AI: keep the army home, or strike back** (`plan.rs` switch hysteresis; `military.rs` campaign staging).
   - AI Sparta switched campaign target six times in 14 turns, each at value 0 ("forced expedition"), so its army stood midway between targets.
   - Two fixes:
     - while a rival at war, or a top threat, stands within reach of home, the army stays home;
     - when the rusher's capital is visible and thinly held, value a strike on it.
5. **Rule question for you: start spacing on small maps.**
   - `mapgen.rs::START_MIN_DISTANCE` = 7 on every map size. On the 4-player Small map (40x26), two neighbours landed at exactly 7.
   - Option: make the preferred 10 the floor on Small, or scale the floor with map area per player.
   - Not a first-turns grace period: you decided early rushes are final. Not free starting Walls or units: Walls are built with Production.
6. **AI attacker (already known): Hard Sparta can't convert a winning position.** From your T25 position it took nothing by T40, 30 of 30 runs.
   - This matters for defence tuning: every defence change so far was measured against AI attackers that never pressed home.
   - Use the storm seat or your tape as the attacker in defence tests, and fix the siege forecast's `insufficient_army` at Therapne-like positions (5 Hoplites, a walled pop-5 city, 1 defender).
