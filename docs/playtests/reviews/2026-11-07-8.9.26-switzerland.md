**What Hard did right, to keep:** Sparta picked the weakest neighbour (Aztecs, one city, one Soldier), declared with three Hoplites already at the walls (T19), took the city in six turns with every Hoplite attacking the same target each turn and lost no unit, and on the very next turn re-aimed the same army at the nearest empty city of its new enemy (Zürich, T26) and took it in four turns. ## 5. Was the loss fair?

Yes, and it is the kind of loss pillar 10 wants possible: a diplomatic choice with a visible price. Two parts of it lean on the interface, not on the AI:
- The offer card names the wars but not the state of the ally (one city, 0 HP, three Hoplites adjacent). That is public information the card could show. A UI question, not a rule change.
- When the ally is eliminated, the wars it brought you stay. That matches Civ, and it is honest, but the card does not say it. Worth one line ("If X is eliminated, these wars continue").
