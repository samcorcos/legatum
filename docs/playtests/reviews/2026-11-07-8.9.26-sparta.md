# Sparta, 8.9.26: five veterans against armies of one

Playtest 8.9.26 (`playtest-8.9.26`, engine `514dbb8fe`, rules version 19). Six empires on a 48x32 map, every AI on Hard: Mongols, Byzantium, Germany, Celts, Aztecs. You won on turn 122 by holding 10 of 10 VP for 10 turns. Every AI finished on 0 VP. 35 minutes of play in one session.

## Coach's verdict

You were right: this game was too easy, and the AI is mostly to blame, with Sparta's kill-heal as an accelerant. You trained five Hoplites by turn 29 and never trained another military unit until turn 97. With those five you took 9 cities by T85 and 18 by T122, and lost one Hoplite (plus two Scouts) in the whole game. Every empire you attacked had one military unit when you arrived. The Mongols, the Aztecs and Byzantium each built a Settler, a wonder or a Granary with your army two tiles from their city. Not one of the 14 Peace offers they sent you came with an army behind it. The coalition was real on paper: the Celts ran a war plan against you from T44 to the end, 78 turns, and Germany declared at T110. Neither one attacked a city of yours. Your eastern towns sat empty and walled six tiles from Celtic Gergovia for 60 turns, and nobody came. Hard playing your seat from turn 2 wins this map 35% of the time (Hard Sparta's average is 10.6%), so the neighbours were soft. But the game was decided by about T50, and that came from your choices: you kept five veterans alive, you hit cities instead of units, and you put your cities into Markets instead of more soldiers.

### Why AI Sparta is third-worst while you dominate with it

AI Sparta wins 10.6% of the eight-empire census (fair share 12.5%; 9.5% in this probe), third from the bottom. The kit is not the weak part. The faction ablation (11-06/07) has kill-heal at −7.5 pp and veterans at −6.3 pp, two of the biggest strengths in the game. AI Sparta pays the heaviest drawback in the game (+50% non-military cost, +17.5 pp) and then doesn't collect the strengths that drawback is meant to buy:
1. **It doesn't storm cities.** Hard Sparta makes 17 city attacks a game to your 91 by T122, and 2.1 captures by T122 to your 18. It fights units, so the +50% Settler cost leaves it small (4–5 cities at T85).
2. **It doesn't keep veterans.** It loses 13.6 units a game in the census, so the Spartan ladder and kill-heal are spent on units that die. Level-3+ units alive at T85: 0.3.
3. **It builds a bigger army instead of a better one.** 7.6 units trained by T85 to your 5, with less Gold and less economy behind them.

This is the same shape as the Mongol review (11-06): his veteran Keshigs against Hard's Spearman-heavy armies that never use the faction's unit. Idea #47 (`mongol_horde`) is the Mongol fix; §6 rec 3 is the Sparta one.

## 5c. Balance read: the kit or the AI?

- **Both, AI first.** AI Sparta is 10.6% while you win 10 VP to 0 with it, so the kit doesn't make Sparta strong; a player who keeps veterans alive does. What made this game easy for you was rivals with one unit and a coalition that never acted. Hard in your seat from T2 wins 35%, three times its census rate, before any of your skill.
- **The kill-heal estimate:**
  - It gave you 933 HP from kills, plus full heals for 23 units on 18 captures (about 900 HP more, an upper bound).
  - At the ordinary resting rate (10 HP a turn in the field, 20 at home), that is roughly 90–180 unit-turns of rest saved.
  - That is 17–34% of your army's 532 unit-turns at war.
  - Rough tempo cost without it: the same conquest about a fifth to a third slower, or one or two more units. You would still have won; the war against one-unit rivals would have run longer.
  - For the AI, the ablation says kill-heal is worth 7.5 pp of Sparta's win share.
- **Design flag (for you, not an AI fix):** after the 11-06 cut of slow resting heal, kill-heal is a strength with no matching drawback in the healing rule. Sparta now heals at the normal rate *and* by kills. The +50% non-military cost and desertion at peace remain. Options to consider:
  - (a) keep it as is: AI Sparta needs every point;
  - (b) pay for it with a drawback that pushes the same style, for example kill-heal only for units at level 2+ (veterans);
  - (c) restore a reduced resting heal for Spartan units outside their own territory.

  The measure is pillar 8: AI-only Sparta share plus his human games.

## 6. Recommendations (ranked by expected effect on how often you win)

**AI fixes**

1. **Defence that answers the army at the gate (extends #48a and the "Defence doesn't scale" pattern).**
   - **Mechanism:** when a visible enemy army of 3+ military units stands within 3 tiles of a city, `production.rs::choose_item` must not pick a Settler or a wonder in that city.
   - `threat.rs::exposure_to` should count the army and the unit's era, not 0/1/2.
   - Walls first in that city when the city has none and the army has no siege units.
   - Not more units overall.
   - **Gates:**
     - fires on his decisive turns: Mongols T19, Aztecs T36, Byzantium T62 (`p881_trace` shows a Settler or wonder chosen there today);
     - the tape-seat windows above, his city captures by T58/T85;
     - the human-seat benchmark, seat-only and opponents-only.
2. **Coalition follow-through: a Contain war must produce a campaign on an undefended city (extends #38a, #40, #48b).**
   - The Celts' 78-turn Contain and Germany's T110 Contain priced success at about 3% and never opened a campaign, though Tlatelolco and two more walled, unmanned towns stood 6–10 tiles off.
   - **Mechanism:** in `plan.rs::contain_candidates`, a known enemy city with no military unit within 3 tiles, and the leader's field army more than 6 tiles from it, becomes a campaign candidate priced on that local picture.
   - Peace offers to the leader stop while the plan is Contain (the Celts offered 5 times).
   - **Gates:** Scenario Lab `celts-strike-the-leader` (Hard 0/50 today); the human-seat gate; `conquest_alarm`'s failure (a public alarm alone) is the thing to beat.
3. **Hard Sparta plays his playbook (new idea #51).** The gene `sparta_veterans_playbook` from §4d:
   - capped war army;
   - keep veterans alive with heal-aware movement;
   - targets by defenders at the gate, storming cities.
   
   Gate: AI-only Sparta share on 9311003 and 8241001, and Hard in his seat on this map from T2 (17/48).
4. **Byzantium uses its answer to Thin Levies.** It never hired a Company while capped at 5 units. Trace `company.rs::plan_company` (`want = uncapped_army − army` is 0–1 at war under `army_war_extra` 1, then the Gold reserve and runway gates) on this tape at T63–T80.

**Design flags**

- Kill-heal after the slow-heal cut (§5c).
- Sparta's +50% non-military cost is the heaviest drawback in the game (+17.5 pp) and AI Sparta doesn't collect the strengths it pays for. That is an AI problem first; don't weaken the kit (pillar 8).
