# Germany, 8.9.27: a capital on Science, two Bombers, five cities in eight turns

Playtest 8.9.27 (`playtest-8.9.27`, engine `730e6aef2`, rules version 21). Six empires on a 48x32 map, every AI on Hard: Switzerland, India, the Mongols, the Aztecs and Athens. You won on turn 153 with 14 VP; Switzerland and the Aztecs had 3. 30 active minutes in one session (0.1 minutes a turn in the first two eras, 0.2 in the middle three, 0.3 in the Industrial era). Game id `web-1794091560504-8ec026e92a999800-bfb5c3002d509000`, checked against the save and the archive. The replay at the tag matches all 153 turns (0 hash mismatches) and reproduces every AI order.

This is a focused review. The general pattern (thin armies win because the AI never attacks empty cities, nobody tracks an economic leader, and nobody answers Bombers) is in the Swiss, Sparta and Celts reviews of the same day and isn't repeated here.

VP race and military strength (archived outside the repository)

## Coach's verdict

This game is different from the other three. **Hard can't win from your seat.** Hard taking over your empire wins 0 of 48 games from T2, 1 to 5 of 48 from T20 to T80, and 0 of 48 from T100. The Aztecs win most of those games. In the Swiss, Sparta and Celts games, Hard in your seat won 56% to 88%. Here the rivals were good enough to beat Hard as Germany, and you beat them anyway.

The win came from one plan in the last fifty turns:

- From T100 your capital ran the Science project for 54 turns.
- Your Gold grew from 366 to 1,884 by T134, with 2 or 3 military units in the field.
- You researched Flight on T134 and bought two Bombers on T134 and T135.
- In 20 turns you killed 25 AI units, took five cities and lost no units.

The Science project is what made it work. Switch it off from T120 and Flight arrives on T136 instead of T134, so the Bomber purchase on T134 can't be made.

On Germany's kit:

- **The Industry project paid off.** It made 11% of your Science after T99.
- **The Ruhr Foundry carried Essen.** It paid for the Pyramids and the Science project.
- **Double supply mattered twice.** It covered one Ruhr Foundry and the third unit that needs Oil.
- **The −2 Food did almost nothing.** It cost you 2 population over 120 turns.

## 2. Germany's kit in your hands

**Method.** Your own orders were replayed at the tag, with one German rule switched off from T2, T60, T100 or T120 (`faction_rules_off`; runner `germany_kit.rs`). The dice and seeds are recorded, so the only change is the rule. On top of that, the control replay accounts for every turn.

**Verdict per rule:**

- **Industry projects: a real strength, used once and used well.** The Science project in a capital that had finished its buildings was worth two turns on Flight. Those two turns were the Bombers' timing. You never ran the Gold project.
- **Ruhr Foundry: the engine.** Essen with a Foundry produced 31–34 Production at 5–7 population. That built the Pyramids in 7 turns and then fed the Science project.
- **Double supply: minor.** It was binding only at the very end, and on Oil.
- **−2 Food: not a drawback in this game.** Your Foundry cities had food to spare, and it cost 2 population in 120 turns. This is a design question for you (pillar 8: a drawback should push the faction into its style), not an AI fix.
- **The other Germany rules:** the 90% building-cost discount moves every timing. Switched off from T2, your orders break by T11. The Five Foundries goal added 2 VP on T152, which didn't change the result.

## Notes for the lead

- **AI, Industry projects as tempo.**
  - Hard values projects only as a fallback (`production.rs:2488` and the fallback at the end of that function).
  - The fix: price the Science project in a city with nothing worth building as turns gained on the plan's target tech (Flight, the era unit). Price the Gold project against an open `decisive_buy`.
  - Measure on the designer seat (this tape from T80 and T100) and with Germany's AI-only win share.
- **AI, the air stage, again.** No AI had a Fighter, and the Aztecs had the same Science as he did on T134 (78 a turn). This is a fifth game of the "air stage is uncontested" pattern; the fix is already on the list.
- **Design question for him:** the −2 Food on a Ruhr Foundry cost 2 population in 120 turns. If it's meant to push Germany to trade Food for Production, it needs to bind, for example −1 Food for each population above 4.
