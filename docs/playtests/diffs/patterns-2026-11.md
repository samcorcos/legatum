# Decision patterns across the designer's games (November 2026)

## Scope and measurement

The input set is completed human-seat games in `~/LegatumData/archive/index.jsonl` (excluding records whose outcome is `abandoned`), deduplicated against finished win saves in `~/LegatumRuns/bug-saves/`, plus the separate Germany 8.9.23 and Sparta 8.9.13 saves. A game enters the pattern totals only when its recording commit equals a `playtest-*` tag and the tape replays with **zero state hash mismatches** on that tag. All **24** eligible games passed; the reports value **640** selected turn/category disagreements (the Celts 8.9.25 game added 2026-11-03 by the coach). The unfinished India 8.9.25 game (stopped at T37, replayed hash-exact on its tag, continued with Hard to T100) adds 14 more, for **654**; the finished India 8.9.25 win (2026-11-05) adds 30, for **684** in the ledger. The ranked table below predates that game; the current sums are in [patterns-ledger.md](patterns-ledger.md). The game reports below link to their ranked turns and paired outcomes. No save or tape is committed.

Each report compares one category of his orders on one turn with Hard's orders on that same turn; both arms then continue with Hard. The top 30 turn/category candidates per game were chosen by the documented outcome-independent heuristic, and each arm has 48 paired reseeded runs. A delta is **his arm minus Hard's arm** at the recorded end turn or an earlier finish. The value columns use VP. A pattern's sum adds its selected turn effects; its mean divides that sum by the number of selected turns. For the approximate 95% intervals, paired deltas are summed within each game and salt before adding independent game variances. These sums rank diagnostic opportunities; they are not the expected gain from implementing every change together. The intervals cover salt sampling, not the heuristic selection, reconstructed opponent memory, different engine rules, or a later AI change.

Legacy tags before the audit example existed have exact replay and Hard orders but no scorer traces. A code path named for those turns is an attribution hypothesis unless an order gate or scorer trace in that exact version supports it. A positive one-turn delta identifies a useful test case, not a proven general AI improvement.

## Recommended AI fixes and proof gates

These are **proposals and closure gates**, ordered by specific positive evidence and how directly a test can reproduce the decision. This audit branch changes no AI behavior. For each proposed benchmark gate, use `tools/sim/hard-vs-human.sh` in the cloud with the established 8 maps × 256 salts, compare seat-only and rivals-only arms, and require the intended win-rate direction to exclude zero at 95%. Then check the combined candidate separately, because previous individually useful AI changes have interfered with one another. The [current-master closure ledger](patterns-ledger.md) now marks the Swiss T140 Bomber fixture closed after `decisive_buy 1` landed; six other listed decisive choices remain open.

1. **Choose viable founding now when delay loses the site.** Inspect `crates/ai/src/civilians.rs::choose_site_scored` and `plan_settler`; price the value of a legal, defensible site that a rival may take before the next route completes. The gate should change Hard's choice at [Tollkeepers 8.3 T36/T44](tollkeepers-2026-10-06-8.3-decision-diff.md) and [Signalwrights 8.9.5 T58/T118/T137](signalwrights-2026-10-18-8.9.5-decision-diff.md), while retaining the border-risk rejection that helped at [Swiss T145](swiss-8.9.24-decision-diff.md). Verify a replayed-turn scenario, then a paired human-seat benchmark with seat-only and rivals-only arms.
2. **Spend a reserved hoard on a decisive wartime purchase (decision gate closed).** Hard kept Gold at [Swiss T140](swiss-8.9.24-decision-diff.md) despite a +2.38 [+1.93, +2.82] VP Bomber purchase, because its routed plan was Peace and the 5,000 Gold reserve blocked the 805 Gold buy. Current `master` now chooses the Bomber at that recorded turn; the [closure ledger](patterns-ledger.md) records the exact engine commit. This single-turn pass does not establish that all reserve errors are closed or that the overall win rate improved.
3. **Keep a winning siege open when answering Peace.** Inspect `crates/ai/src/diplomacy.rs::treaty_score`, `wants_peace_why`, and gene `peace_own_danger`. Refusing at [Swiss T147](swiss-8.9.24-decision-diff.md) raised paired wins by 25.0 [+12.6, +37.4] percentage points, although VP fell by 0.6; [Crucible T78](crucible-2026-10-09-8.6-decision-diff.md) also favors refusal in VP. The gate is refusal at Swiss T147 while a separately constructed dangerous war still accepts relief, followed by the paired benchmark. The aggregate refusal VP interval includes zero, so this must be conditional.
4. **Protect Settlers against a surprise war from Peace.** Inspect `crates/ai/src/hard_defense.rs::garrison_can_escort` and `guard_exposed_settlers_duel`, with gene `settler_escort`. The existing escort policy did not cover the exposed rival Settlers he took at [Germany T16](germany-2026-11-01-8.9.23-decision-diff.md) and [Swiss T41](swiss-8.9.24-decision-diff.md). The gate is an escort or safe route for those rival Settlers from their own visible view before his declaration, plus a scenario that leaves a threatened capital defended. Measure rival Settler losses and designer-seat wins in the paired benchmark; the one-turn diff supplies no numerical effect for this multi-turn weakness.
5. **Make the Settler production gate conditional on a defendable colony.** Inspect `crates/ai/src/production.rs::item_value` and genes `settler_open_cities` and `colony_compound`. At [Swiss T45](swiss-8.9.24-decision-diff.md), the undeveloped-city gate zeroed the Settler before colony value, and his order gained +1.19 [+0.51, +1.86] VP. The gate is a positive Settler score and order at T45, a scenario where a colony lacks escort or a legal site and remains rejected, and a paired benchmark. The cross-game Settler-production row is negative, so simply loosening the quota is not a supported fix.
