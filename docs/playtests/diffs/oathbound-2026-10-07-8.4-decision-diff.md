## Method and limits

Engine: `playtest-8.4` (900d58c4e725). Replay: 61 turns, 0 hash mismatches; 0 AI strategic order mismatches in 140 seat turns.
Candidate heuristic: category weights, resolved effects, early turn and active war priority, with category and phase caps plus two per turn; 14 valued candidates. The table ranks by absolute win change (percentage points) plus ten times absolute VP change; this is a display order, not an AI value function. Branches: 1344 runs, 48 salts, dice reseeded; each single category is swapped for one turn, then every seat plays Hard through T62 or an earlier finish.
The swap measures an order bundle in a category, not the independent value of every order inside it. When a branch finishes early, its final VP is used. Confidence intervals describe dice sampling only; they do not cover model bias, category interactions, or the fact that the recorded opponents also respond as Hard after the fork. A 0 [0, 0] interval means no difference appeared in these 48 runs, not that the choices are equivalent.
