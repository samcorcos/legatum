What we've learned

  • 2026-11-15 Sparta 8.9.57: eight late Swiss Gold military buys were six Spearmen, one Pikeman and one Archer; several circled or sat near Zug instead of joining the war plan. A generation floor cut late buys at least two upgrade steps old from 801/1343 to 124/787 in 50 paired games, still above the near-zero target; all 124 were forecast-proven empty-city rescue exceptions, with zero non-rescue floor leaks. Re-enabling the named Gold fund made rivals weaker from the designer's T40 seat (41/48 to 47/48 seat wins, paired p .03125) and lost the Swiss hollow-Mongols card (31/64 to 0/64).
  • 2026-11-07 Training mode landed as a lobby level: battle search while he plans changes Hard's orders in 80% of real battles, but 90% of those changes lean on units the seat can't see, and it doesn't beat Hard yet (war suite -2 points). It's a source of lessons, not a harder level.
  • 2026-10-31 Audit round 6: resistance in safe captures triggered 57 unnecessary production alarms. Removing them did not establish stronger Hard play: pooled seat-only -0.49 points, rivals-only +1.07, both uncertain. The gene stays off. After a combat rebalance, audits must use verified original positions: replaying old orders on new rules drifts away from the designer's game.
  • 2026-10-31 Audit round 4: Hard sent its Settlers far away because a neighbour's known city's own border looked like a hidden city, and far sites were never checked for danger while near ones were. Fixing both (off for now) lets Hard found where you would: in three separate 512-game tests Hard in your seat won about 6 points more, and its rivals were about 2 points tougher for you. Ready for the full 2,048-game test.
  • 2026-10-28 "Defend where the enemy army is" is neutral in full games (your-seat AI -1.1 pts, rivals -0.3, all inside noise) but wins wars: war tests +6.5 pts, adaptive Sparta's wins halved, Japan T142 holds. Shipping in 8.9.15. A full 2,048-game test now costs about $0.10.
  • 2026-10-28 Fighters help once rivals bomb: Hard in the designer's seat +3.2 pts; as rivals, -1.3 for the designer (not yet significant). They change nothing in the war tests. The campaign planner is neutral in full games (+1.2 / +1.0); its gains stay in the war tests.
  • 2026-10-28 Defending where the enemy army is (buying or upgrading the era's best unit at the threatened city, striking wounded attackers) lifted the war tests from 48% to 53% and cut an adaptive Hard Sparta from 19 to 12 wins of 32, at no speed cost. Risk found: with every AI using it, AI attackers fail against AI defenders.
  • 2026-10-28 Against the app's real Hard rivals (which bomb), Hard in the designer's seat wins only 16%, not 40%: the old test used the experimental Search level as rivals, and it never dropped bombs. Re-measured that way, Fighters help (+3.2 pts, was -4.7) and 8.9.14's modernization gain holds (+3.7).
  • 2026-10-27 The 8.9.13 Germany-map drop comes from the unit-valuation switch on that one map, not engine drift (turning it off recovers +16 pts there but costs Switzerland 8.3).
  • 2026-10-27 Of every AI change we had, only army modernization (unit upgrades and sending units home to upgrade) helped in both roles: +2.4 pts as the designer's seat, -6.5 for him as rivals, war suite 41% to 47%.
  • 2026-10-27 The 2,048-game test's rival AIs (Hard+Search) never dropped bombs, unlike the app's Hard. Air genes must be judged again after that test fix; Fighters looked bad (-4.7) only because the rivals never bombed.
  • 2026-10-27 Army quality matters more than tactics: replaying the designer's own Athens defence, the Swiss army (Spearmen, Archers) could never take a walled city, whatever the plan.
  • 2026-10-27 Unit upgrades are the one switch that measurably helped in both 10-27 games (Athens from T150: 31 to 26 of 32 wins; Hard Sparta from T1: 7 to 2 of 32).
  • 2026-10-27 Wars on the leader are valued but never fought: Sparta was at war with Athens for 55 turns and never attacked a city; no AI attacked or retook one of the designer's empty cities in either game.
  • 2026-10-27 No AI ever built a Fighter. Bombers stay as strong as they are (designer's decision); the AI must contest the air.
  • 2026-10-27 Bigger armies don't help: a higher unit quota made the AIs worse against an adaptive Sparta (they wrecked their economies).
  • 2026-10-27 The Sparta game was decided by T40-60 (pillar 11 says nobody should know the winner by T100).
  • 2026-10-27 A passing overall test can hide a big single-map drop: 8.9.13 passed (+1.0 pts) but the Germany map fell from 360 to 281 of 512.
