Legatum is a historical 4X on a hex map: explore, expand, build, compete. Eight empires, simultaneous turns (everyone submits orders for the same state, so a rival's army can move while you decide). Winning is zero-sum: victory points (VP) come from eras' objectives, titles, cities and a faction goal, and a rival's loss is your gain.
- Every unit gets one order per turn. A city builds one item at a time; Gold buys the item (at about 2 Gold per Production, Swiss 1.75).
- Walls make a city very costly to storm and act as its garrison (a walled city needs no troop of its own). Walled cities can still be taken by a concentrated force that accepts losses; unwalled cities with a weak garrison are the easy prize.
- A city that falls to 0 HP is taken by walking a unit in. Capturing cities is the fastest way to grow; conquest is the core loop, "tall" is not viable.
- Peace and Alliance are treaties. An Alliance makes you a defender in your ally's wars and can pull you into wars you did not choose. Declaring war is an order; a Peace can be refused or accepted.
- Land units from the Classical era on need their era's strategic resource. Settlers found cities (about 3 to 4 tiles apart); a Settler is vulnerable until it founds.
- Barbarians raid. Scouts reveal the map; information is worth a unit.
Faction kits: Sparta (all military start veterans, XP +50 percent, Hoplite phalanx +5 strength next to another Hoplite, kills heal; Settlers and non-war builds cost +50 percent; ten turns without a battle starts desertion). Switzerland (Gold from population and interest; buys at 175 percent; Alpine Redoubt replaces Walls; pays upkeep beyond two units; The Reserve goal for 5,000 Gold). India (+1 Food per city, bonuses for nearby Indian cities, cities 3 tiles apart; far cities lose output to corruption up to 100 percent; non-capital cities remit no Gold until a Revenue Office).
