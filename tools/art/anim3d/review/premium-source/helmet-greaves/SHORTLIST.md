## Greaves

No good standalone CC greaves exist on Sketchfab. Options, in order:

1. **Hoplite by tolnaydavid** (CC BY, already downloaded): its `Leg_armor_low` mesh is a pair of bronze greaves, sculpted. No download needed.
2. [Aegis Sentinel - Ancient Blue Hoplite Warrior V1](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/a2619fc3c48a420e9b6fe6091031a401), pixforge.id, CC BY, 92.6k tris for the full figure: extract the greaves and decimate them.
3. [Low poly Spartan Armor for Roblox](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/c2c66de092254c57814862c0c2e971a5) and [female Greek_armor](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cd9e9b606a204f87aa72e2a0a58c9105) were checked and rejected: blocky, or no greaves.
