# Legatum faction design

Factions carry most of Legatum's flavour. Load `legatum-rules` (what is possible) and `legatum-design` (general philosophy) alongside this skill. Decisions of record live in the design doc's decisions log; this skill holds the method and the current state.

## The method
1. **Start from the story.** Who are they, and what would they obviously be good and bad at? Every rule must read as obvious from the story. Rejected for failing this: hills for Celts; "+50% unit cost until Steam" for Germany; "+30 per unit" ("just inflation").
2. **One broken rule** (TI4-style): a capability nobody else has, changing *what* you can do, not just how fast. Prefer bending or relaxing an existing system over adding a new one. Rejected for adding a whole mechanic for one faction: Mongols contracts; traffic tolls (no trade routes exist).
4. **A path to 10 VP** that uses the broken rule, and a goal that is a real choice (a goal completed in 98% of games, like Byzantium's old exploration goal, is a freebie).
5. **A visible counter** other players can read and act on (walls vs rushers; wedging into India's city cluster; Celts land vs invaders).
6. **Scaling:** bonuses as percentages or through existing progression (promotions), not flat numbers that fade (+4 strength) or premiums that balloon (+25% cost).
7. **Usefulness test:** would a player actually use this in this game? Moving cities and hit-and-run (impossible with one order per turn) failed it.
8. **AI must play it,** both as the faction and against it. Every mechanic needs AI support, and rivals must adapt to it (avoid Celts land, target the rich Swiss, wedge into Japan's city cluster).
9. **UI must explain it** (pillar 5): costs, penalties, bounties and countdowns are visible; unusual mechanics need coaching (backlog G26).
10. **Keep it short:** each rule fits in one sentence. Four or five lines per faction is the ceiling.
11. **Every rule must move its faction's win share** (designer, 2026-11-05): switching a strength off must lower the faction's share and switching a drawback off must raise it, or the rule is dead weight and is redesigned. `tools/sim/ablate.sh <faction>` measures it (`legatum-sim --rule-off`, report in `docs/sim-results/faction-ablation/`). First read, Germany (4,096 paired Hard games): all four strengths (Ruhr Foundry, the 10% discount, Industry projects, the Five Ruhr Foundries goal) are dead, within 0.4 pp of nothing; the movement drawback is the one German rule that moves the share (+3.4 pp when removed). (superseded 2026-11-06: the movement drawback was removed in the Germany rework; the Ruhr Foundry moved to the Forge slot and the drawback is now −2 Food, see the Germany entries below.)

## Standing rules

- **Special units that exist (rules 34, user 2026-11-14, `legatum-rules` `references/factions.md`):** Aztecs Eagle Warrior (replaces Spearman: +4 strength, +10 Production); Celts Gaesatae (replaces Phalangite: +8 attacking, -5 defending); Athens Peltast (replaces Composite Archer: 3 moves, same shot); India War Elephant (replaces Knight: +8 strength, 3 moves instead of 4, no city-charge penalty); Switzerland Reisläufer (replaces Pikeman: +50% against mounted); Germany Panzer (replaces Tank: +1 move, uses 1 Oil). Sparta, the Mongols (Keshig) and Byzantium (Tagmatic Soldier) are in the table. Treat a faction without a special unit and a special building as incomplete.
- **Every faction has a cost, a special unit and a special building, all painted** (user, 2026-11-15, symmetry ruling; Civ 6 package, 2026-11-14). A faction or piece is not done with placeholder art.
- **A goal's cost scales with the empire** (user, 2026-11-22, rejecting a one-city tomb project: "one city pausing production for 20 turns is too easy"): every city pays, or the empire's scarcest thing does. A held goal's city is public and capturable ("a target like the heir"). History: `references/new-factions-2026-11.md`.
- **One unusual cost per rule break;** a second price stacked on top reads as punishment (user, 2026-11-16, Rome).
- **Every faction goal is worth exactly 2 VP;** tune the threshold or cost, never the VP (user, 2026-10-06). A goal is a cost or a strategic trade-off in theme, never "do your thing more", never a chore of busywork, never map luck, and goals differ in kind across factions (user, 2026-10-06). Every goal has a super-high cost or rivals can take the points away.
- **Every rule must move its faction's win share** when ablated (`tools/sim/ablate.sh <faction>`); dead rules are redesigned or cut (designer, 2026-11-05). Read AI-only ablations with care: the AI can overspend on a drawback (2026-11-07).
- **One idea in about 30 words** per ability or drawback (user, 2026-10-18); four or five lines per faction at most.
- **A faction exception the player cannot see reads as a bug:** exceptions must be visible at the moment of decision (the forecast names them) or not exist (user, 2026-10-31).
- **When a faction loses a yield, the UI drops it too** (user, 2026-11-22, Egypt hides Production; no Quarry offered).
- **Unique units carry names players recognise** and a renamed unit gets new art (user, 2026-11-07). No unique piece may be a bare rename (user, 2026-10-17).
- **A Mercenary Company is the seller's own special unit; the hire shop sells only special units** (user, 2026-11-14). Contracts last 30 turns (user, 2026-10-17).
- **The AI must play the kit,** not a generic game with a faction label (user, 2026-11-05); AI genes may be on for some factions and off for others (user, 2026-10-11).
- **Measure identity per faction, never in aggregate** (user, 2026-10-03): Sparta and Mongols by captures and first-war timing; Switzerland by interest; Byzantium by Companies; India by cluster and Revenue Office coverage; Germany by project output. Judge AI combat by efficiency (captures per war, units lost per capture).
- **Balance target:** best/worst faction win ratio at most 1.5x at Standard, about 2,000 games plus held-out seeds, scripted Hard. This is the aim, not the state: the 2026-11-22 census (rules 50, 11 factions, 2,112 games) read 9.1 (Egypt 21.4%, Rome 2.3%; 2.6 among the nine finished) in `docs/sim-results/2026-11-22-census-eleven-factions-rules50.md`; census win share is per game, convert to per seat (see `docs/learnings.md`).
- **Start sites:** every founding territory is the same 8 Food and 8 Production (rules 24, 2026-11-07); only India has a start preference.
- **Keeping it current:** after any faction discussion or change, update the table and the one-line rulings here, and put detail in the matching reference file, in the same turn.

## Validation checklist for any faction change

Rules reference updated; WASM behaviour tests; AI plays it (telemetry shows the mechanic used); rivals' AI reacts; UI explains it; eight-player Hard with per-faction intervals and the ratio; ablation shows each rule moves the share. Full checklist: `references/lessons-and-validation.md`.

## Decided and rejected (one line each)

- Factions are real peoples, grounded in history; no fantasy or sci-fi (user, 2026-10-10).
- Japan is not playable until Tokugawa lands; its slot is India (serde alias `Japan`) (user, 2026-10-25).
- Switzerland is not a pacifist turtle; triple military upkeep stays (user, 2026-10-31, 2026-11-06). Large hoards are fine (user, 2026-10-17).
- Sparta: desertion removed and no upkeep penalty; expensive Settlers and non-military items are the price; kill-heal stays; retire level-3 veterans (user, 2026-11-06/07).
- Byzantium kept (user, 2026-11-07). On master: Mercenary Companies, thin levies and the Heir; bought peace, Hire an army and the Scriptorium are cut (user, 2026-10-18 to 2026-11-06). Decided but not on master as of 2026-11-22 (no Theodosian code in `crates/core`): the Theodosian Walls kit, the Varangian Guard and "take the capital and the empire collapses" (user, 2026-11-06/07; see the table).
- Celts: barbarians never attack them and are no threat to them either way; the sack pays 60 Gold per population, no tech steal; the Ambusher is cut (user, 2026-11-02/03/06).
- Mongols: +1 move, the Keshig (Classical), the Yam network; slip-past and Peace passage cut; the rush counter is AI skill, do not weaken the rush (user, 2026-10-06 to 2026-11-06). No Walls ever, captured Walls torn down (user, 2026-11-22; they won 24.7% of AI-only games on rules 51, so the loss is accepted).
- Germany: each Production building costs 1 Food; the movement drawback and first-building discount are cut (user, 2026-11-05/07, 2026-11-15).
- Athens stays the science faction; its drawback is Greece Captive (a captor gains a free technology); ruins give +20 Science on top (user, 2026-10-18, 2026-11-06).
- India: regional courts (Akbar), corruption by distance (user, 2026-10-25).
- Egypt: the Nile (double Food, no Production, surplus Food builds) replaced Food-funded construction; the softer "+50% Food, -25% Production" was rejected (user, 2026-11-21).
- Aztecs: Flower Wars, no horses, New Fire shown as torches, never blood (user, 2026-10-10, 2026-10-31).
- New factions beyond Egypt and Rome (United States, Bohemia, Inca, Persia, Portugal, Russia, Spain, Venice, France): not chosen (2026-11-15).
- The rule freeze of 2026-11-07 was lifted on 2026-11-11 (see `legatum-workflow`).
