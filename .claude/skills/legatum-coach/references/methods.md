# Coach: methods (replays, rubber-band accounting, what-ifs, seat models, tracing)

How to replay a save at its tag, read the archive, account for rubber bands, branch what-ifs, choose a seat model, trace genes, find the right build and game, and run forced-war arms.

Moved verbatim from SKILL.md on 2026-11-22 (original order kept). The current rules are in SKILL.md; where this file disagrees with SKILL.md, SKILL.md wins.

## Methods
- **Replay at the release tag on the box.** Extract `archive.tape` from the save's `game` string to a file in a throwaway worktree at the tag, then `tools/cloud/remote.sh <wt> --with-uncommitted --artifact coach-out -- cargo build --release -p legatum-sim --example game_dump --example playtest_ai_trace && target/release/examples/game_dump tape.json coach-out/dump && target/release/examples/playtest_ai_trace tape.json coach-out/trace 0`. `game_dump` gives every event, per-turn state (with `decadence_percent`, `power_history`, titles, `title_since`) and VP by source; `playtest_ai_trace` gives per-turn city yields, `gold_per_turn` and upkeep. Check "hash mismatches 0". About 20 minutes on the box.
- **The save alone already says a lot:** `archive.telemetry` (per turn: VP, cities, Gold, army, population, Science, wars), the tape's orders for every seat (purchases, declarations, treaties), and `ai_opponent_memory` (plan switches with their value terms, campaign targets and why they ended, threat log, alliance attempts).
- **Rubber-band accounting.** Per turn and empire: city yields are net of decadence, so gross = net × 100 / (100 − rate); Swiss interest = floor(treasury / 100); diffusion = 10% of a tech's base cost per other empire that knew it (cap 50%), and the user's marginal share of each AI's discount. Translate into tempo: e.g. the winning Great Work's build time with gross instead of net Production. Account for the strongest AI too.
- **What-if branching.** `extract_state` at the tag for the branch turns; a throwaway continuation runner loads the state, plays the user's seat with Hard and with the human-proxy playbook, applies per-branch overrides (AiParams per seat, rule experiments, injected declarations, refused peace) and varies only AI RNG by salt; 30+ salts per branch, seat win rate and VP gap against a same-turn control. Rubber bands off = `decadence-slope=0,crown-upkeep-after=200` in `GameState.rule_experiments`. Budget about $10 a review.
- **Seat models (8.9.2 Swiss):** proxy v4 reproduced his result best (wins 85–88% from T100, T133, T150 like he did); Hard in his seat from T1 wins 0/64 on his map, and Hard taking over mid-game can drop the decisive build in progress (at T193 it abandoned the Grand Bazaar), so late branch points need the `tape` seat (his real orders). Controls swing with the seat model: always compare against the same turn and seat's control, and report two seat models. The tool and configs live outside the repo in `~/LegatumRuns/coach-tools/` (README there); about 3 minutes and $0.03 per 20 branches × 96 runs.
- **What-if variance (8.9.2 Sparta):** salting only the AI seeds leaves the first ~90 turns nearly identical (Hard is close to deterministic, the dice come from the game seed), so 30 runs are one sample. Reseed the game's dice per salt as `war_suite` does (`reseed: true` in the extended runner, `~/LegatumRuns/coach-892-sparta/`); one control went from 21/30 seat wins to 1/30. Knob and production-percent genes don't change the early army (`production.rs::desired_army` sets it): force a strategy with forced wars, no-peace and a proxy seat instead.
- **Seat models for an early rush (8.9.4 Sparta):** the `tape` seat reproduces his game exactly (salt 0, 0 hash mismatches) but misfires once a defender stands on a tile his orders used, so a defence branch against it is an upper bound; reseeding the dice breaks it outright. `proxy_v8_sparta` sat at home and lost its capital; Hard in his seat took 0 cities by T40 even from his T25 position. Use the scripted `storm` seat (Hard economy, Spearmen nonstop, all units on the nearest at-war city, attack at 3 within 2 hexes) with reseeded dice, plus a `defend` floor for AI seats; and a throwaway core flag for a rule counterfactual (`COACH_NO_PHALANX`). Runner, configs and README: `~/LegatumRuns/coach-tools/coach-894/` (about 1 minute and a few cents per 240 games). Measure city fall rates by a turn, not only sweeps.
- **Seat models for a late builder win (8.9.4 Swiss):** from T120 on, Hard and proxy v4 in his seat win 0–2 of 30 (Byzantium wins), so they sit at the floor and can't show a drop; his own orders (`tape`, no reseed, AI salts only) win 30 of 30 from T166 and T186 even after the hash drifts, so from mid-game the tape seat is the one that measures him. - **Two blind reviews of one game (8.9.30 Swiss, normal coach vs Fable, 2026-11-11):** both named the same deciding window and the same top three findings, so the method is sound; each found causes the other missed. Rules that came out of the comparison:
  - **Run the takeover curve first.** The one-turn diff's horizon ranks late Settler moves above a multi-turn commitment (finishing the Mongols) made 60 turns earlier; the curve plus per-turn plan notes finds it.
  - **Outcome-check every denial forecast:** compare each "break the hold" or denial term with the VP that actually moved next turn. A trace alone reads as "the fix fired".
  - **For a Swiss game, tabulate the Reserve break per city per turn** (bounty against the margin over 5,000) instead of telling the anecdote.
  - **Run rival what-ifs for the loser** (e.g. the Mongols accepting the Aztec Peace at T47: +35 points). Only the Fable pass did, and it was the largest single number in either review.
  - **Check a playbook pattern's sign against Hard-in-his-seat before restating it,** and split spending patterns by faction (for Switzerland, saving wins).
  - **Fog-check every "should have":** use only what the rival seat could see.
  - **Name the code cause, or say it is untraced.** Each pass traced one cause the other left as a pointer (`Ctx::build` hostile-only-at-War; `hoard_denial_value` under a partial view). A cause read in code but not reproduced on a fixture is a hypothesis.
