# Art: October decisions (resources, landmarks, improvements, audit, icons, emblems, map pass, portraits, roads)

Dated art decisions from 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-18, with as-built details, and the review-plan decisions.

Moved verbatim from SKILL.md on 2026-11-22 (original order kept). The current rules are in SKILL.md; where this file disagrees with SKILL.md, SKILL.md wins.

## Decisions

- **Resource art (user, 2026-10-10):** painted medallions are the chosen resource art. The six full-colour Wheat, Cattle, Stone, Copper, Iron, and Coal images replace monochrome resource glyphs everywhere. The map draws them at the existing marker size with a device-resolution high-quality downscale; a fine red hairline retains the strategic cue over the painted brass frame. City/banner clearance, hover and selection outlines, improvement marks, and improvement-under-resource-under-unit/banner layering remain intact. Far zoom keeps the simple, high-contrast bonus/strategic category marks, as specified by the zoom-tier rule. UI resource icons and the Guide use the same shipped PNGs (`app/src/assets/resources/README.md`).

- **Landmarks (user, 2026-10-24):** a finished Great Work is a landmark on its own tile beside its city (the painted unlock miniature at about 1.75 hex wide; a gilt-ringed icon badge at the far tier). Its construction plays through its own painted stages (foundations, half built with scaffold hugging the real structure, nearly complete), made from the finished art with an image edit so the framing never jumps (`assets/construction/README.md`). See legatum-ui.
- **Improvements on the map (user, 2026-10-04):** variant C at normal zoom: the land itself transforms (fields ploughed into the hex, a mine cut into the hill, terraces, a quarry face), drawn in the atlas ink style. Variant B when zoomed out: a small corner badge per improved tile. The mock was a direction only; the shipped art must look "sophisticated and serious like the game itself": engraved line and hatching in the map's pen weights and earth palette, no cartoon shapes or bright flat fills. A Claude agent reviews screenshots at both zoom tiers against this skill before it merges.
- **Improvements, as built (branch `claude/p8-improvements`, 2026-10-06; `app/src/map/improvementArt.ts`, reviewed and revised the same day):** drawn live as small cached sprites per (kind, form, size), clipped to the hex and under every lens, overlay, unit and banner, with the colours and pen weights taken from `style.ts` (tone inks, terrain palette, `pen.hair/thin/line`); no solid fills. Normal zoom (hex radius 22 px and up) changes the land: Farm is a light plot of ink contour furrows over a pale tilled wash that keeps the terrain colour, under a light hedge (Irrigated Farm: more rows and a visible cut water channel with sluice); Pasture is a post-and-rail paddock with a single rail, trodden ground, grass strokes and a gate gap (Ranch: a second rail and a trough); Quarry is a crescent of stepped benches cut into one side of the hex, each riser hatched, a few squared blocks on the floor (Deep Quarry: another bench and a crane); Mine is a hillside shaded on its far flank with a timbered adit whose dark is ink hatching, rails and a spoil heap (Deep Mine: a light headframe). **Zoomed out the badge is a ring on the resource's own marker, not a second glyph:** a solid ink ring means improved, a dashed one dormant, on the medallion (18 to 22 px) and on the category dot (below 18 px), so the two read as a pair and nothing competes with the map pass's far-tier marks. **Dormant** is one shared check (`isDormant` in `app/src/improvements.ts`: owned, known, unimproved, strategic deposits included): the medallion fades to 62% with the dashed ring, and the picker count and tile card use the same check. The glyphs `improvement-farm/pasture/quarry/mine` serve the picker rows.
- **Art audit decisions (lead, 2026-10-05; the audit report was a working file and is not in the repository):**
  - Rewards get a small per-kind emblem set (camp, ruin, capture, growth, tech), engraved, not the player's crest.
  - Heraldry keeps three tiers: embossed gold raster crests only on grand screens (title, faction picker, victory); engraved seals and glyphs everywhere in game. No gloss gradients or heavy shadows on in-game banners. Since 2026-11-21 the crests are rendered from the seals by code, not painted (see "Heraldry as built" below).
  - Japan roots keep the player tint but read as organic: tapered and branching, with no dark outline, beads or barbs.
  - On the map, player colour is a tint: unit counters are a parchment-ink plate with a player-colour rim, and borders are a translucent wash with a thin stronger line. High-chroma fills are for small legend chips only.
  - Every lens is translucent, with the terrain readable under it (Settle, Resources).
  - Text glyphs used as icons (warning, swords, check/cross, triangles, letter badges) become real icons on the 24 grid. Every building tier and unit gets its own icon (no reused family icon, no `■` fallback).
  - The zoomed-out tier is designed: banners collapse to a seal and name, labels de-collide, and resources and ruins keep a simple mark.
  - City art becomes era- and size-scaled engraved town vignettes. `astra/city-art-eras` (unverified Sol work) is the starting point, and the user sees the result before it ships, because it changes the game's look.
  - Map colours and pen weights come from `style.ts` and tokens only.
- **Icon pass (art pass increment 1, 2026-10-05):** the icon set is solid silhouettes with knocked-out detail (the majority style; the README says so). 40 icons added: 16 building tiers, 9 faction pieces (named by the piece: `unit-wayfinder`, `building-customsbastion`, now `building-alpineredoubt`), Marksman and Siege Engine redrawn on the 24 grid, `ui-*` marks and `mark-*` map order badges. Faction glyphs were reduced to one shape each (those drawings were replaced by the historical charges on 2026-11-21). War Shrine is a standing stone with a sword cut into it point-down, guard swept toward the blade (the first stele with a straight guard read as a gravestone cross). The Four Cities milestone icon was removed on 2026-11-21 (nothing loaded it). `src/icons.test.ts` fails if a unit or building lacks its own icon.
- **Reward emblems (art pass increment 2, 2026-10-05):** every reward card wears its own engraved emblem in the medallion ring (ring in the player's colour, engraving in ink, the celebration layer's gold glow behind it) instead of the faction crest. Same engraving language as the seals (`heraldry/src/kit.mjs`), but a bare charge with no rim, so an emblem is never mistaken for a faction seal. Files `assets/rewards/emblem-<kind>.svg`, generated by `rewards/src/build-rewards.mjs`. What each means:
  - `camp` (barbarian camp cleared): a broken standard, banner torn and pole snapped, over a ring of cold hearth stones.
  - `ruin` (every ruin reward: Gold, Science, Population, free Scout): a broken arch over a plinth holding a recovered relay-star sphere.
  - `captured` (city taken, and the sober lost card): a gate with the banner raised on a staff over it.
  - `founded` (city founded): the cornerstone, relay star carved on its top, set on the claimed hex with survey stakes.
  - `growth` (a city grows to an unlock): a sprout crowned by the relay star rising from its hex.
  - `milestone` (milestones and titles, anything that adds to the chronicle's VP): the relay star in a laurel tied with a ribbon.
  - `greatwork` (a Great Work completed): a stepped monument under the relay star in the same laurel.
  - `tech` (a partner's technology): the relay star within its orbits. Your own technologies keep the unlock card's rayed seal (accent ring, branch icon), which already shares the double-ring frame.
  - The faction goal keeps the faction crest (it is the faction's own moment). Victory has its own takeover. The lost-city moment is told by the alarm screen first, so its card only exists in the report.
- **Map pass (art pass increment 3, 2026-10-06; branch `claude/art-map`):** the audit's map findings, built.
  - Lenses are washes: Settle tiers at 0.5 (0.32 remembered) with a hairline in the tier's own pigment, a thin ink line only on the three ranked tiles; Resources fades non-resource tiles with a parchment wash (0.3), keeping the biome; own-resource tiles 0.34. Numbers live in `style.ts` `lens`; the legend chips stay solid.
  - Empire colours on the map (one per faction, `theme.ts` `FACTION_PALETTE`) go through `mapPlayerColor(owner)` (`style.ts`: the faction's UI colour mixed 30% with umber, hue kept, steel stays neutral) and are never used as fills. Military counters are the muted seat colour over parchment (about 78%) with a thicker darker rim and the glyph on a small parchment inset, so the colour is the first read (lead, 2026-10-06: the first pass, thin seat rims on parchment, made red, purple and green hard to tell apart; "identify empires everywhere" outranks tint purity for small counters). Civilian counters stay parchment chits with a seat rim, so military and civilian also read. The owner roundel is a parchment disc with a seat ring and an ink numeral; banner population squares are the seat colour thinned toward parchment, ink numeral, with a small three-heads population mark before them and no seat-number circle (2026-11-07, `atlasBanner` in `sprites.ts`); every named banner carries the faction glyph at any zoom. `e2e/art-map.spec.ts` "seat colour strip" shoots all nine factions side by side. Borders: band 0.32, thin stronger line (superseded 2026-10-09 by the bold border, see legatum-ui "Territory must be readable at a glance"; `style.ts` `borders` holds the numbers, `seat.border` the palette). `seat` and `pen` (hair 0.6, thin 1, line 1.5, heavy 2.2 px) in `style.ts` are the only places for those numbers; `tone` holds the shared inks (shadow, halo, good, bad, gold).
  - Yield glyphs use the yield bar's own inks (`--y-*`), a parchment hairline instead of a dark sticker rim, numerals in Libre Franklin. No `system-ui` anywhere on the map.
  - Japan roots: sepia wood (68% sepia, 32% seat colour), 0.6 alpha, no outline, no knots; rootlets leave toward the city at a shallow angle, curl, fork once and taper to a point; all pieces fill as one path so overlaps never darken into beads or barbs. A hairline vein in the seat colour stays. Mistake to avoid: rootlets that leave at 90 degrees read as thorns.
  - Barbarians: an aged plate hatched dark, a heavy ink rim, the torn corner stained with the accent red; camps are an inked palisade (hatched stakes) with a red pennant, no cream halo, no black fill. **Units superseded 2026-11-01:** barbarian units are painted raiders (see "Barbarians are painted" below); the torn plate only stands in while a painting loads.
  - Zoomed-out tier (below 22 px per hex radius, `isFar` in `grid.ts`): banners become a seal (parchment disc, seat ring, faction glyph, a small star badge with a gilt ring on an original capital: the founder's star, filled, or outlined once you have scored it; the near banner ends in a larger version of the same seal) with the name beside it in small caps, placed right, left, below, above (own and capital cities first), dropped only where no spot is clear; units become pips (square military, round civilian, no icon or number); ruins a diamond; every known resource a category dot (green bonus, red strategic) at any zoom; region and sea names de-collide (seas, then ranges, then regions) and keep clear of seals. De-collision depends on zoom only, never on the pan, so names never flicker as the map scrolls.
  - Shots: `e2e/art-map.spec.ts` with `src/test/mapArtFixture.ts` (eight empires, parade of every seat's counters, camps, a Settler) writes the review set; set `ART_MAP_SHOTS=<dir>`.
- **Leader portraits are used (faction sheet art pass, 2026-10-06):** the eight oil paintings now appear on the faction picker, as the hero of each sheet and as round gallery thumbnails (never on the map or in game panels). They are framed like the seals: house double ink rule, a muted-gilt hairline, four-point star studs at the corners, the embossed raster crest pinned over a corner. No text over the paint. The picker loads 360 px JPEG derivatives (now `people-<faction>-360.jpg`, `sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 88 -Z 360`), not the 512 PNGs.
- **Historical faction portrait set (user-approved mapping, 2026-10-10):** Switzerland → Switzerland (16th-century Confederacy); Mongols → Mongols (13th-century Mongol Empire); Sparta → Sparta (5th-century BCE); Germany → Germany (1870s-1880s industrial/Prussian-Imperial era); Japan → Japan (Edo period); Byzantium → Byzantium (10th-11th-century Constantinople); Celts → Celts, specifically Boudica-era Britons (1st century CE); Athens → Athens (5th-century BCE). Keep names and mapping aligned with `docs/design/historical-factions/portraits.png`. These are paired representative people, not named leaders; the `people-<faction>` art is what the app shows.
- **Next consumers of the portrait frame (8.3 review):** the Tier A moments (victory, defeat, war declared, city lost alarms, foreign city card, lobby seats) are still crest-only; the 8.3 faction sheet is the reference for wiring `PortraitFrame` and `MedallionRow` into them.
- **Premium pass 2 art (2026-10-06):** no new paintings. The ringed medallion (`ui/ringMedallion.tsx`) is the faction sheet's `.md` language made general: tone rings (green, red, ink), a gilt ring (`#8f6f1c`, 3.7:1 on paper) with the holder's crest for a title or milestone won, a dashed hollow ring while open; it takes icon names, reward emblems (`emblem-*`) and `treaty-*` (the house star). The treaty emblem is built from the house star only (a ring, two interlocked rings for an Alliance, a red ring and cross for War): there is no separate treaty art to commission. Reward emblems are used small (22-26 px) in the turn report; they read as engraved silhouettes at that size, so keep them in ink or tone colour, never tinted per player.
- **Courier Hall landmark: variant C, the Portolan rose (lead pick, 2026-10-06; as built 2026-10-07 in `app/src/map/courierHall.ts`, renamed `yamRelayStation.ts` on 2026-11-22; mocks in `docs/design/courier-hall-variants.png`).**
  - A compass-rose plaque (vellum disc the town reads through, degree ring, four ink cardinal points, four intercardinal points in the holder's `mapPlayerColor`) sits on the Hall city, under banners and units. Faint rhumb lines (16 at normal zoom, 8 zoomed out) fan across the holder's land and are clipped to its border, so a captured Hall repaints in its captor's colour. Numbers live in `style.ts` `ATLAS.hall`.
  - **Walled cities:** the wall ring has towers at its north and south corners and runs close past east and west, so the rose shrinks to 0.76 of the hex there and stays inside it (full size collided with both towers).
  - **Zoomed out** the rose is dropped for a gilt-ringed badge (parchment disc, the token icon) up and left of the city seal, opposite the capital's star; the far name placer keeps clear of it. A remembered city shows it faded.
  - The token icon `building-yamrelaystation` is the rose with a fleur-de-lis on the north point, so it never reads as `unit-wayfinder` (a ring around a star). It is the goal token in the race panel, the goal card, the report lines and the build card.
  - Fallback if the rose ever reads badly: B, the gate arch and roads (needs thinning).
- **The Sparta leader is a man** (user, 2026-10-07: "can we have the person in this be a man? feels weird the way it is now"). He was regenerated from the same prompt with only the subject changed, so every use in the game (sheet, gallery, roundels, moments) picks it up through the same file names.
- **Roads: variant B, the engraved road (lead's art delegate, 2026-10-07; `app/src/map/roadArt.ts`; shots from `e2e/roads-art.spec.ts`).** Three variants were shot on generated maps at phone size at normal and zoomed-out zoom: A a single umber pen line over a vellum halo (too faint at the far tier), B a cased road (an ink rule with vellum up the middle, so two thin rules read at both zooms), C a dotted ink trail (atlas-like, but busy zoomed out). A red dashed route was dropped at the mock stage: it collides with the movement-route preview's red dash. B won. Roads flow through edge midpoints (a tile with two links is one smooth quadratic, a junction a star), stop short of a city's centre so the town reads, are drawn above terrain and below the Hall's rhumb lines and rose, banners and units, and are public: every explored road tile is drawn, remembered tiles at 0.6. The road ink is `ATLAS.tone.ink` at 0.82 and vellum 0.95; width `max(3.4, 0.17 x hex radius)` near, 3 far.
- **The user delegates art calls to the lead (2026-10-05: "use your judgment on the art").** Decide from the mocks and this skill. Show the user the result, not the options, unless a call changes the game's overall look.

- **Far-zoom marks (about 64 px, shown at 0.3–0.45 zoom) use heavier ink and a bolder silhouette than the detailed art they replace** (ui-batch-1010c review, 2026-10-11: the painted ruin and remembered-camp marks read weaker than the old bold diamond). Small resource medallions take an ink rim or shadow rather than a heavy dormant fade.

## Review-plan art decisions (user, 2026-10-18)

- **Heraldry:** historically grounded charges for every people; the user approves the concept sheet first (see `legatum-factions`). Built on 2026-11-21 for all eleven: see "Heraldry as built" below.
- **Future era:** keep the era, but repaint its painting without humanoid robots or drones: a grounded near future (civic architecture, power, rail, cultivated land).
- **Portraits:** Sparta/Athens and Switzerland/Germany pairs are redone so they differ in pose, setting and value pattern at 48 px (still two unnamed adults each).
- **Celebration paintings:** wired in two stages, nine small vignettes in reward cards first, three 16:9 takeovers later. Keshig gets hooves on an integrated base; Spearman, Tank and Drone Swarm get full-resolution sources. Plan items CQ15, A1-A8, CL33.
