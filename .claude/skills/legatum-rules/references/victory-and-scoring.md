# Victory and scoring

Part of the Legatum rules reference (index: `index.md`). Answers: How are VP scored and won (VP hold, capitals victory), what are era objectives and World Ages, and what does the game history record?
Moved verbatim from `game-rules.md` on 2026-11-22, in the original order; the original section headings are kept inside.

## 6. Victory

### Victory points (the current rule set)

Code: `core::scoring` (decadence, target, the public `army_strength` / `economy_value` / `advancement` numbers), `core::objectives` (World Ages), `economy`
(decadence in the yield breakdown), `view` (`eras`, `objective_progress`, `PlayerPublic.army` / `economy` / `advancement`). AI in `ai::objective_pursuit`
(objective strategy).

**The three crowns are gone (designer, 2026-11-11).** Largest Economy, Strongest Army and Most Advanced no longer score VP: games ended near turn 150 against a target of 250, and the crowns were surplus VP outside the target formula. `GameState::titles`, `Title`, `VpSource::Title`, `Event::TitleChanged`, `Chronicle::Crown`, `PlayerView::titles` and `PlayerPublic::crowns` are deleted, with every AI valuation that counted a crown (title investments, title denial, army-title guards, Most Advanced research denial, the crown signs in the runaway and hollow reads, the crown input of the value network and macro model). VP now come only from World Age cards, held original capitals, world wonders and faction goals; the 10 VP target is unchanged. The public army, economy and advancement numbers stay on `PlayerPublic` because the AI reads them as public facts (rival armies, rival treasuries, the win-chance features), and `army::breakdown` stays for the army forecasts; none of them scores. Era numbers and counts come from `data::era_index` / `era_count` / `ERA_NAMES`
(the nine eras of `core::eras`; **VP numbers eras 1-based**, 1 Ancient .. 6 Machine Age .. 7 World War .. 8 Modern .. 9 Future, `objectives::era_of` = `eras::current_era` + 1). World Ages cards (`age_pool`) cover eras 2..9.

**Near-victory warning and rival actions (UI only, 2026-11-11):** `celebrate/alarms.ts` raises `victoryNear` (ranked just below `victoryHold`) once when a team's VP crosses from below `target - 2` to `target - 2` or more, below the target, never on the turn its hold starts and never without a before view. The alarm, the hold alarm for a rival and the race panel's alerts (`nearestRival`) share `rivalWin.ts` / `ui/RivalWin.tsx`: "<People> are N VP from winning.", "At war with them: ...", **Declare war on <People>** (the existing declaration dialog, offered only when `warDeclaration` is legal; a block shows its reason; hidden when already at War or allied) as the alarm's one primary, else "See the race"; **Offer Alliance to X** for each empire already at War with them (existing `ProposeTreaty`, cost in red); and "Show what holds their VP" (the race panel). The turn report leads with a red (green for your own team) line for it. There is no coalition UI.

**World Ages are the only era scoring (owner, 2026-11-22: "world ages and then we'll iterate on world ages from there"; rules 50).** Built 2026-11-11 as the `objectives=deck` experiment; the experiment switch, the lobby's "Rule experiments" section, the `objectives=era` arm (three objectives per era: Build, Expand, Fight, scored once you entered the era) and everything only that arm used (the Techs, Captures, CapturesFromAhead, CapturesFromLeader, Kills and BreakLeaderHold kinds, `FightStats` captures/kills/leader counters, `Levels::techs`, `era_pool`, `GameState::capitals_taken`'s era use) were removed on 2026-11-22. `RuleExperiments` has no objectives field (a save or tape that still carries `objectives_deck` parses and ignores it); the WASM staging room's `rule_experiments` is an empty struct so an older room still parses. `objective-scale=N` remains (it scales the peaceful cards).
*Rule as the player reads it:* "When the first empire reaches a new era, a new Age begins for the whole world and one card, drawn at random, is revealed to everyone. Any empire may complete it for 1 permanent VP, whenever it can; the card never expires. Only what you do after the reveal counts." (designer, 2026-11-11: the 30-turn window was rejected, "otherwise there might not be a path to 10 points".)
`objectives::age_pool(era)` holds six
cards for eras 2-3 and seven from era 4 (Renaissance); the seeded `drawn_from_pool` (`Rng::derive(seed, [era, OBJECTIVES, 97])`,
one pick) draws ONE card per Age (user, 2026-11-11: "only one, randomly decided option per age, not 3 to choose from"), the same for every empire; it may be a Fight card, which a faction that cannot fight for it simply cannot score that Age (three of six, or three of seven from the Renaissance, are peaceful). At the end of the turn in which any living empire first reaches an era, `objectives::update` gives
every living empire an `EraRecord` for it (`entered` = the reveal turn, `base` and `levels` = its fight counters and holdings
at that moment), so a laggard can score an Age its technologies have not reached; it just cannot use the units or buildings it has not
researched. `objectives::revealed_era` is the newest Age any empire has a record for; `PlayerView.eras`, `objective_progress`
and `objective_accounting` stop there for every viewer, the AI included, so an unrevealed Age's cards are in no view (pillar 6).
`EraView.entered_by` still means "reached the era by technology". Scoring is public (`EraObjectiveScored` is visible to all).
An empire scores each Age's card once and at most one Age per turn, the oldest first (R2); after any score `restart_counting` re-snapshots
the Ages still open (R1), so what scored one Age never scores another. Counters count only what happens after the record's snapshot.
*The cards* (Tribute, a Gold payment, and Monument, a Triumphal Arch build, were cut in rules 31, designer 2026-11-13; Demobilise, disbanding 2 costly units, was cut in rules 37, designer 2026-11-15: "it could have just been gold ... it needs to be something that causes the player to take an action they otherwise wouldn't necessarily take"; the three peaceful cards were added in rules 50, 2026-11-22, so every faction has a card it can reach without war; every card is something done in play, none is bought):
- **Veterans** (`Veterans { n: 4 }`, every Age): your units earn 4 promotions. A promotion is one level gained by any of the empire's units through XP after the reveal, counted in `FightStats::promotions` at the one place a level is gained (`rules::promotion::grant_xp`, `after - before` levels). Levels a unit starts with (Sparta's veterans are created at level 1, a Company hired at level 1) or has at the reveal never count, a unit bought or built at a level counts nothing, and `restart_counting` re-snapshots it like the other counters. The meter reads "Promotions earned since the Age began: 2 of 4".
- **Humble the mighty** (`HumbleTheMighty`): capture a city from an empire ahead of the taker on VP (the turn-start `CaptureVpSnapshot`), counted in `FightStats::humbled`.
- **Breach** (`Breach`): capture a city with Walls of any tier, counted in `FightStats::walled`.
- **Gain 3 cities** (`Cities`, Renaissance on): net cities gained by founding or capture since the snapshot. Not scaled.
- **Patrons** (`Buildings { building, cities }`, every Age, Build family): "Patrons: add a <building> to N more cities", counting cities that hold the building or a later step (`objectives::Levels::buildings`), net of the snapshot. An idle building does not count. Building and count by era (8-empire counts): 2 Library 4, 3 Library 4, 4 University 4, 5 University 3, 6 Bank 3, 7 Bank 3, 8-9 Research Lab 3 (`Library`, `University`, `Bank`, `ResearchLab`). Chosen so every faction can build it: no faction replaces these lines except by a renamed equivalent, which the card names (`app/src/data.ts` faction objective text).
- **Prosperity** (`Population { n }`, every Age, Expand family): "Prosperity: grow your population by N": 2: 50, 3: 42, 4: 34, 5: 25, 6-7: 23, 8-9: 25.
- **Frontier** (`Territory { n }`, every Age, Expand family): "Frontier: gain N tiles of territory": 2: 80, 3: 62, 4: 49, 5: 35, 6-7: 31, 8-9: 33.
The three peaceful counts reuse the old era objectives' Build and Expand thresholds (near the 70th percentile of what entrants gained per era, `docs/sim-results/era-objectives/step1.md`) and are raised 12% per empire below eight and multiplied by `objective-scale` (`Objective::scaled_for`, `scaled_pool`); the draw index is taken over the scaled pool, so a lobby size never changes which card is drawn. They are a first pass to iterate on (owner, 2026-11-22).
A capital or wonder city (`objectives::prize_city`: an original capital or a city with a Great Work or faction landmark) never counts for Humble the mighty or Breach, because it already pays VP. A Celtic sack carries `prize` and `walled` on `CityRazed` so a sacked walled city still counts for Breach. Camps and ordered Razes do not count.
The AI has no paid cards: every card goes through `objective_pursuit::chosen_objective` (the `ai::objective_deck` module and the `ClaimObjective` action were removed in rules 40). Veterans is a fought card: Hard prices it as 25 turns per promotion still needed, only at war with an army (`legacy_objective_effort`), with the same cheapest-effort choice as the other fought cards; it adds no gene. The peaceful cards use the existing Cities/Population/Territory/Buildings effort estimates. Cards of an Age that has not begun are not in its view, so no term for them exists yet.
App: when an Age begins (`AgeBegan`), every player gets the **score check** takeover (`celebrate/ScoreCheck.tsx`, model `celebrate/scoreCheckCards.ts`; user, 2026-11-22), after the opener's own era-up: a score track from 0 to the target with every empire's crest on its VP, and your score sheet, one row per way to score (Age cards II to IX, rival original capitals, world wonders, your faction goal) drawn as boxes (kept, held, this Age's new card, still to win, Ages to come; a capital you hold is a held box, a Celtic sack a kept one) with how to win the next point and the VP still open that way. Every player can see it again from the Age's turn-report line ("Show score check"). It replaced the `AgeBanner` toast and the era-up's "Victory points open now" panel; the era-up in a World Ages game shows only the era. "How you win" shows the cards with progress (`app/src/ageCards.ts`). There is no claim button: a card scores at turn end when met. The sidebar's "How you win" line follows the oldest open Age (the one that scores first): "Classical Age · 1 VP", the card and your count, clickable to open the ledger. A rival scoring an Age card is one turn-report line naming the empire.
There is no capture requirement for winning by VP
  (designer, 2026-10-31: "you shouldn't be able to win with zero conquest" is to be reached by point
  design and measured, not enforced): the 10-turn VP hold is the ordinary VP win rule. Holding the last original capital also wins (rules 37, below). Turn-limit
  ranking applies only if a game explicitly configures one; the 1,200-turn simulation safety cap
  does not award a winner (designer, 2026-11-01: no turn-limit backstop for late stalemates).
  The deck-against-era report script (`tools/sim/objective-deck-report.py`) was deleted with the era arm on 2026-11-22; its last results are in `docs/sim-results/objectives/deck-2026-11-01.md`.
- **Target:** the solo target per team member (a pair needs two members' worth), held 10 turns at Standard
  (hold rules below unchanged). The solo target uses the earnable era, goal and world wonder points plus one, at most 10
  (`RuleExperiments::solo_vp_target`): with nine eras (as with seven or eight) that is 10 solo and 20 for a pair, the user's rule
  (Age cards 6 + goal 2 + world wonder 2 = 10, so a solo winner reaches it exactly, but the world wonder and some goals can change hands). On the
  old four eras the same formula gave 8 because 10 stalled 75% of 4-empire games; with seven eras no game stalled
  (256 Hard + Search games per lobby, longest 460 turns). Knob: `vp-target=N`.
- **Rival capitals (held):** 1 VP for each rival original capital an empire holds now, lost the turn anyone retakes it or it is razed; a Celtic sack keeps its point for good (`VpSource::CapitalSacked`) (rules 50; `scoring::capitals_held`, `VpSource::CapitalTaken`, end-of-turn `Event::CapitalTaken` / `Event::CapitalLost`). It stacks with the Fight cards; the 10/20 target and 10-turn hold are unchanged, and losing the point can break a hold.
- **Public treasuries (designer, 2026-11-13, rules 31):** every empire's current Gold is public to every viewer, alive or not (0 once dead): `PlayerPublic::gold` (`view.rs`), shown with a Gold icon on each empire's row in the race panel. The Hard search's determinized rival states read it instead of estimating a treasury from the public economy figure (`ai/src/search/determinize.rs`); the hidden-state tests no longer vary a rival's Gold.
- **Public strength numbers (2026-11-11, replacing the crowns):** `scoring::army_strength` (the points of every ground military unit and hangar aircraft in a city, Scouts included, civilians excluded: the higher era-scaled melee or ranged strength plus its promotion bonus minus its supply penalty, squared over 20, times HP / 100, truncated at each step), `scoring::economy_value` (city Gold + Production) and `scoring::advancement` (furthest era entered x 1000 + technologies known) are public on every `PlayerPublic` and score nothing. The AI reads them for rival armies, rival treasuries and the win-chance features. The wound-strength penalty, terrain, fortification, phalanx and class bonuses are situational and are not in the army number.
- **Decadence is off in an ordinary game (designer, 2026-10-31).** `RuleExperiments::decadence_slope` defaults to zero, and `scoring::decadence_percent_now` returns zero at that setting. No city yield, growth or Swiss interest is cut, and the UI shows no decadence line or Power explanation (`crates/core/src/experiments.rs::DEFAULT_DECADENCE_SLOPE`, `scoring.rs::decadence_percent_now`, `economy.rs::yields_for_item`, `app/src/ui/GuideContent.tsx`, `VpHoldings.tsx`, `TopBar.tsx`). Bankruptcy still disbands a unit through its ordinary upkeep rule; the separate overreach warning and reason are gone (`rules/cities.rs::pay_gold`, `view.rs::EconomyView`).
  - **One optional experiment:** `decadence-slope=c` turns on the proportional rule, with no saturation curve or Gold surcharge. Power is the trailing 50-turn average of city Food, Production, Gold and Science yields; lead is Power above the mean of living empires, including the leader. After a full window, the rate is `round(lead_percent * c / 100)`, zero at or below the mean. It cuts each city's Production, Gold and Science and Swiss treasury interest by that rate, and raises growth cost so population grows more slowly; Food itself is unchanged. Lines appear only when a cut is nonzero (`scoring.rs::power`, `mean_power`, `refresh_decadence`; `economy.rs::decadence_cut`, `decadence_growth`, `swiss_interest_cut`). Power stays in the public view because Hard's economic threat estimate reads its lead even with decadence off (`view.rs::PlayerPublic`, `crates/ai/src/threat.rs::economic_signs`).
  - The AI reads the actual rate of zero for target strain and does no marginal decadence calculation when the slope is zero (`crates/ai/src/objective_pursuit.rs::burden`, `crates/ai/src/raze.rs::raze_value`); win chance uses observed economic output and has no decadence assumption (`crates/ai/src/winchance.rs::WinFacts`).
- **Counting (R1, R2; user 2026-10-20: "progress counts from the last era score"; built 2026-10-21, kept for World Ages).** Each Age record keeps two
  snapshots from when counting began: `EraRecord.base` (`FightStats`: humbled, walled, promotions, filled from the turn's events) and `EraRecord.levels`
  (`objectives::Levels`: cities, population, tiles and, for each building, the cities holding it or a later step). A card's progress is the empire's count now minus the snapshot, never below zero, so losing a city
  is not a gain and a city, building or fight that was already there never counts. Whenever an empire scores an Age,
  `restart_counting` takes a fresh snapshot for every Age it has not scored, so what scored one Age never
  scores another and an older open Age needs fresh work.
  The snapshot is taken after the reveal turn's gains, so a city founded that turn is already in the baseline. `EraRecord::counting_since` records that turn, and the view exposes the baseline and current total for each gain count. All captures in a turn are judged against the same pre-resolution VP snapshot, before any capital point or held wonder transfers (`rules::resolve_turn`, `objectives::CaptureVpSnapshot`). A Celtic automatic sack carries the removed city's `original_capital`, `prize` and `walled` status in `CityRazed`, so it counts for the Fight cards even though the city no longer exists at objective scoring (`rules/battle.rs::capture_city`, `objectives::record_fights`). An ordered Raze is not a new capture.
  **At most one Age scores per empire per turn, the oldest first (R2)** (`objectives::update`).
  Events: `EraEntered {player, era (1-based), first}` (emitted by `eras::learn`, not by `objectives::update`; when several empires enter an era that had no first on the same turn, `eras::draw_same_turn_firsts`, right after `process_research`, draws the first with `purpose::ERA_FIRST` and rewrites `GameState::era_firsts` and the events' `first` flags, rules 50), `EraObjectiveScored`, and `AgeBegan {era (1-based), first}` (public, emitted by `objectives::update` the turn an Age's card opens, once per Age; the turn report shows it as one line that replaces the first empire's `EraEntered` line and opens the score ledger). The VP target (10 solo, 20 for a pair) and the 10-turn hold are unchanged. The world wonder
  (GreatLibrary, 2 VP) stays, and so do the faction goals (2 VP each; Egypt and Rome have none).
- On nine eras the eight earnable Age points plus a 2 VP faction goal (for a faction with one) and a held 2 VP world wonder can reach 12 (11 on eight eras, 10 on the old seven); the world wonder points now transfer with their tile (see Wonder tiles above).

**Winning by VP means holding the target** (user decision 2026-10-06). A team wins after staying at
or above its target for **10 consecutive turn ends** (`data::VP_HOLD_TURNS`).
- At the turn-end check (`scoring::update_vp_holds`) a team at/above target with no hold starts one
  with count **1** (the turn it reaches the target is the first turn held; event
  `VictoryHoldStarted`, public to everyone), and gains +1 at every later turn end. It wins at the
  turn end where the count reaches the needed number, so at Standard it holds the target on 10
  consecutive turn-end checks and wins at the end of the tenth.
- Dropping below the target (a title lost, a city captured, a goal broken) deletes the hold:
  count back to zero, `VictoryHoldBroken { turns_held, .. }`. Regaining the target starts a new
  hold from 1. An eliminated team also loses its hold.
- State: `GameState.vp_holds: BTreeMap<TeamId, u32>`, skipped when empty. Public view: `TeamStanding.victory_hold: Option<{turns_held, turns_needed}>`.
- Several teams completing on the same turn end: highest VP, then population, then a draw (the old
  tie-break order, applied to the completers only). A team that completes first wins even if another
  holder has more VP.

**Winning by capitals (designer, 2026-11-15, rules 37: "hunting every last city and Settler is tedious").** A team wins at the end of the turn in which it is the only team that still holds an original capital of its own, in a game with more than one team (`scoring::holds_own_capital`, run in `scoring::check_victory` after last-team-standing and before the VP holds; reason "{team} holds the last capital: every rival's capital has fallen"). A team holds one while any member's original capital (`City::original_capital`, a first-founded capital, never a replacement) stands and is owned by a member of the same team. It has fallen when another team owns it (an occupation lock included: the captor owns the city) or it is gone (ordered Raze, Celtic sack); retaking it puts the team back in. Every empire has its original capital from turn 1. Barbarians are no team. If no team holds its own, or two or more do, the game goes on; a team whose capital is razed can never win this way but still wins by VP. A victory by capitals is a victory, not a draw, even with VP below the target. Public: `PlayerPublic::capital_standing` (see the race panel, §10).

Other game-ending rules are unchanged: one team alive wins outright and instantly (also mid-hold);
at a configured turn limit the highest VP wins (tie = draw; checked after the holds, so a hold that
completes on that turn wins first).
Because a sacrificed VP source can no longer win on the spot, rivals always get N turns to exploit it.
AI response: every AI treats a holder as the top threat (`coalition::select` ranks holders first;
AI war, peace and targeting in `ai::diplomacy`, `ai::plan`).

### Game history (post-game report data, 2026-10-17)

- `GameState.history: history::GameHistory`, skipped while empty. `history::record(prev, &mut s, &events)` runs in `rules::resolve_turn` just before
  `s.turn += 1`, after scoring, so a winning turn's `GameWon` is recorded; `resolve_tactical_turn` does not
  record. Integer data only, no map iteration order.
- `GameHistory { first_turn, empires, chronicle }`. `empires[p]` (indexed by player id, barbarians excluded)
  holds one entry per resolved turn, entry `i` being the state after turn `first_turn + i`: `vp`
  (`scoring::vp_total`), `cities`, `population`, `science` (the turn's `science_history.last()`), `gold`,
  `army` (`scoring::title_metric` StrongestArmy), `techs`, `era` (`objectives::era_of`, 1-based).
  `first_turn` is 1 for a game recorded from its start. History that no longer lines up with the turn
  (series length or empire count) restarts at the current turn instead of failing.
- `chronicle` is a list of `history::Chronicle` entries (tag `kind`, each with `turn`) mapped from the turn's
  events in `chronicle_entry`: `Founded`, `Captured`, `Razed`, `War` (`by` set when one team declared),
  `Peace`, `Era`, `GreatWork`, `Wonder`, `Objective`, `Goal` (first completion
  only), `Eliminated`, `HoldStarted`, `HoldBroken`, `Won`, `Drawn`. Events of barbarians are skipped, except
  a capture or razing where only one side is a barbarian.
- **Hidden until the game ends:** `PlayerView.history` is `Some` only when `state.finished` and the view is
  `ViewDetail::Full` (`view::player_view_cached`); it is `None` for the Decisions and Map views and during
  play, so rivals' numbers stay private. The online host (in the host's tab) sends views as they are.
- **No backfill:** a save from another rules version is refused outright (`wasm::json::parse_state`), so history is never reconstructed from telemetry. A compatible archive is kept when its last tape hash matches the loaded state (`wasm::Session::from_save_json`: "saved archive does not match game state" otherwise); its tape hashes include the history.
