# Air

Part of the Legatum rules reference (index: `index.md`). Answers: How do aircraft, air strikes, recon, rebasing, hangars, air defence, aircraft promotion and aircraft sight work?
Moved verbatim from `game-rules.md` on 2026-11-22, in the original order; the original section headings are kept inside.

**The air layer (Machine Age to Future, with the Modern era's jets and Air Defence; `rules/air.rs`, `data::is_aircraft`).** **The Biplane** (Flight, Machine Age, 2026-11-14; user: "they should be able to attack too, but smaller radius and not overwhelming damage") is the first aircraft: strike strength 60 (design 40) within `data::BIPLANE_STRIKE_RANGE` = 3 of its city, no city bonus and **half damage to a city's HP** (`data::BIPLANE_CITY_DAMAGE_PERCENT` 50 via `data::city_hp_damage_percent` and `combat::city_damage_percent`, applied in `battle::plan_fight_on`, `city_force` and the forecast, which reports it as `BattleForecast::city_hp_percent`); it guards like a Fighter (`data::is_fighter` is true for it) within `data::BIPLANE_GUARD_RANGE` = 2 (`fighter_guard_radius`); and **every aircraft gives sight** (user 2026-11-15: a Bomber could not see its targets; before RULES_VERSION 36 only the Biplane did): its team sees every tile within `data::AIRCRAFT_SIGHT` = 4 of its city, terrain ignored, as native sight (`visibility::add_air_sight` over every `data::is_aircraft` kind, so it also makes contact), which is what a Howitzer's indirect fire and a strike's visible-target check need. It uses 1 Oil. With Air Power it upgrades to a Fighter or a Bomber, the player's choice. Fighter and Bomber (class Air) are built like units (Air Power, World War era; the Modern era's Jet Fighter and Jet Bomber, Jet Engines, are the same with one more tile of reach) but complete into a city's **hangar** (`GameState::aircraft`, `state::Aircraft {id, kind, city, hp, xp}`, ids from the unit id space; at most `data::AIR_BASE_CAP` = 2 per city, or `STEALTH_AIR_BASE_CAP` = 3 for an owner who knows Stealth Technology, enforced by `spawn_tile` and the placement/buy blockers: "The hangar is full"). They never stand on a map tile: they are not garrison (`rules::is_military` is false for aircraft), cannot be targeted on a tile, never capture, use 1 Oil supply (Biplane, Fighter, Bomber, Jet Fighter, Jet Bomber) or 1 Silicon supply (Stealth) while based but pay no Gold upkeep (`data::pays_upkeep`), earn XP and promotions like ground units (see Promotions; RULES_VERSION 27, user 2026-11-10), appear with private HP, supply, level and XP only to their own team (`PlayerView::aircraft`: `level`, `xp`, `xp_next`); a rival who can currently see their base sees aircraft kinds in `air_sightings`, and sighted aircraft and reported air battles leave `air_contacts` without exposing hidden hangars, and are destroyed with their city when it is captured or razed (`air::lose_city_aircraft`, a `UnitDestroyed` event each). Four orders, one per aircraft per turn (`Order::AirStrike {plane, target}`, `Order::AirRecon {plane, target}`, `Order::Rebase {plane, to}`, `Order::UpgradeAircraft {plane}`; validation in `air::validate_orders`, errors name the rule): any aircraft can **strike** a visible enemy unit or city (the same target choice as a ranged shot, `battle::target_at`, so a city with HP absorbs it) within its strike range from its city: a **Bomber** `BOMBER_RANGE` = 8 (user 2026-10-19; it was 5) with effective strike strength 114 and +36 against city HP (unscaled 64 and 20 in `siege_city_bonus`, before era combat scaling), a **Jet Bomber** `JET_BOMBER_RANGE` = 9 with strike strength 137 and +43 against city HP, a **Fighter** `FIGHTER_STRIKE_RANGE` = 5 (was 3; its guard radius `FIGHTER_RANGE` stays 4) with effective strike strength 103 (unscaled 58) and no city bonus, a **Jet Fighter** `JET_FIGHTER_STRIKE_RANGE` = 6 (guard radius `FIGHTER_RANGE` 4) with strike strength 124 (user 2026-10-17: "let fighters strike, but with more limited range"; `unit_stats` `range`/`ranged`, so `validate_strike`, `forecast_air_strike` and the AI read one table). **Recon** (`Order::AirRecon {plane, target}`, designer 2026-11-15: "both steps on bomber recon"; `air::validate_recon`, `air::settle_recon`, `visibility::add_recon_sight`): the aircraft spends its one order this turn to scout any tile on the map from 1 to its own strike range from its city (`unit_stats(kind).range`: Biplane 3, Fighter 5, Bomber 8, jets 6 and 9, Stealth 7 and 10), seen or not, war or not. The tiles within `data::RECON_RADIUS` = 2 of the target (19 tiles), terrain ignored, are in the owner's team's native sight for **the next turn only**: `settle_recon` records an `AirRecon {owner, target, turn}` as the last step of the ranged phase (after strikes, rebases and upgrades, so nothing changes this turn's fire or movement), and `add_recon_sight` applies it only while `state.turn == turn + 1` (the planning turn, its strike validation, its movement rounds and its end-of-turn exploration), after which `settle_recon` drops it at the next ranged phase. It is live sight of those tiles on that turn, not a snapshot, so units that moved in show; it explores the terrain for good (native sight) and can make contact like any native sight; allies share it as they share any sight, a rival team sees nothing of it. Refusals name the rule: not an aircraft ("A Scout is not an aircraft: only aircraft can scout from the air"), not yours, off the map, "pick a tile away from <city>", "target is out of range: N tiles from <city>, range R", "already has an order this turn (one per aircraft)" (so a Recon and a strike for one aircraft cannot both stand; the first listed wins, the second is refused). A Fighter that recons still guards (like a rebasing Fighter; `used_fighters` is filled only by valid strikes). The AI has no Recon yet and reads only its own `PlayerView`. App: the Recon button on the aircraft card (key A, `AirWing.tsx`) lights every on-map tile in range (`airWing.ts::reconTargets`), a click on the map orders it (`MapCanvas.tsx`), Esc closes the picker. A strike resolves in the ranged phase with no retaliation (a temporary map unit with the aircraft's own id stands in its city for the stage, so the existing fight, event and damage code runs unchanged, then returns to the hangar with its HP). **Aircraft ignore line of sight** (user 2026-10-18: a Bomber "blocked by line of sight" over a Forest): `los::ranged_clear` is true for class Air as for Siege, so a strike needs only the target within range of its city and visible to its team (`battle::check_visible`). A Fighter or Bomber can strike at an existing War **or a War declared this turn** (decision D7): `rules::validate_orders` uses `units::declared_wars` and checks air orders against the same projected state as ranged orders (a surprise war from a valid `DeclareWar` in the list, `diplomacy::surprise_war`), and `resolve_turn` opens surprise wars before the ranged phase, so the strike resolves the same turn as a ground attack does; a War that only starts next turn does not open it. A **Fighter on guard** intercepts: for each strike in aircraft-id order (a Bomber's or a Fighter's), the lowest-id eligible enemy Fighter (at war with the striker's owner, not yet used this turn) whose city is within its guard radius (4 for Fighter, 5 for Stealth Fighter) of the target tile meets the striker in a melee exchange instead; a Stealth Bomber can only be intercepted by a Stealth Fighter (`air::STAGE_AIR`; the strike does not land, both take damage). **Air Defence (Guided Missiles, Modern era; `BuildingKind::AirDefence`, 320 Production, no yield, no hangar slot; designer, 2026-11-14):** a strike on the tile of a hostile city that has one is met the same way, before any Fighter, once per Air Defence per turn (`air::air_defence_at`): a temporary Jet Fighter (`air::AIR_DEFENCE_INTERCEPTOR`, full HP, the Jet Fighter's own strength and the city tile's terrain, no fortification) takes the Fighter's place in the same melee duel, the strike does not land and the striker takes the damage; the Air Defence takes none and the temporary unit never survives the phase (not in the hangar, not on the map after). It guards its own city's tile only, not the tiles around it. A Stealth Bomber passes it exactly as it passes an ordinary Fighter. The strike forecast shows it: `BattleForecast::air_defence` (`AirDefenceForecast`: the interceptor's and striker's strengths, the expected damage to the striking aircraft and its chance to be lost) when the attacked enemy city is seen with an Air Defence (`CityView::has_air_defence`, kept in `CityMemory`), none for a Stealth Bomber. The battle event names the interceptor as a Jet Fighter (placeholder wording until the Modern era's art pass). **A Fighter with a valid strike order this turn has used its one order and cannot intercept** (`resolve_ranged_phase` validates strikes before marking Fighters used); a Fighter whose strike fails validation, one with no order, or one rebasing, still intercepts. A **rebase** moves the aircraft to any other own city with room, at any distance (user 2026-11-02: "it just makes the logistics of moving them around easier"; `data::REBASE_RANGE` = 10 is gone, and the only checks left are own city, not the current base, and hangar room); duplicate orders do not reserve extra hangar slots (`air::validate_orders`). Rebases apply sequentially in aircraft id order, so two aircraft cannot swap between full hangars. It resolves after the ranged phase (it cannot strike that turn; `Event::AircraftRebased`). `forecast_attack_mode` forecasts a hangar plane's strike (`forecast_air_strike`, Bomber or Fighter; a Fighter's interception is not predicted, rival hangars are hidden, but an Air Defence's is, since the building is public). AI: `crates/ai/src/air.rs` (a Bomber or Stealth Bomber strikes the best target in range every turn, scored by forecast damage times the target's cost; a Fighter strikes or stays on guard (gene `fighter_strike`, default 1; 0 keeps it always on guard): it guards while a rival at war in the Flight era or later has a city within its era’s longest strike plus this Fighter’s guard radius (Biplane 3 for a Machine Age rival, Bomber 8 from Air Power's era, Jet Bomber 9 from Jet Engines', 10 for the Future era; `air.rs::bombers_likely`), unless a strike is worth more than `GUARD_VALUE`; cities base a Bomber when a war is within reach (a Biplane, valued below it, when a war is within the Biplane's reach), and a Fighter to cover Bombers, one aircraft per city; a Biplane upgrades along its default line (Fighter) when the Gold allows (`hold_or_upgrade`, which asks for a spare of the new form's resource only when it changes); `air_rebase` defaults on and moves idle planes to strictly better own hangars (every own city is a candidate, by value) or out of endangered bases). App: the city panel's Air wing (`ui/AirWing.tsx`, states in `airWing.ts`: ordered, ready, guard, idle); every aircraft with no order for the turn is an `air` decision ("needs orders") in the Return queue and the dock count (`turnFlow.ts`, `airWing.aircraftNeedingOrders`), with a target in reach or not (user 2026-10-18: a bought Bomber never came up): its card offers Strike (targets with forecasts; a team at Peace appears as "declare War and strike", `commands.promptWar`, and a War queued this turn makes its targets strikeable at once), "Stay on guard" (Fighter) / "Hold" (answers it for the turn, `store.airHeld`), Rebase, and Sleep (`store.sleepAir`, `Hold.asleep`: asks nothing until an enemy is in strike range), Fighters answered "stay on guard" or asleep are listed in This Turn (`ui/AirWatch.tsx`), the map rings the cover while the city is selected (`map/airOverlay.ts`: Biplane guard 2 and strike 3; Bomber strike 8, Fighter guard 4 and strike 5; Jet Bomber strike 9, Jet Fighter guard 4 and strike 6; Stealth Bomber strike 10, Stealth Fighter guard 5 and strike 7) and a finished aircraft gets a card stating its job. Not built: aircraft have no map art, banner badge or far-zoom mark (they are not on the map), and no Fighter patrol of its own units.

**Aircraft promote the same way (RULES_VERSION 27, user 2026-11-10):** `Aircraft::xp` (serde default 0, omitted at 0, so old saves load) rides on the temporary map unit that stands in for a plane during the ranged phase (`air::ghost`, written back with its HP), so the ground-unit code runs unchanged: a striker earns the ranged-shot XP (or the city-attack XP, 3, or 10 for the blow that drops the city to 0), an interception is a melee exchange that gives the striker the attacker XP and the guard the defender XP, and the level adds the same 10% of base strength per level to the strike and interception strength as its own breakdown line ("Level N: +X%") in the forecast (`forecast_air_strike` reads `AircraftView::level`) and the resolved Battle alike. XP and level survive the Stealth upgrade and any rebase. Sparta's rule reads on "military", so Spartan aircraft start at level 1 (`promotion::starting_xp`, shared with `unit_with_id`), earn XP +50% and may reach level 6; the War Shrine start bonus is not implemented in code for any unit. A promotion is a `UnitPromoted` event (the aircraft's id) like a ground unit's, and `army::aircraft_value` (Strongest Army) counts the level. A Bomber levels risk-free when nothing intercepts it (accepted by the designer, no limiter). Only the enemy/own-strength term of fight XP doubles on a kill (`unit_fight_xp`). Fights with barbarians award XP only until the first promotion, at the first threshold (`barbarians::BARBARIAN_XP_MAX_LEVEL`, `cap_barbarian_xp`).

**Aircraft sight (2026-11-15):** every aircraft in a hangar sees `data::AIRCRAFT_SIGHT` = 4 tiles around its city, terrain ignored (`visibility::add_air_sight`), and an ordered Recon adds `data::RECON_RADIUS` = 2 tiles around its target for one turn (`add_recon_sight`; the air layer, §Eras).
