# Units

Part of the Legatum rules reference (index: `index.md`). Answers: What units exist, their costs and classes, the unit triangle, Siege, one unit per class per era?
Moved verbatim from `game-rules.md` on 2026-11-22, in the original order; the original section headings are kept inside.

**Indirect fire and built-in Siege (2026-10-31).** Steel lets Howitzers (2026-11-14), Artillery and Combat Drones shoot through Forest, Hills and Mountains, but the target must be visible to their team. This applies to order validation and combat forecasting (`rules::battle::ranged_target`, `forecast::forecast_attack_mode`); shots remain range 2 and cannot capture a city. Both kinds already receive full built-in Siege from the Industrial rule (`data::built_in_siege`, `siege_cover`): they ignore Walls, Castles and Star Forts without buying Siege or using Iron for it. Hard uses the same indirect-fire check for target selection and siege positioning, prices visible enemy gun fire with actual wall cover, and places a melee screen beside a positioned gun (`ai/tactics.rs`, `military.rs`, `siege.rs`).

Orders and the wire format name a unit or building by its Rust identifier, which is the PascalCase of the display name (`ResearchLab` = **Research Lab**, `GreatLibrary` = **Great Library**, `YamRelayStation` = **Yam Relay Station**; `eras_tests::identifiers_renamed_in_rules_52_load_from_their_old_names` checks every one). Two exceptions keep their identifier: `TechId::Cannonry` is displayed as **Ballistics** (the name `Ballistics` in an old save means `Feudalism`, `TechId::from_name`), and `IndustryProject::{Gold, Science}` are displayed as "Industry: Gold" and "Industry: Science". `Castle` and `StarFort` are Walls tiers 2 and 3; the older names `Citadel`, `Arcology` and `Monument` also load as aliases. Walls tiers add up: Walls +10, Castle +5, Star Fort +5 city strength (`combat::city_defense_with_fort`, `fort` 0/1/2; the city fires with any tier); the Mongols build none of the three. **Railways:** `rules::units::reset_turn` gives a unit that starts its turn on a road `RAILWAY_MOVES` (2) extra moves for an empire that knows Railways, else `ROAD_MOVES` (1); app route previews use the same bonus (`app/src/path.ts::roadMoves`, `crossingFaults`).

**Units/buildings** (the listed Production cost, `data::unit_stats` / `building_stats`).. Each identifier is the PascalCase of its display name (`data::unit_name`, `data::building_name`; renamed 2026-11-22, rules 52). The names used before (`Soldier` = Spearman, `Ranger` = Archer, `Trooper` = Pikeman, `Marksman` = Crossbowman, `AssaultTrooper` = Musketman, `Laboratory` = Library, `RelayUplink` = Great Library, ...) still load from old records through the serde aliases on `UnitKind`, `BuildingKind` and `ImprovementKind` in `crates/core/src/types.rs`; the engine never writes them. The unit data is `data::unit_stats` and is exported to the app with the rules (`app/src/rules.generated.json`: `unitStats`, `factionUnitStats`, `standardUnitStats`, `factionBuildingStats`, `standardBuildingStats`, `terrainYields`, `cityGaps`, `unitNames`, `unitOrder`, `classBonusPercent`, `mountedStrengthPercent`, `mountedCityPenaltyPercent`).

**Three land classes and the triangle** (`data::unit_class`, `combat::class_bonus`; user decision 2026-10-10, "just 3 types"; normal melee is the spear line): the **melee line** beats **mounted**, **ranged** beats the melee line (by range, no bonus), **mounted** beats ranged. The **Military Engineer** is a civilian, not a class (§4, "Walls and the Military Engineer"). **A unit gains `combat::CLASS_BONUS_PERCENT` (40%) of its own base strength** (its faction's `unit_stats_for` strength, rounded down: `class_bonus_strength`) against the class it beats, whether it attacks or defends, in melee or in a meeting engagement: *Melee line against mounted (+40%)* (melee line unit vs a mounted unit) and *Mounted against ranged (+40%)* (mounted unit vs a ranged unit, **in melee only (designer, 2026-11-10; it was also given under fire):** when the mounted unit charges, or the two meet or fight in melee; a mounted unit that is the target of a shot, a Keshig's included, gets nothing, so a Field Cannon (65) shooting Cavalry (79) on open ground is forecast 16 damage, not 4; `combat::fight_bonus_of`, rules version 26). A percentage (designer, 2026-10-31, replacing the flat +18 of 2026-10-10), so the bonus remains 40% of the unit’s strength while both fighters are before the World War era, but later era steps can outweigh it: Spearman +8, Phalangite +10, Pikeman +12, Musketman +17, Rifleman +24; Horseman +10, Cataphract +13, Knight +16, Cuirassier +22, Cavalry +31. The bonus is its own breakdown line, label stating the percentage (`combat::MELEE_VS_MOUNTED_LABEL`, `MOUNTED_VS_RANGED_LABEL`), in every fight (`rules/battle.rs::plan_fight_on`, `combat::add_fight_class_bonus_rules` with the unit's faction) and forecast (`forecast.rs::forecast_attack_mode`), projected to `app/src/combat.ts::classBonus` through `Game.combat_projection`. The kind-only `combat::class_bonus` is the bonus in melee (a charge or meeting); `fight_class_bonus` (no faction edge) serves the AI's estimates of a fight and takes the shot into account; `class_bonus_for` / `fight_class_bonus_for` take the faction. **Mounted units are stronger than their era's melee line and cost more (designer, 2026-10-31, the Civ 6 way: its Horseman 36 against the Spearman's 25; "i basically never buy things from that class until there are tanks"):** every horse's (Horseman to Cavalry, `data::is_horse_mounted`) design strength is `data::MOUNTED_STRENGTH_PERCENT` (130%) of its era's design melee line (`mounted_design_strength`), so a spear line with its bonus is a few points ahead of a rider (Spearman 28 against the Horseman's 26), an even-to-good trade per Production rather than the old massacre (a Horseman charging a Spearman dealt 13 and took 67; now 28 and 32). **Riders storm cities badly (2026-10-31, chosen in the combat lab to keep city capture the melee line's job; the -33% itself decided by the designer on 2026-10-31):** a horse (`data::is_horse_mounted`; 2026-11-14, it was "a pre-Modern mounted unit") charging a city loses `combat::MOUNTED_CITY_PENALTY_PERCENT` (33%) of its base strength, the breakdown line *Mounted against a city (-33%)* (`combat::add_mounted_city_penalty`, in the fight and the forecast): Horseman 26 - 8 = 18, Cataphract 33 - 10, Knight 42 - 13, Cuirassier 57 - 18, Cavalry 79 - 26, a little below the era's melee line; shots (the Keshig's bow), and armour (the Machine Age EarlyTank, the Tank, the Autonomous Armour) pay nothing. Validation: `combat_scenarios --mounted` (`crates/sim/examples/combat_scenarios/mounted.rs`) and a `combat_lab` subset, `docs/sim-results/unit-classes/mounted.md`. Mounted units have 4 moves (`data::MOUNTED_MOVES`; Germany ordinary, Mongols +1 as for every unit); **the Machine Age EarlyTank has 3** (`data::EARLY_TANK_MOVES`, the proposal's "armour is slow"), **the Tank has 5 and the Autonomous Armour 6** (`data::TANK_MOVES`, `data::AUTONOMOUS_ARMOUR_MOVES`; user 2026-10-19, the 6 on the correction that the Future armour must not be slower than the Tank). **The triangle ends at the Modern era (user, 2026-10-20; this replaced the open-ground rule of 2026-10-19, which let the melee line keep its bonus against armour only in a city, Forest or Hills):** a class bonus applies only when *both* units are from before the Modern era (`combat::TRIANGLE_END_ERA_TIER` 6, the era tier of the Infantry, Artillery and Tank; the Machine Age, tier 5, is inside the triangle, so Trench Infantry gets +40% against a EarlyTank and a EarlyTank +40% charging a Howitzer; `combat::triangle_applies`, used by `class_bonus`, `fight_class_bonus` and `add_fight_class_bonus_rules`, projected to the app through `Game.combat_projection`). So an Infantry against a Cavalry or a Tank gets no *Melee line against mounted*, a Tank against an Artillery or Cavalry against an Artillery gets no *Mounted against ranged*, and the forecast simply has no line; a Rifleman against a Cavalry, or a Cavalry against a Field Cannon, still does. From the Modern era units differ by strength, cost and moves only. Unit cards for Modern and Future units say so (`unlockCardModel.unitMatchup`), and the Guide has one sentence. The bonus grows with the unit's strength until Modern, while later era steps can outgrow its advantage (the last step is +50%).

**Outrider (corrected 2026-10-31).** Any melee unit may order an attack on a visible horse (Horseman to Cavalry, `data::is_horse_mounted`; 2026-11-14, it was "pre-Modern mounted unit") from a reachable distance. If the attacker began beyond adjacency, it walks toward the horse's turn-start tile without following it. A horse that stays is attacked; one that rides away escapes, and the order ends with a MoveBlocked event. An attacker that began adjacent still pins the horse and strikes it if it steps away (G21). Ranged attacks, city strikes and mounted attacks are unchanged. There is no Spearman or Pikeman exception. Armour (the Machine Age EarlyTank, the Tank, the Main Battle Tank, the Autonomous Armour) has no Outrider (`combat.rs::outrider_blocks`, `rules/movement.rs::start_pursuits`, `aim_pursuits`, `finish_pursuits`, `forecast.rs::forecast_attack_mode`).

**Future technology rules (2026-11-01).** `Robotics` unlocks the Hunter-Killer, an armed four-legged autonomous melee unit; `HumanoidRobot` is a serde alias. `StealthTechnology` replaces Quantum Computing at the same cost and position (`QuantumComputing` is a save/tape alias). Fighters upgrade to Stealth Fighters (strike 7, guard 5) and Bombers to Stealth Bombers (strike 10, +48 against city HP); both take Silicon. Only a Stealth Fighter may intercept a Stealth Bomber (`rules/air.rs`); aircraft upgrade in a hangar with `UpgradeAircraft`, paying Gold and requiring spare Silicon. `FusionPower` adds +5 Production in each city and releases Factory and Research Lab tiers from Coal/Oil (`resources::building_need_for`). `RecursiveAi` gives +20% Production in each city (of the Production after Fusion's +5 and faction bonuses, before natural-wonder bonuses) as a separate breakdown line, and once, when it is learned, records every map tile as explored with public city, improvement and border memory for the research's team (`eras::learn`, `visibility::reveal_all_to_team`); it gives no new sight of units or combat targets, and later changes to the map stay under ordinary fog.

**One unit per class in every era (user revision, 2026-10-10; this replaced the lead's two-era-generations idea).** Each of the three classes has its own unit in each of the nine eras, unlocked by a technology of that era (Ancient: from the start; Classical: Mathematics, Engineering, Iron Working; Medieval: Feudalism, Machinery, Chivalry; Renaissance: Gunpowder (Musketman, Arquebusier), Metallurgy (Cuirassier); Industrial: Rifling, Ballistics, Military Science; Machine Age: Replaceable Parts, Steel, Combustion; World War: Mass Mobilisation, Rocketry, Armoured Warfare; Modern: Mechanised Warfare, Guided Missiles, Composite Armour; Future: Robotics, Autonomous Systems), with historical names (the Future list is a living data set). **The strength curve (user, 2026-10-18, playtest 8.9.4: "each tech era is a big jump", "the difference between a tank and a second-era pikeman should be vast"; this replaced the even steps of 2026-10-10).** **The step grows (user, 2026-10-19, replacing the flat 30%).** Each era makes the melee line stronger than the last by a step that is 5 points bigger each time, `data::ERA_STEP_PERCENT` = 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50%; the Machine Age (2026-11-14) splits the 45% Industrial-to-Modern step into 21% and 20% (1.21 x 1.20 = 1.45), so the table was 25, 30, 35, 40, 21, 20, 50%, and the Modern era (2026-11-14) adds a 20% step before the Future's: `data::ERA_STEP_PERCENT` = 25, 30, 35, 40, 21, 20, 20, 50%, `data::MELEE_LINE` = 20, 25, 32, 44, 61, 74, 89, 107, 161 (exact products rounded to the nearest, a .5 rounding down), so the steps are +5, +7, +12, +17, +13, +15, +18, +54: every World War strength is what it was before the Machine Age, and the Future, one era step on, is about 20% above what it was (Hunter-Killer 161, was 134). Every other strength is a **design number** (the old straight-line value: Knight 32, Field Cannon 47/36, Walls 10) that fixes how the unit compares with the melee line of its own era, then rides the curve: `data::era_scaled(design, era) = design * MELEE_LINE[era] / DESIGN_MELEE_LINE[era]` (`DESIGN_MELEE_LINE` = the old 20 + 6 per era), rounded half up (`DESIGN_MELEE_LINE` = 20, 26, 32, 38, 44, **49**, 50, **50**, 56 (the Modern era takes the World War era's 50, so each Modern unit keeps its World War line's design numbers and stands one 20% step above it: 107/89 = 1.20): the Machine Age's 49 lies between its neighbours and is the one value that puts its units on the proposal's numbers, a factor of 74/49 = 1.51; the proposal guessed "about 1.39"). So same-era balance between the classes is untouched, and only the gap between eras grows. `unit_stats` returns the scaled numbers (`design_stats` the design ones); the era of a unit is the era of its technology (`unit_era_tier`). Faction edges are design numbers too and are added before scaling (Byzantium +4, Celts -2 melee and +3 ranged on the archery line, the Keshig's bow 18), as are the siege bonuses vs city HP (`siege_city_bonus`: the Bomber, Jet Bomber and Stealth Bomber 20 design), so the Keshig and the Byzantium Legionary keep their edge over what they replace. Terrain, fortify, wounds and the Hoplite phalanx stay flat; the class bonus is a percentage of the unit's strength (40%, 2026-10-31), while both fighters are before Modern; later era steps can outweigh a counter. Cost still rises by a fixed ratio (melee 1.38, mounted 1.36, ranged 1.27 from a dearer base of 66). Results on the accelerating curve (mean rolls, open ground, `crates/sim/examples/era_duels.rs`): same era, the first striker wins narrowly (melee kills melee in 4 attacks with 10 HP left); one era ahead, the newer melee unit wins in three attacks with 51 to 56 HP left up to the Medieval era and in two with 81 to 90 HP left from the Renaissance on; two eras ahead it wins in two attacks with 81 to 91 HP left (one attack from the Modern era on); three eras ahead in one or two, and from the Industrial era on a unit two eras ahead kills anything in one blow. With the triangle ended at the Modern era (2026-10-20) a same-era Tank still beats an Infantry (3 attacks, 51 HP left; the Infantry attacking dies after dealing back 29) on strength alone, and a Tank kills a Rifleman, an era behind, in one attack. The old validation below was for the straight line and is kept for the same-class numbers at the start of the game. Validation with the scenario simulator (`combat_scenarios --eras`, four of the newer unit against the same Production of the older, Human policy, `resolve_tactical_turn`; `docs/sim-results/unit-classes/eras.txt`): the same class one era ahead wins 61 to 70% (melee), 61 to 67% (mounted) and 61 to 64% (ranged; 43% for the Combat Drone); two eras ahead 81 to 91% (melee), 71 to 82% (mounted) and about 50% (ranged: unanswered fire lets an older shooting swarm keep pace); three or more eras 94 to 98% (melee), 88 to 98% (mounted) and 57 to 65% (ranged). The Hard policy (the scripted AI on both sides) plays these duels timidly and reports far lower numbers (newer units 0 to 20%); read the Human column. Earlier scenario duels found a counter-class unit one era behind could win in early eras; their Future-era cavalry result conflicts with the current Modern-era triangle cutoff and is not a current-rule claim. No composition beat every archetype at 60%+ in those duels (`responses.txt`). Every existing unit can **upgrade straight to the newest researched unit of its line for Gold** (`upgrades_to`: `UPGRADE_COST_PERCENT` (200%) of the cost difference, with a breakdown in the unit panel and on the research card; the final unit must have spare supply for its resource; every skipped step is charged), so an army is not stranded by an era change; the AI upgrades idle units when the Gold and its runway allow (`military.rs::hold_or_upgrade`). The faction rules read the class, and a faction that cannot build a class (the Aztecs and horse-mounted units) is one more line in `data::faction_unavailable_reason`, as Mongols's no-Engineers rule is.

Spearman, Archer and Horseman are available from turn 1, so the triangle exists from the start. Upgrades follow `upgrades_to` through every researched step in one order (summed Gold price, `rules/units.rs::upgrade_plan`, `check_upgrade`; **the one branching upgrade (user, 2026-11-14):** a Biplane's first step is the player's choice of Fighter or Bomber (`data::upgrade_choices`, `upgrade_plan_via`, `Order::UpgradeAircraft { plane, to }`, `to` None = the default Fighter), then the chain goes on along that line (a Bomber to a Stealth Bomber once known); the Air wing shows one Upgrade row per line with its price, `AircraftView::upgrade_options`; final supply only, `resources::unit_upgrade_need_to`; every land military unit from the Classical era on uses 1 of its era's resource while it exists, so its builds and upgrades need a spare one, and an upgrade switches to the new era's resource: §3 "Each era is defined by its resource", `resources::unit_need`, `upgrade_need`). Faction rules read the class: the Sparta melee line ignores wound penalties and Oathbearers replace Spearmen; the Byzantium Pikeman is the Tagmatic Soldier, and it and every later melee-line unit get +4 design strength (era-scaled: +4 to +10) and +10 Production (`data::unit_stats_for`); the Mongols Cataphract is the Keshig, a mounted unit with a bow (§8); Mongols raise no Walls line and buy no Siege before the Medieval era (`data::MONGOL_WALLS_ERA` 2); Germany has ordinary unit movement. The AI chooses its mix by value: `crates/ai/src/composition.rs` (ladders, era quality) and `production.rs::composition_bonus` (what a class is worth next to the enemy army it can see, a balance term when it sees none, and a rush bonus for mounted).
