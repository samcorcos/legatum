# Movement, roads and routes

Part of the Legatum rules reference (index: `index.md`). Answers: How do units move, path, pass through friends, use roads, and avoid enemy reach?
Moved verbatim from `game-rules.md` on 2026-11-22, in the original order; the original section headings are kept inside.

**Movement**: a unit can always take ≥1 step even if it can't afford full terrain cost. `MoveUnit`
is a persistent multi-turn goto; `MoveUnitPath` is a confirmed path re-validated each round. Meeting
engagements (two hostiles into the same tile, or a swap) get no terrain/fortify bonus either side.
Two hostile military claimants entering a tile held only by a hostile civilian meet as on empty
ground. The survivor advances and captures the civilian once; a city or military holder still
defends its tile (`rules/movement.rs::movement_round`, meeting claimants and capture pass).
At 0 HP a hostile city can be captured by a melee claimant when its garrison steps out in the
same round. The departing garrison is a movement dependency; if it remains, the claimant stays
out. The owner's own units stepping in that round are its supporters instead (§4, "Supported defence"). Among friendly claimants, only one that can advance is selected to capture, with another
eligible claimant taking over in the same round if the preferred one is blocked
(`rules/movement.rs::movement_round`, `team_captors` and dependency pass).
No named zone-of-control, but pathing routes around hostile units (`rules/movement.rs::next_step`).
Peaceful foreign cities block routes; a unit
cannot stop on that city (`rules/movement.rs::next_step,movement_round`,
`app/src/path.ts::previewRoute`, `app/src/map/reach.ts::reachTiles`).
An allied city is transit-only: a unit may cross its tile during a route but may not end a move
there. Crossing the city and reaching the first tile beyond it is one atomic move; if the unit
cannot pay for the exit this turn, it stays outside the city or takes another route (`rules/movement.rs::next_step`,
`movement_round`). An order targeting one is refused with “Allied units can pass through Bern but can't stop
there.” A unit already in an allied city must move out on its next move. Order validation and the
scenario state validator mirror this (`rules/units.rs::allied_city_destination`,
`scenario::validate_state`). AI destination selection, client path preview apply the
same endpoint rule while leaving the city traversable en route (`proxy_v7.rs::go`, `diplomacy.ts`,
`path.ts::previewRoute`, `map/reach.ts::reachTiles`). Allied same-class units are likewise transit-only;
`movement.rs::stationary_friend` and `movement_round` hop across their tiles atomically.

**Roads (2026-10-07; laid by the Courier Hall, §8, and by any empire's Roadworks, §3).** `Tile::road` (a link mask, bit d = neighbour d, `roads::NODE` = road tile) and `Map::road_hub`. **Entering a road tile costs 1 move whatever its terrain** (`Map::move_cost`, `TileView::move_cost`, client path preview), and **a unit that starts its turn on a road tile has +1 move** (`rules::units::reset_turn`; `moves_left` can exceed the unit's max). Roads never grant passage: `passage_allowed` is unchanged, so Peace borders stay closed except in a Hall holder's land. Every cost consumer reads the same function: engine steps, `movement_path`, the AI's `Ctx` cost table and route searches, `settlement`, forecasts, and the client's preview, reach lens and movement lens. `path::find_path_by_step` on a map with roads scales costs by 256 and takes 1 off each road tile, so equal-cost routes prefer roads (a map without roads searches exactly as before, so old tapes are unchanged). `path::path_turns` gives the +1 to a unit that starts a later turn on a road. The view exposes `TileView.road` on every explored tile, always current (public). Client preview: `app/src/data.ts::tileMoveCost`, `app/src/path.ts` (`findPath` road tie-break, `pathTurns`, `planTurns`, `roadMoves`).

**Passing through your own units (2026-10-21, Civ 6; replaces "Friends on the route", 2026-10-03, playtest-6.2 "The Last Bridge").** Still one unit per class per tile at the end of every movement round, but a unit may *pass through* a same-class friend of its team, or its own garrisoned city (the garrison is the friend), during its move; it may not *end* its move on one. User: "it should be reasonable for this unit to be able to walk through the city and through my unit to get to the other side." A friend **stays** this round when it is parked (no goto, no moves, stopped) or attacking in place (goto is an adjacent enemy unit or city, `movement.rs::attacking_in_place`); that includes a friend out of moves for now that still has a destination. One costing chooses every land route (`crates/core/src/path.rs::route_around_friends`), used by resolution (`movement.rs::next_step`, confirmed `MoveUnitPath` steps included), the AI's movement preflight (`crates/ai/src/movement.rs::movement_conflict`) and the client preview (`app/src/path.ts:routeAroundFriends,previewRoute`). Rules: (1) **A hop.** When a unit's route starts across friends that stay, it crosses the whole run in one atomic move and lands on the first tile beyond (`path::hop`, `movement.rs::movement_round`): it pays every tile it crossed, `UnitMoved` goes from its tile to the landing, and no round ever ends with it on a friend. The landing goes through the usual claim rules (unit-id order, reservations, bounces), so two units hopping to one tile still take turns. (2) **Stopping short.** A hop it cannot pay for this round (`total > moves_left`, but it fits full moves) waits for next turn on the last free tile it passed, keeping its destination. A hop with no landing is the plain step into the friend, which fails as before with MoveBlocked "blocked by P0's Soldier, which did not move" (or "...which stayed to attack"; `movement.rs::stayed_reason`, the client says "your Soldier", `app/src/format.ts`): the goto is the friend's tile (the unit comes up beside it), an enemy or neutral unit or city holds the landing (an attack cannot start from two tiles away, so such a route goes round the friend instead), or the crossing costs more than the unit's full moves (a friend on hills plus the tile beyond for a 2-move Soldier; two friends in a row). Planning never picks such a crossing when another route exists (`path::crossing_faults`: that run's first tile is a wall for the search); with none, the plain terrain route is kept and the unit waits behind the friend (a choke). (3) **Waiting friends (2026-10-09, playtest 8.6 "units moved backwards").** A friend that stays only for now (out of moves, same team, still has a goto) whose crossing cannot be made is waited for, not circled: the unit walks round only if the way round takes fewer turns than waiting behind it (`route_around_friends`'s `waiting` argument; resolver `next_step` and AI preflight). Parked friends and attackers staying put are walls for such a crossing. (4) A friend with a move out this round is followable (friendly chains move together) but risky, so a route avoiding every such tile wins whenever it arrives in as few turns (`path::path_turns`, which treats a hop as one atomic move that waits a turn when it does not fit). (5) A route through friends that stay wins over a way round unless the way round arrives in fewer turns. (6) A tile a lower-id friend only passes through this turn is reserved for one movement round, not walled for the turn. A follower may request it and wait one round under the same-owner claim rule, keeping its moves; when that route arrives no later in turns than going round, it follows the friend. A tile where the friend ends its move or stays to attack remains a wall for that round of route planning, and a shared final destination still uses the ordinary claim and bounce rules. The preview shows no warning for a crossing it can make; it says "Moves through this tile right behind your Soldier." (neutral ink, `isCalmRouteNote`) for a transit reservation, "Your Soldier is using the direct path this turn, so this unit takes another route." or "Direct path blocked by your Soldier, which stays put this turn: taking a longer route." when it goes round, "Path blocked by your Soldier, which stops on this tile this turn: this unit waits behind it." when the friend will stop in a choke, and warns "Path may be blocked by your Soldier: it must move out first." for a friend that must leave, "Path blocked by your Soldier, which stays put this turn: this unit waits behind it." for a crossing that cannot be made, and "Your Soldier stays on that tile: this unit can't end its move on it and stops beside it." for a destination held by a friend (`path.ts:routeRiskText`); `RoutePreview.crossed` lists the friends passed through so no turn marker lands on one (`planTurns(..., crossable)`). The preview card headline gives the turns to arrive and, for a route much longer than the straight line, says it bends round terrain or units (`path.ts:routeTurnsText`). Ties break on hex order. Several units ordered onto one city declare in unit-id order; shared final tiles still use the normal claim and bounce rules, while units can follow through earlier claimed transit tiles. The client preview only reserves the first pending step for a lower-id unit: a later unit's speculative route or an earlier unit's future step cannot preempt this mover (`app/src/path.ts::previewRoute`). Reissuing a confirmed move recalculates its path against current orders (`app/src/commands.ts::moveSelected`). Allied same-class units are parked friends for crossing; they and allied cities are transit-only (`movement.rs::stationary_friend`, `app/src/path.ts::previewRoute`).
**Routes avoid known enemy reach (player previews only, 2026-10-11, p881).** A route plotted by the player (the hover preview and the order `moveSelected` queues, both `previewRoute(..., { avoidEnemyReach: true })`; the AI's `previewPath` calls leave it off) prefers, among routes that take the same number of turns, steps that no visible enemy military unit can strike (`app/src/enemyReach.ts`). Model: a unit that steps away is hit where it stood (G21), every unit takes one step per round, so the j-th tile of this turn's route (where the unit stands at the start of round j+1) is dangerous when an enemy with at least j+1 moves can be next to it within j steps (the turn's last tile: within moves-1 steps); ranged shots and city fire land before movement and cannot be dodged by routing, and the destination is the player's choice and never counted. `path.ts::saferRoute` re-plans with the dangerous tiles pruned (up to four times), keeps the original when nothing as short exists, and sets `danger` ('leaving' when the first step is exposed, else 'way'); the preview shows "Enemies can catch this unit leaving." / "...on the way." A route to an enemy's tile (an attack) is never rerouted, and a confirmed `MoveUnitPath` is only checked, never replaced. The core's own pathing (`path.rs`) is unchanged: it follows the submitted path.
All factions, including Mongols, route around occupied enemy tiles and take parting hits when struck while leaving; moving civilians remain pinned by a hostile military unit entering their tile (`rules/movement.rs::next_step,movement_round`). A confirmed route through a friend is legal (`units.rs::check_known_path`).
