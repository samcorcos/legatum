# AI: Hard genes and recent AI decisions

Part of the Legatum rules reference (index: `index.md`). Answers: What AI-only genes and decision rules (Hard defence, purchases, campaigns, Settlers, Peace, coalitions, faction playbooks) exist and what are their defaults?
Moved verbatim from `game-rules.md` on 2026-11-22, in the original order; the original section headings are kept inside.

## 10. AI architecture

**Hard threatened-city production (AI only, 2026-11-20; `crates/ai/src/production.rs::ranged_relief_needed`, `item_value`, `plan_emergency_production`; gene `production_threat_agreement` default 1).** When a visible enemy can bombard a city without a friendly ranged reply, or hostile military is within three hexes of a garrisoned city with no shooter nearby, the routine production score values a trainable ranged defender above a routine melee quota. A garrisoned or walled city with threat exposure also values that defender when a visible rival army is within four hexes, including before war. The emergency pass uses the same read, but leaves a shooter-only production choice to routine scoring; it still interrupts for a local defence deficit or exposed capture. The ranged value is applied after general production and modernization weights so those weights cannot recreate the pass disagreement, even when its ordinary quota score is zero. Outside an opening, threat exposure plus a visible rival within four hexes also stops routine scoring from resuming a Settler that the threat pass interrupts. Gene 0 restores the earlier behavior. This changes AI choice only; order legality, combat and the rules version are unchanged.

**Hard siege survival clock (AI only, 2026-11-21; `purchase_defense.rs::city_survival_clock`, gene `siege_survival_clock`, default 0 since 2026-11-21 after the held-out Athens and Rome hybrid cells scored 8.6 with it on against 25.6 off; 1 turns it on).** Hard forecasts up to eight turns of attacks on each threatened own city from units in its player view. Each round uses the engine combat forecast against current city HP, Walls and garrison; the projection carries wounded attackers, melee retaliation, the walled city strike, rest healing and visible attackers advancing into range. `DefenseRisk.turns_to_fall` is the first projected capture turn, counted from the current order. If the visible force breaches the HP shield but cannot finish the garrison, its breach turn plus two garrison rounds supplies a provisional production deadline. Hold, rescue, door production, emergency production, purchases, upgrade holds and the Walls deadline read this forecast. An immediate purchase gets rescue priority only when it prevents this turn's fall; otherwise a delay still faces the normal Gold and upgrade value checks. A unit or Walls option that finishes on or after the deadline cannot rescue that city; among timely unit builds, the score credits the turns left for it to defend. A city with no forecast capture within the window retains the previous broader front valuation. The old optional `siege_matchup` pass and gene were removed; its separate six-turn approximation did not share the live hold forecast. Gene 0 (the default) keeps the former one-turn danger read and build timing; gene 1 turns the clock on for paired measurement. The clock is conditional on visible attackers continuing their assault; it does not reveal units in fog or know future orders. On the Sparta 8.9.57 T29 Gergovia state, two reinforcements are still unseen by the Celts, so the legal clock does not predict the T32 capture until they enter view. This changes AI decisions only, not combat, order legality or `RULES_VERSION`.

**Egypt on the Nile, AI (2026-11-21).** All difficulties (`production.rs::egypt_nile_choice`, applied to every `choose_item` result): Settlers, the Heir and combat units pass through; an idle city chooses Grow; otherwise the city chooses Grow when the Food still missing from its growth threshold is repaid by the extra surplus of one more population within `egypt_grow_payback` turns (gene, default 30, capped at a third of the turns left), the extra surplus estimated as the city's doubled Food per worked tile less 2. A city on Grow is reconsidered every turn like any project, so it returns to building once growth stops paying. The increment-1 Hard gene below applies only to the old rule.

**Egypt Hard, increment 1 (2026-11-15).** The `egypt_food_buildings` gene has shared default 0 and Egyptian Hard builtin 1 (`crates/ai/src/params.rs`). Its production picker reads only its own `PlayerView`: building option turns come from current surplus Food rather than Production, a zero-surplus building is rejected or parked so growth can resume, and after the Royal Granary a city below population three trains or expands to resume growth before more optional buildings. Units, improvements and wonders retain their normal Production valuation. The first increment does not yet model deeper multi-city growth opportunity cost or the planned faction goal.

**Campaign supply cap (AI only, 2026-11-15; `crates/ai/src/plan.rs::price_campaign`, gene `supply_race_cap` default 1 for Hard).** A campaign still values a captured deposit using `supply_term` and `supply_shortage_percent`, but the separate supply term cannot exceed the candidate's largest positive capture, victory-race, or denial term. The comparison includes the capture's win-chance change, VP hold closeout, elimination path, coalition city prize, rival objective denial, and breaking a hold; it does not use an unrelated soft-target or war-footing bonus as the ceiling. With no positive race or denial term, supply contributes zero. Setting the gene to 0 restores the uncapped term. The public economy projection, resource supply, city capture rules, and save shape are unchanged.

**Hard named Gold fund (2026-11-15, AI only; `named_fund` default 0 for Hard after the purchase gate failed; 1 turns it on).**
`gold_hold.rs::named_fund` chooses one use from the Hard AI's own `PlayerView` and routed plan:
first a visible at-war army within eight tiles of an own city with fewer than two nearby troops
(two defenders that survive a visible attack, ranked by the purchase scorer when enabled,
released at three tiles),
then the whole next-unit-tech upgrade bill for combat units when research is due within ten turns
(released when known), then a Campaign or Contain army purchase for a known target (the priced
`decisive_buy` candidate when one changes capture odds, released once a field unit reaches
staging range). The one-city
purchase-slot rule still applies: a defensive burst buys in the exposed city and nearby cities
where slots and Gold allow; it can cancel queued Siege refits to free the Iron and Gold for them.
The burst respects the immediate upkeep deficit and a live faction goal floor. Routine Gold buys
and single ground refits wait while the fund is
named. Its quote, not 25 turns of net income, is the hold ceiling; the older income cap still
applies when no fund qualifies. With `hard_purchase_scorer` 0, the affected troop-buy passes put
the newest affordable unit era ahead of strength and price, subject to the generation floor below; with the scorer on, its forecast value
chooses the fund's unit and each purchase (`gold_hold.rs`, `hard_defense.rs::second_troop_buy`,
`production.rs` purchase passes, `modernization.rs::plan_modernization`). This changes no core
combat, save shape or rules version.

The comparison includes the capture's win-chance change, VP hold closeout, elimination path, coalition city prize, rival objective denial, and breaking a hold; it does not use an unrelated soft-target or war-footing bonus as the ceiling. **Hard supported defence (AI only, 2026-11-15; `crates/ai/src/hard_defense.rs::supported_defence_orders`, gene `supported_defence` default 1, 0 restores the former orders).** After ordinary movement reconciliation, Hard checks visible enemy melee units that can reach an own city within two turns, including a rival that could legally declare War. Before a legal declaration, it stages a garrison and nearby melee reserves when at least two of its own non-Scout melee troops are available; a sole escort is left on its existing assignment. During an ongoing war it changes orders only when at least three visible melee attackers can strike immediately and the engine can forecast their pooled assault. In a multi-city empire, this pass preserves field assignments under a routed Campaign or Contain plan, including staging before another rival declares; a last-city Campaign or Contain yields to a visible non-Scout melee attacker, but not to Scouts alone. It leaves existing ranged garrisons to their fire orders. During that imminent assault, a melee garrison ordered out stays unless an adjacent defender has better HP per worst average hit against the visible attackers and can exchange places. Hard prices up to two reachable non-ranged supporters with the core's shared-damage and pooled-counterattack forecast, charging their expected wounds and the damage they would take outside. Ranged units remain available to fire from their existing tile, and enter an empty city only when no melee defender can reach it. This changes AI orders only; supported-defence resolution remains §4 and `RULES_VERSION` stays 40.

**Capitals victory in Hard (AI only, 2026-11-15, rules 37; `crates/ai/src/capitals.rs`; gene `capitals` default 1, 0 switches the guard and target terms off for ablation and old-behaviour tests; constant `winchance::CAPITAL_STAKE`).** Reads only public `PlayerPublic::capital_stands` and the fogged view. (1) Win chance: `TeamFacts::capital` per team; every team still holding a capital shares `CAPITAL_STAKE` 4,000 milli-logits (500 with eight holders, 2,000 with two) and the chance is 10,000 when we are the only holder and 0 when a rival is; `plan.rs` projects a capture of a standing rival capital (drops its flag) or of our team's fallen capital (restores ours) through `project_capital_capture`. (2) Own capital: `capital_pressed` is true while our standing original capital has a known rival military unit (not a Scout, not barbarian; seen at war or under a legal declaration, or remembered) within `CAPITAL_REACH` 6 hexes that can walk there. Then `secure_capital` (first pass in `plan_military`) keeps one troop in it (a shooter first) out of every other pass or calls the nearest troop that can defend it (never a city's only garrison); `garrison_cities` no longer skips it as sheltered or spared; `hold_capital_guard` holds the last troop whatever `capital_guard` says (the gene `capital_guard` itself stays 0); `production.rs` values a missing garrison and Walls there at 4,000, above the garrison floor (2,200) and every other build. (3) Rival capitals: `capital_race_vp` adds, on top of `capital_capture_vp`'s +1, a bonus to campaign, conquest, opportunity and decisive-buy target values (100 per point): 4, 2, 2, 1... as fewer rivals still hold one, 10 for the last standing rival capital while ours stands (the win), 0 without a capital of our own, 10 to retake our team's fallen capital (2 when another member's still stands), 5 to restore a third team's capital when we have none and exactly two teams hold one. `capital_restore_vp` adds the same scale to `rival_capital_vp` in `raze_value`/production's raze test (razing keeps the founder from retaking). Sim: `Outcome::Capitals` / `VictoryType::Capitals` (`stats.rs::winner_holds_last_capital`), and `human-proxy-report.py` counts `capitals`. Not done: defending an ally's or another rival's standing capital (only denial by retaking a fallen third-team capital and by taking the leader's), and `city_lost` does not price losing our own capital.

**Hard military purchases (2026-11-15, gene `hard_purchase_scorer`, default 1).** Every military Gold-buy path uses `production.rs::military_purchase_score`: door, readiness, emergency, frontline, second Settler escort, surplus army, campaign reinforcement and the threat pass. The price includes the researched refit chain a newly bought old unit would still need. Combat value uses visible rival units, the engine's forecast for a legal attack from the purchase city, the combat damage and class-bonus functions for other prospective fights, endurance through two exchanges, and movement. A unit that would die without changing the outcome is therefore a poor bargain even when its listed Gold price is low. The door and second Settler escort passes also wait up to five income turns for a trainable option scoring at least twice as well per Gold when the city survives this turn (`military_purchase_viable`). A city that would fall this turn can still buy the old unit if `defense_risk` says that purchase saves it. A healthy city with strong intrinsic defence can also buy an old garrison against a visible door front arriving next turn, since the city shields that unit through the first exchange. When the current research completes within three forecast turns and upgrades an owned combat unit, optional military buys wait if the city's no-purchase forecast survives; rescue buys that prevent a fall do not wait (`production.rs::military_upgrade_hold`). Gene 0 restores the prior scorer and hold behavior, with the separate generation floor still applying if enabled. The AI reads only its own `PlayerView`; this changes no combat rule, order legality or `RULES_VERSION`.

**Hard purchase generation floor (2026-11-15, AI only; gene `purchase_generation_floor` default 1 for Hard).** Beneath the shared purchase scorer, each military Gold-buy pass can buy only the newest researched unit in the needed melee, ranged or mounted upgrade line that its city can train. If that unit is blocked by strategic supply, only its immediate predecessor on the same line is eligible. If the eligible unit costs more Gold than available, the order is dropped and Gold stays in the treasury. An empty city forecast to fall this turn may instead buy the cheapest affordable unit whose added body prevents the fall. `production.rs::enforce_purchase_generation` checks final orders after every buy pass so a pass with an incomplete shortlist cannot bypass the floor; 0 restores the former orders. When a named fund pays for a recruit, `gold_hold.rs::record_fund_deliveries` records its city, kind and objective; `military.rs::deliver_fund_units` binds the new unit on the next view and routes it to the funded campaign front or threatened city before routine military passes. Funded upgrades also return to their front. The route uses only the own fogged `PlayerView` and saved AI memory, with integer decisions and deterministic tie breaks. No core rule or `RULES_VERSION` changes.

**Threatened-city tactical valuation (AI only, 2026-11-08).** `tactics.rs::enemy_threat` values a visible attacker's expected damage to an own city by how much of the city's remaining HP it removes. If the attacker can capture it this turn, the stake is 400 + 80 per population, the same city value used by assault forecasts. `shot_value` and `focus.rs` price a shot by that threat, so ranged fire goes to attackers as a breach approaches (`enemy_threat` also scales damage by the city's shield: `(max HP + HP) / HP`, so the same hit counts more as the city nears a breach). `door.rs::door_strikes` orders its targets by threat first, then HP. `counterattack_target` ranks favorable melee strikes by the harm they avert (win chance next) and considers adjacent city attackers as well as bombardiers for a sortie, subject to its existing forecast, loss and reply tests. In `door.rs::rotate_exposed_garrisons`, a garrison at risk of falling or losing its city shield can swap with an adjacent line unit when the replacement is forecast to survive and has greater defense value against adjacent attackers; a two-unit friendly swap is legal in the core movement model. `production.rs::buy_ready_defenders` ranks affordable units by the class bonus against visible attackers within three hexes of the city, then by defensive role and strength per Gold. The siege stance, siege-war, campaign and breach passes (`breach.rs`, `siege.rs`, `military.rs`) exclude a unit occupying its own city while a visible enemy military unit is adjacent (`military.rs::last_garrison_under_attack`); tactical shots and favorable sorties remain available. A wounded garrison of a city already at 0 HP shoots an adjacent attacker rather than resting, since that attacker decides the city (exception to the late-garrison-fire rest rule in the 2026-10-24 audit entry; `defense_tests.rs::wounded_garrison_of_a_breached_city_shoots_the_adjacent_attacker_instead_of_resting`). No core combat or purchase rule changes.

**Designer imitation seat (2026-11-09; measurement only, no rules change).** `crates/ai/src/designer.rs::brain` is Hard (`ScriptedBrain` at `AiDifficulty::Hard`) with the gene file `crates/ai/src/designer.json` and two order-level habits applied after Hard's orders (`DesignerBrain::habits`): Walls first in a city without them from turn 25 (`WALLS_FROM_TURN`), and purchases dropped except aircraft and cities with a military unit of a civ at war within 3 hexes (`drop_purchases`, `DANGER_RADIUS`). Only sim examples use it (`scenario_gate --him designer`, `designer_predict`); no app game, difficulty or default does. On the held-out tape it predicts 26% of his decisions against Hard's 23%, and it is about as hard for Hard to beat as Hard is (`docs/sim-results/scenario-gate/README.md`). The paired scenario gate that uses it is `crates/sim/examples/scenario_gate.rs` (`tools/sim/scenario-gate.sh`).

**Mongol horde playbook (2026-11-07; AI only, gene `mongol_horde`, shared default 0 and Mongol Hard builtin 3 after measurement).** Levels 1–3 apply to Hard Mongols only (`crates/ai/src/params.rs`, `production.rs::mongol_horde`). Level 1 raises a war army to at least five units beyond its cities and fills the war quota and early counter with the best available mounted unit; a threatened city's garrison floor still asks for a melee unit. The Rush opening trains mounted troops rather than ranged support, Keshigs get a 100-point unique-unit production term, and Iron Working gains research value after three mounted units (`production.rs::item_base_value,rush_opening_item,uncapped_army,unique_unit_bonus`; `research.rs::tech_base_value`). Level 2 spends available Gold and spare Iron to upgrade Horsemen to Keshigs while keeping promotions; this priority bypasses the ordinary Gold runway and Forge opportunity-cost comparison but still obeys the core supply budget. A wounded Keshig beside an enemy retreats to a safer tile before the assault planner takes it (`modernization.rs::plan_modernization`, `military.rs::hold_or_upgrade,mongol_keshig_upgrade,plan_military`, `supply_value::unit_outbids_industry`). Level 3 treats Keshigs as shooters in a siege of a walled city: they stage at range two and shoot every turn while the city has HP, then charge to capture at zero HP; an unwalled city can be charged directly (`city_assault.rs::coordinate_city_assaults_on,city_assault_succeeds`, `siege.rs::siege_city`, `military.rs::fire_units`). Each unit still has one order per turn and the core's combat and faction rules are unchanged.

The level-3 builtin won 18.38% and 19.68% of 4,096 paired Mongol seats on seeds 9311003 and 8241001, versus 16.87% and 17.48% with the gene off. On the eight-map human-seat gate it raised seat wins from 472 to 502 out of 2,048; enabling it only for rivals changed them to 469. The faction best/worst ratio worsened on both AI-only seeds (2.289 to 2.429; 2.024 to 2.279), and `mongols_keshig` re-ablation at level 3 was inconclusive (18.03% to 17.95%, rule off). See `docs/sim-results/ai-quality/mongol-horde-1107/README.md`. The unit's price or Iron requirement, rather than a bow buff, is the next balance variable if the spread remains high.

- **Sparta's expedition plan (2026-11-03; AI only).** The `sparta_valued_expedition` gene in
  `crates/ai/src/params.rs` suppresses the synthetic value-zero Campaign and its unconditional
  plan override. A Campaign can compel the expedition only when its priced value is positive
  and at least as good as the best competing plan (`plan.rs::plan_candidates,choose_plan`).
  Diplomacy follows that valued Campaign (`diplomacy.rs::sparta_expedition_target`). At zero,
  the legacy forced expedition remains available for a paired comparison.

- **Coalition push (2026-11-01; AI only).** `coalition_push` defaults to 1. In `plan.rs::contain_candidates`, a containment coalition counts field strength only when visible armies approach a known target city; the leader-race value includes an equal-survivor floor when the planner's current solo chance is zero and scales by its own contribution. A held-VP contest likewise scales by the planner's approaching field force. `plan.rs::coalition_fronts` counts distinct threatened cities rather than several allied flags beside one city. `campaign.rs::coalition_city_push` adds a reachable leader-capital or held-VP city prize to campaign, assault and plan scores. Setting `coalition_push=0` restores the prior strength, race, hold, front and city scoring; the older `coalition_front` gene remains independent.

- **India's regional court plan (2026-10-31, AI only).** Hard prices each provincial Revenue Office by the Gold it will remit after completion: half the city's production-focus preview plus the Office's +3 Gold, rounded down (`india_remittance_value` 400 per remitted Gold per turn). A safe provincial city can start this Office before the opening queue; exposed cities keep the defense priority (`india_early_office` 1). From turn 60, `india_office_goal_value` 800 values missing Offices while the Imperial Diwan goal is pending, and normal production scores the Diwan once every owned city has an Office. The extra last-Office bonus and forced Diwan order are available as genes but default off after paired tests found that forcing the chain increased eliminations (`india_last_office_value` 0, `india_diwan_commit` 0). Production-focus funding and runway forecasts count only Gold actually remitted, including zero before an Office (`production.rs::remitted_city_gold`, `funding.rs::fund_production_runway`). A threatened city may train a needed troop despite India's ten-turn runway veto (`india_emergency_defense` 1); Hard also counts an assessed rush-capable neighbour as a funding front before War, so an anticipatory guard can start (`india_prewar_guard` 1). India's Hard personality uses expansion 70, military 60, science 40, economy 70 and aggression 40; it avoids optional city campaigns and second fronts while funding its regional courts (`campaign_contain_only` 1, `second_front_temperament` -60). Containment wars require a funded siege route (`leader_commit` 2); threatened cities use stronger local defense weights (`defend_margin_percent` 250, `door_defense` 150). This changes AI decisions only; the Diwan remains a held, transferable 2 VP goal.
**Recent-city retake commitment (2026-10-31; gene `retake_hold`, default 0 after the seat-only gate was inconclusive (`docs/sim-results/ai-quality/retake-hold-1031/README.md`); AI only).** `crates/ai/src/params.rs` defines 0 as off and N as the first N turns of a campaign. `crates/ai/src/campaign.rs::reconcile_campaign` records each city last owned by the AI in saved `CampaignMemory::last_owned`, using its own `PlayerView` only. Against a city it owned within the preceding 20 turns, `InsufficientArmy` does not end the campaign during that window while committed units within four hexes have at least half the combined strength of the city, its visible garrison and a half-city wall allowance. `crates/ai/src/plan.rs::missing_plan_patience` also keeps the routed objective through a missing candidate forecast while that hold applies. A collapse below the floor, a negative campaign value, a new wall before arrival, a funding deadline, peace or a missing target still ends it. The committed force still follows the existing `campaign_persist` reinforcement rule and existing military routing; this gene changes no combat or capture rule. The paired-game diagnostic in `crates/sim/examples/hard_vs_human.rs` reports campaign start/end pairs within three turns and the number of cities recaptured within 20 turns of being lost, per AI and per game.

- **Military exploration and ruins (2026-10-31; AI only, gene `military_scout`, default 0; `crates/ai/src/military.rs::military_scout`).** After combat, emergency defence, garrisons, ruin collection and campaign passes claim units, a healthy uncommitted land military unit may move toward an explored, passable frontier tile that reveals unknown land or holds an unclaimed known ruin. The option compares three points per unknown tile and `ruin_bonus` for a ruin, scaled by `military_scout` percent, against route cost, distance from the nearest own city, lost fortification and missing HP. Routes exclude exposed tiles and recent remembered enemy reach and use `campaign_approach` for a safe waypoint. The gene at 0 leaves the prior military orders unchanged. No core exploration or ruin rule changes; any unit already collects a ruin by entering its tile (§2).
- **Captured-city production alarm (2026-10-31, decision audit round 6; `capture_alarm`, default 0, opt-in; AI only).** In `production.rs::plan_emergency_production`, plain Hard with the gene at 1 does not treat a neighboring city's resistance alone as a reason to interrupt economy with a unit. A neighboring city at half HP or less, within eight hexes, still raises that alarm when a visible non-barbarian enemy military unit is within six hexes of it. Direct local threat, ranged relief, the lone-city front rule, and all other production passes are unchanged. Zero restores the prior resistance-or-enemy condition. No rules, visibility, yields or orders change. Results: `docs/sim-results/ai-war/audit-round6.md`.
- **Decision audit across rule revisions (2026-10-31, offline diagnostics).** `audit_states` exports every recorded position using a historical engine and refuses any tape hash mismatch. `decision_audit --states FILE.jsonl` verifies those positions against every tape hash before using them for shadow observations and branch starts; the branches and one-turn comparisons still resolve under the current engine. This avoids replay drift after a combat rebalance. `--prod-detail` now includes every own city, its incoming production, the planning passes that set production before routine scoring, and local defense evidence. These traces are diagnostic only; the untraced AI records nothing.
- **Battle tactics: focus fire and stragglers (2026-10-30, genes `focus_fire` 100 and `gather_hold` 50, on by default since 2026-10-30 (2,048-game gate neutral, `docs/learnings.md`); AI only, no
  rule change; AI war plan step 2; `crates/ai/src/focus.rs`, measured in
  `docs/sim-results/ai-war/w24-focus/`).** Hard only (`hard_defense`), this turn's view, no memory.
  `focus_fire` runs in `plan_military` after `recover_wounded`, before `ranged_fire`: for each
  visible enemy field unit (not on any city tile; cities stay with the assault coordinator), the
  free shooters in range with a clear line and the chargers at or above `recover_hp` that reach it
  this turn (not a garrison standing in an own city) are ordered shots first (expected damage,
  then id), then charges (forecast with the shots queued, `melee_forecast`); every prefix of up to
  six attackers is priced: kill chance is the exact distribution of the summed per-roll damage
  (`kill_chance`, integer counts over 41^k rolls) against the target's HP less shots already
  ordered; prize = kill chance x (target cost + `enemy_threat` HP x our army's mean cost / 100) x
  `focus_fire`% + the surviving share of damage at half value; price = each charger's forecast
  damage (counterattack split among the plan's chargers, `queued_split_forecast`, plus city fire)
  at its cost, loss chance x cost / 2, and after a kill the extra incoming fire on the target's
  tile over its own; and every attacker's value as a lone hit. The best positive plan over all
  targets is ordered, its units leave the pool, repeat. `gather_hold` runs after `rest`, before
  `fire_from_holds`: a military move (not an attack, retreat, assault group or coordinated
  assault, nor one whose destination is an own city or beside one) whose end tile this turn
  (fogged route, `can_step`) takes an expected share of visible fire (`incoming_at`, divided by
  1 + our units ending beside it) of at least `gather_hold`% of the unit's HP, and more than
  staying would, stops at the last route tile below that share or holds (`SkipUnit`).
- **Defend where the enemy army is (2026-10-27, gene `door_defense`, default 100 since 2026-10-28 (8.9.15), parts mask `door_parts` 29; AI only, no
  rule change; idea #23, AI war plan W2; `crates/ai/src/door.rs`).** Hard only
  (`threat_aware`), from this turn's view, no memory. A *front* is an own city that a visible
  enemy field unit (combat class, not barbarian, not standing in its own city) can strike
  within `DOOR_TURNS` 3 turns of march (`strike_turns`: distance less its range or 1, over its
  moves); each unit threatens its nearest reachable city plus `threat_aim_slack`. Threat is
  the units' Strongest Army value (`army::combat_value`, strength squared, HP-weighted) times
  `door_defense` percent; defence is the same value of own combat units within 2 plus the
  city's own (`CityView.strength` squared, HP-weighted). Only the gap is acted on:
  `plan_door_defense` (in `plan_cities` after the hard-defence rescue purchases, before
  `plan_modernization`) greedily buys, per Gold, the unit option with the most
  `modernization_power` at the city (one purchase per city per turn) or a one-step
  `UpgradeUnit` of a unit within 2 that has no enemy in its strike reach, until each gap is
  closed; it keeps solvency (`reserve_deficit_turns` of deficit, plus ten turns of the next
  unit's upkeep for purchases), a held Swiss hoard and `live_goal_floor`, but not goal or
  siege savings; strategic supply is checked with `supply::within_budget`. A front still
  short builds the strongest unit it can finish within arrival + 3 turns, keeping Walls under

- **Capture horizon (gene `capture_horizon`, Hard, default 0 = off, 2026-11-10; `crates/ai/src/plan.rs::capture_turns`, `price_campaign`; Lab card Ride to Dyrrachium):** a Campaign candidate is priced by when its capture lands. With the gene at N > 0, a siege of a standing walled city whose forecast force has no Siege and no Siege on offer (gene `siege_for_walls`) counts its siege turns after the march at N percent in `capture_odds`, and the `supply` term counts only the share of the 10-turn plan horizon left after that capture turn (`(10 - turns) / 10`, at least 0). Measured seat-only at 200: Ride to Dyrrachium 2 to 48 of 50 (each part alone stays at 2), Hold Biel and Two Cities unchanged; not yet measured on the designer seat, war suite or censuses, so it stays off.
- **Short-horizon objective (gene `short_objective`, Hard, default 0 = off, 2026-11-10; `crates/ai/src/short_objective.rs`, called in `scripted.rs::planner_through_plan` after the second front and before the operation review):** each turn, from the seat's own view, it nominates (1) every enemy city of a team at war with us, or of the team the routed plan is about to declare on (fresh sighting, legal declaration), within 15 tiles of an own military unit and not given up in the last 5 turns, whose campaign forecast (`plan.rs::campaign_forecast`, the committed force first) captures it within 5 turns, and (2) every own city a visible at-war army within 5 tiles is forecast to take within 5 turns (`opportunity.rs::forecast_siege` played from the attacker's side, the team with the most units at the door, each unit sent to the nearest tile beside the city). It prices in Production, not the plan layer's win chance: a city is worth 400 + 80 per population; a capture is worth its city at its `capture_odds` (forecast turns under `capture_horizon` 200, or the gene's own value) less the forecast's expected Production lost, and less the sum of every endangered home city's fall odds times its worth. It holds the best through a pinned operation (`OperationKind::Pin`, honoured with this gene on): a Campaign with odds of at least 15% and positive value, or, if none, a Hold on the most endangered city (`plan.rs::execute_hold`), but a Hold only when the routed plan takes the army away (a Campaign, a Hold, none yet, or Contain on a team not at our door; not the quiet plan, Peace or Contain on the attacker). The held objective is kept unless another beats it by `switch_margin` (a Hold yields to any positive offence), it is met, its city is gone, or its capture odds stay under 15% two turns in a row (then that city is left alone for 5 turns). A running operation it doesn't own (a second front, a told objective) is left alone. It starts no war and never reads a scenario's win condition.
- **Strike force keeps its moves (gene `strike_upgrade_wait`, Hard, default 0 = off, 2026-11-10; `modernization.rs::in_strike`, `upgrade_waits_for_attack`):** a unit the running strike counts on doesn't upgrade: the active campaign's committed force, a running operation's roster, or a unit that reaches an assault tile of the routed Campaign's city within `march_turns` (`opportunity.rs::strike_force_within`). A unit within 4 tiles of an own city that has an at-war enemy unit within 4 tiles still upgrades (it is defending).
- **Hold plan swaps in the stronger defender (2026-11-10, a bug fix; `plan.rs::execute_hold`, `door.rs::garrison_swap`):** when the Hold plan's city already has a unit, the Hold first makes the swap `door.rs::rotate_exposed_garrisons` would make (an adjacent free melee unit forecast to hold in its place, when the one inside would let the city fall, lose its garrison or have its shield broken) and only otherwise holds or upgrades the unit inside. - **Runaway coalition (gene `runaway_coalition`, Hard, bit flags, default 55 (bits 1, 2, 4, 16, 32; bit 8 off) since 2026-11-08, bits 1 and 2 since 2026-11-07; `crates/ai/src/runaway.rs`; designer's Horde scenario and the 8.9.26 reviews):** the runaway is the strict VP leader (or a team counting down), read by its score on public signs and acted on by a seeded draw whose percent chance is that score (no cutoff since 2026-11-08: `runaway_read` is the score, `runaway` the draw, held 8 turns): 15 per VP of lead (up to 4), a quarter of its army's excess over the next team's (up to +25), 10 per city captured recently (up to 4), 20 per empire eliminated (up to 2), 60 for a hold; never our own team. Bit 1 (diplomacy): `treaty_score` adds `runaway_alliance_value` to an Alliance with a team at war with the runaway (15 plus up to 50 for the share of the runaway's public army the pair can bring, each side's army weighted by `helper_reach_percent`); `join_war_value` (calls to arms, wars an Alliance brings) adds 40 against the runaway; `wants_peace_why` refuses Peace with it (gate "runaway leader", checked after India's leader bit) unless one of our cities is about to fall (`collapsing`), even when the runaway endangers our home (the ordinary gates' escape). Bit 2 (second front): `start_second_front` runs before the operation review each turn when no operation runs (5 turns after the last one ended); if we are at War with the runaway, or allied with a team that is and may declare, it picks the runaway's known city where at least half its public army (Strongest Army values of its visible units) stands 8+ tiles away, none of its units stands within 6 of our cities, the city or a tile 3 from it is reachable from one of our cities within 12, and our land units within 12 have threat power at least 1.5 times the city's (2.5 times if walled) plus 1.5 times its visible defenders (an unseen city is credited its share of the army not seen away). A rich but army-less runaway therefore never starts one: no city passes the army-away test, and nobody near it is at War with it (Swiss hold probe, 64 runs: 0 second fronts). It commits an `Operation` strike (15 turns, roster radius 12, no purchases) that the executor honours under bit 2 even with `operations` 0 (`operation.rs::operation`). AI decisions only: no rule changes, `RULES_VERSION` unchanged.
- **Wariness, the standing bloc and all-in coalition wars (gene `runaway_coalition` bits 4, 16, 32 on by default (55 with bits 1 and 2), bit 8 off; `claude/coalition-bloc`, 2026-11-08, designer's revision of the EU4 memo `docs/design/ai-strategy/eu4-coalitions-2026-11-08.md`; AI decisions only, no rule changes):** with any of these bits on, a Hard AI works out a hidden **wariness** toward every other empire when it decides (`runaway.rs::wariness`, never shown to the player; nothing is stored, and with the bits off it is never read), from public information only: cities that empire captured recently (0, 3, 8, 30, 45, 55 for 0 to 5: one or two barely register, three to five are a roll-over), 20 per empire it eliminated (up to 2), a fifth of its visible army's excess over ours (up to 30), 6 per VP of lead over us (up to 24), scaled from 25% to 100% by how near it is (`helper_reach_percent`: the next target is the one in its path). There is no threshold; every yes/no choice is a seeded draw (`wary_draw`, stream per purpose, partner and 8-turn bucket) with the wariness as its percent. **Bit 4:** `treaty_score` adds `runaway_bloc_value` to an Alliance, at Peace included, with a partner wary of the same empire as we are: 40% of the weaker wariness, 30% of the share of that empire's public army the pair can bring (reach-weighted), 10 for a partner next door; the offer and the acceptance both go through that draw. `bloc_size_cost` is waived, by a draw at the weakest member's wariness, for a bloc whose members are all wary of one empire outside it (no cap on its size: dogpiles are fine, designer 2026-11-08). Our Alliance rule makes the bloc a coalition: an attack on one member brings the rest in at once. **Bit 16 (all in):** at War with an empire that a team outside its side is also at war with, and by a draw at wariness squared over 100 percent (a mild worry almost never; the first version, every war all-in, cost the Hard seat 3.6 pp), `start_second_front` commits a strike on its weakest reachable city with no army-away test and a roster radius of 20 (`ALL_IN_RADIUS`), and the roster keeps one defender in each city of ours with an enemy unit within 8 tiles (`operation.rs`: a member must not empty a city the enemy takes next turn). **Bit 8 (strike a hollow runaway):** a runaway counting down, or read as hollow (`hollow_bonus_from`), is struck on any weakly held reachable city with no army-away test and no war under way; the operation declares when assembled (radius 20). **Bit 32 (wary guard):** `desired_army` adds one troop per city of ours within 10 tiles of the empire we fear most, by the same draw. Bits 8 and 16 are honoured by the operation executor like bit 2 (`operation.rs::operation`). The runaway score (above) still names the runaway for bits 1, 2 and 8. No hard line remains in this path: the runaway, the bloc waiver and the all-in strike are draws on a score (all-in at wariness squared over 100 percent), and one or two captures give at most 8 wariness.
