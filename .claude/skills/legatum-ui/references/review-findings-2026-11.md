# UI: findings from UI reviews, 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-22

Rules proposed by UI reviews (train-1103, 8.9.23, train-1102, Celts and six-empire lobby, ui-1105, Germany, 2026-11-07 landings, online lobby, open-source launch, Egypt prices, game upload).

Moved verbatim from SKILL.md on 2026-11-22 (original order kept). The current rules are in SKILL.md; where this file disagrees with SKILL.md, SKILL.md wins.

## From the train-1103 review (2026-11-01, world wonders, no alliance fee, faction trims)
- **Offer text is gated by treaty kind.** An Alliance card never reuses Peace lines ("Accepting ends your wars..."). The tour shoots an Alliance offer from an empire at Peace.
- **A flat glyph beside painted neighbours is a blocker.** A feature ships with painted art or with the item hidden; never with placeholders (the four new wonders shipped as black icons beside the painted Great Library).
- **A world wonder changing hands (capture, loss, raze) gets a Tier A card** naming the wonder and the signed VP that moved. A completion card carries its yield and VP lines and "only one in the world".
- **A destructive confirm lists everything it destroys** (raze: the wonder, its VP, the buildings), like a forecast lists every consequence. Its confirm button looks destructive, not like Cancel.
- **A list states its rules once:** one sentence plus a tooltip, never a repeated paragraph. Wonders use the normal build-card layout.
- **A disabled Buy shows its reason beside it** ("Only Switzerland can buy wonders" (superseded 2026-11-06: wonders have no Buy at all; the app follow-up landed 2026-11-07)); an exclusivity rule ("only one in the world") is on the card, not only in prose.
- **Re-stage committed fixture saves when a rule makes something exclusive,** so no shot shows an impossible holder (four empires holding the Great Library).
- **A replaced building keeps one name everywhere, from the viewer's faction:** goal title, era objective, ledger, sheet, card, hover and detail (Ruhr Foundry, never Factory, for Germany). A rule row names the viewer's own building: "+1 Production for each Workshop, Forge and Factory", and "Workshop, Ruhr Foundry and Factory" for Germany; the supply tooltip, missing-resource alert, Lens legend and Factory card do the same (`familyNames`, `forgesName` and `resourceUsers` in `data.ts` and `resources.ts`). Never "Factory/Ruhr". One function rewrites core's effect lines for every surface (`factionEffectLabel` in `data.ts`). A test over the player-text corpus fails if Germany's text says "Forge" outside a comparison.
- **A faction countdown shows once, as a turns-left bar,** not as a sentence repeated in summary and body (Sparta desertion).
- **Tour specs assert numbers exactly** (`10/10` contains `0/10`) and use the fixture's own turn. Any failing tour spec means missing shots: the reviewer lists them against the previous archive.

- **A city's per-turn yields are always visible in its panel (user, 2026-11-01, 8.9.22):** "i just built this city and i would like to know how much food / production it has and i don't see it anywhere on this screen." Put Food, Production, Gold and Science per turn directly below the population and strength line, with source breakdowns. Never put these core numbers behind a disclosure, and label the next tile's yield so it cannot read as the city's total. Comparing cities is a recurring need: link the Cities overview from the city panel (Codex `city-yields-visible`).

- **No line drawings of objects on the map (user, 2026-11-01, 8.9.22):** "what the heck is this? i thought we fixed all the simple drawings?" about the wonder-construction crane, a canvas ink A-frame added after the art pass and floating over the next hex. Canvas-drawn objects (cranes, workers, tools, buildings) are flat glyphs beside painted art: a blocker. Effects added after an art pass need the same art bar; borders, rings, arrows and text are UI and fine. **Removed 2026-11-01 (`claude/no-ink-construction`):** the crane, ink workers, scaffold ticks, string line, plot stakes, stock piles, Walls' dashed ring and post, the founding stakes and the pen nib. A construction site is only the painted stage sprites and the finished landmark. **Test for any new map drawing:** if it depicts a building, person, machine or tool and is made of canvas strokes, it needs painted art or it does not ship; a fallback that draws an object when art is missing counts too (the unreachable ink rampart in `terrain.ts` went the same day). **Finished 2026-11-01 (`claude/painted-placeholders`):** barbarian units are painted raiders (class roundels zoomed out), the city air-wing badge wears the unit icon, and Walls under construction show their painted stages. **Animations count too (user, 2026-11-02, a promo trailer: "i noticed you still use the old art for bombers"):** the air-strike pass drew the aircraft in canvas strokes because an audit of the standing map never saw it, and it shows only during a turn's playback. Audit `combatFxDraw.ts`, `moments` and every other animation layer by the same test. Fixed in `claude/painted-aircraft`: the Fighter, Bomber, Stealth Fighter, Stealth Bomber and the Drone shot fly painted plan-view sprites (`assets/units/air/`); smoke, dust, flames, rubble and impact bursts are effects and stay strokes.
- **A build card's summary line gives the number, not a sentence about it (user, 2026-11-01):** "why doesnt it show the +n visual on the bottom? it just says that it 'adds' but doesnt show what it adds?" (Quarry card). Improvement cards now show their gain (e.g. "+1 Production" on a Stone Quarry; "+2 Production and +1 Iron supply" on an Iron Mine, since only strategic resources give supply); apply the same to any card whose summary says "adds" or "improves" without an amount.
- **Every standing order has its own map badge and a hotkey (user, 2026-11-02):** "the icon for 'auto fire from city' shoudl not be 'sleep' -- and also, there shoudl be a hotkey." Auto-fire showed the Sleep moon, so an archer firing every turn looked asleep. Auto-fire is now a crosshair (`mark-autofire`) on key V (volley; A is WASD panning, `gameplayKeys.ts::PAN_KEYS`). City automation: I toggles with the last focus chosen, Shift+I steps Balanced → Military → Science → Gold (user asked for a better automate hotkey; chords like A-then-M were rejected as hard to discover). A new standing order ships with a distinct badge, a hotkey on its button, and a Guide line.
- **"More" always adds something (user, 2026-11-01, Germany's Industry projects):** "when i press 'more' it just shows me the same text as the thing above it." An expander that repeats its summary is a broken promise. The expanded text gives what the line can't: the rule's cost or catch, what it upgrades to and when, the faction it belongs to. `buildCardSummary.test.ts` fails if any build card's More equals its summary or opens with it (train-1102 review: Granary, Market and others opened with "+2 Food."); `data.ts::moreText` drops a first sentence that restates the card line, and a card's own text and its description must use the same research (Germany's More showed two stat lines once Industrialization was known).
- **Every active item can be stopped in one click (user, 2026-11-01):** "there's no way for me to just 'cancel' this production order. I have to queue something else, then move the other thing up, then press 'x'." The Now producing line has its own stop button (progress stays banked, the next queued item starts). Any running thing with a remove control in a list needs the same control where it runs.

## From the 8.9.23 review (2026-11-01, train-1101d fixes)
- **A ledger row follows one contract.** One-line label (the rule lives in the expansion and the tooltip, never a second label line), chevron, token cells (one token per thing earned, in the owner's ink, with its count in the title), and a section heading that stays true for every row under it. A row that does not fit the heading widens the heading ("Faction goals +2 each · capitals taken +1 each") or gets its own section. A closed ledger fits at 1000x600 with every row whole; the `score-ledger` tour asserts it, and a missing ledger shot is a blocker, not a flake.
- **A yield that can go negative shows its net, signed.** The city panel's Food is what is left after the population eats: "+2", or "−3" in red for a starving city, never the gross. The hover lists the top 3-4 sources, then grown, eaten and left each turn; the Cities overview reads the same number first and the gross second ("61 grown").
- **A standing order looks pressed.** Fortify, Heal and Skip show a filled accent with a check, and a selector that restyles a group of buttons must also carry the active state. Test it on computed style in a browser, not only `aria-pressed`: a pair rule had hidden `.act.active`.
- **A point a click can score leads the forecast.** The original-capital +1 VP is a gilt line with the VP seal right under the header, at least 12 px, worded "+1 VP, kept for good: your first capture of Berea". The grey footnote is for facts that do not change the decision.
- **A rare scoring moment is Tier A with the loser's face.** The original-capital card shows the defeated faction's paired `people-<faction>` portrait through `PortraitFrame` (greyed, never the old `leader-*` art), a VP seal, "Taken from <empire>" and the once-only rule. A rival's version of the same card is toned as bad news. Never the word "leader" in the card copy.
- **A ledger row on a tech card never ends in an ellipsis.** The deciding number wraps to a second line ("Land units cost 10% less Production"); a title whose longest word cannot fit steps down a size and breaks only between words ("Industrializatio / n" is a blocker). Each rule row on a card has its own painting: rows that name a Workshop, a Library, Roadworks or a Mine show that building, not the tech's shared image.
- **A tech card never states a unit's stats in a separate rule row (user, 2026-11-02, Stealth card: "im not sure this 'rule' is necessary here on this tech card?").** A rule that only describes a unit the same card unlocks (its reach, guard, intercept or supply) is a clause on that unit's subline ("139 ranged · strikes 7 tiles, guards 5 · uses Silicon", wrapping, never an ellipsis); the upgrade path is "From Fighter" on the same row, like "From Tank", never a "can upgrade" rule (the 2026-10-25 rule; the user caught a merged "Fighters and Bombers can upgrade to their stealth versions" row on 2026-11-02: "i thought we got rid of the 'upgrade' thing in the cards across the board?"). The clauses are parsed from core's effect lines (`techCardUnlocks.ts`), never copied into TS; `techCardUnlocks.test.ts` fails when a rule row repeats a unit the card unlocks.
- **Equal choices are equal cards.** The post-tree Science choice is three Tier B cards, each with its yield icon, a "+3 [icon] per turn" headline and the per-city split in sentence case; the chosen card has a selected state with a check, not the primary button style. No red note in the header, no stray leading "·".
- **A state change is told on every surface that names the unit.** "Healed to full, needs orders" is on the unit card's status, the Next decision sub-label, the queue and the report, not only the report.
- **Information is neutral ink; red is for risk.** "Moves through this tile right behind your Spearman." is a plain note; "Your Hoplite is using the direct path this turn, so this unit takes another route." is a note too. Only a path that can fail is red.
- **Small controls are at least 24 px, and use `Tip`, not `title`.** The stop ×, Compare cities and every ledger chevron. A stop or skip that hands over to the next queued item skips to the first item that can start, or says why none can.
- **A worker with a price shows its price, not its supply.** The Stealth upgrade button reads "Upgrade to Stealth Fighter · 120 Gold"; the Silicon it uses is a note beside it, and a disabled button says which need is short.
- **A goal measure caps at its target** ("5 of 5 Ruhr Foundries"), as every other goal does.
- **Tour mouse parks go to the left margin** (`mouse.move(5, height / 2)`), never the map's bottom-right corner, where the minimap and tooltips sit. Fixtures carry rule flags (`MCity.originalCapital`) so capital screens are staged by the rules, not by patching the view.
- **Copy to keep out** (enforced in `app/src/test/copyRules.ts`): "per Granary family", "ring-2", "+1 VP (permanent)", "Factory/Ruhr", "one movement round behind", a hyphen for a minus before a number ("costs -50%", "-2 Food"; any whitespace-hyphen-digit in rendered text), "Gold is too short for", "stays banked in <city>", "is ready: <verb>", "guard radius", "gross Gold", "resting defense".
- **A faction goal's action is never hidden (user, 2026-11-01, Aztecs):** "i have no idea how to engage my +2vp faction specific goal. where in the UI is that button?" The New Fire's button sat in a collapsed sidebar disclosure and disappeared entirely while blocked. Every action goal shows its button without a disclosure; a blocked action shows a disabled button with the exact reason and progress. The first available turn offers it once more as the dock's primary decision (see "A decision is offered once" below); the sidebar button stays. The faction sheet and Guide point to the sidebar and G (Codex `goal-action-visible`).
- **No card row ends in an ellipsis, on tech, unlock, build or ledger cards.** Every row wraps whole (`overflow-wrap: break-word`, `text-wrap: pretty`); a rule that is a sentence (an unlock card's Effect row) takes a reading size, not the title size. "Fighters can upgrade to Stealth Fighters: range 7, guard …" on the Stealth Technology unlock card was the last one (`.unlock-name`, `.unlock-cost` no longer nowrap).
- **One wording per rule on every surface.** Stealth: "Strikes up to 7 tiles away; guards 5 tiles around its city" (unit summary, blurb, unlock row and Guide); the narrow tech-card ledger row uses the shortest form that keeps the tiles ("Stealth Fighter: strikes 7 tiles; guards 5 tiles", two lines at 1000x600, which `benefit-ledger.spec.ts` asserts). Never "guard radius", "range 7" or a bare "guards 5". Core's effect lines are the source (`rules.generated.json` is regenerated from them: `UPDATE_UI_RULES=1 cargo test -p legatum-ai --test ui_rules`).
- **A standing and a pending order of the same kind use one sentence** (`sleepOrderText` in `orders.ts`: "Healing until full health, then asks for orders", "Fortified until woken", "Waiting until woken"), on the unit card, the order list and the tile card. A standing order is neutral ink (`.up-status.standing`); a pending one keeps its green; red is only for needs-orders ("Awaiting orders", "Healed to full, needs orders") or a broken order.
- **A decision is offered once, and only one control shows a Return hint.** A ready faction goal is the dock's primary ("Kindle the New Fire" over "+2 VP, kept for good", or "Counts toward +2 VP" for a goal that takes several steps), drawn filled even with a unit selected (`unitCardLeads` lets a goal lead); the sidebar's goal button shows only G. No This Turn card repeats it, and the title is never repeated in its own line ("is ready: kindle it for"). The dock never recommends a move that is bad now: a timing action (Raise the Warbands, Share the Learning) is offered only in the window where a good player would take it, not just because it became possible (user, 2026-11-21, Celts turn 1: "for some reason the recommended action is raise the war bands? why is that?"). Dismissing is the dialog's Cancel or Ready anyway.
- **New sidebar blocks keep the race glance whole at 1000x600, with the `sidebar` tour as the gate.** The race headline, objective, goal button, primary and minimap are whole and unobscured together (`expectSidebarGlance`), in the slim 196 px rail too. At short heights the goal row is one line (icon and the action button); its rule, reason, progress and key move into the button's `Tip`, and only a blocked reason stays, as a 12 px note. Run the `sidebar` tour before merging any change to the right sidebar, not only the spec for the new block.
- **Every state a status can show gets a tour shot.** The unit card's status was shot for pending Heal and "healed to full" but not for a standing Heal (an order from an earlier turn), which read in the wrong colour and the wrong words. Stage each state from a fixture that can produce it (a standing order carries `standing_order`; a pending one is queued this turn).
- **Sakoku's blocked line:** "Not closed yet · 0 of 30 turns", not "Open: 0 of 30 turns closed".

## From the train-1102 review (2026-11-02, painted aircraft, air labels, walled garrisons)
- **A floating map label avoids the city tile and the unit tokens, not only banners.** Air-range labels were placed clear of banners but landed across the base city's painting when every ring was off screen at close zoom; `airLabelLayout` now treats the base city's tile as occupied (`airOverlay.test.ts`).

## From the 2026-11-03 pre-merge UI review (Celts and six-empire lobby)
- **A faction rule that changes who threatens whom rewrites every sentence its viewer reads, including empty states.** If barbarians are visible to the Celts, the empty Threat lens must say they cannot attack the Celts; it cannot say no enemy military is known. Danger marks for those barbarians use neutral ink, while the Celts may still choose to attack them.
- **Own city banners stay readable under parked foreign stacks.** Draw the viewer's city name, growth meter and Build prompt above neighbouring foreign unit tokens even without a siege alarm. Threat lens ×N chips also keep off banner names.
- **Changing a default changes every sentence that states it.** The Standard lobby starts six empires; tour names, screenshot names, copy and size-switch behavior must agree. An untouched lobby follows each size's default, while a customised lobby keeps a count already in range.

- **Banner order (2026-11-04 UI review):** a banner rises above tokens only to beat foreign tokens (an alarm, or units not yours beside your city). Your own units, their order badges and HP bars always draw above your own banner. Check any layer change against the most common case: your units beside your own city on turns 1–3.
- **An alarm's detail card is never inside a collapsed disclosure** (2026-11-04): a count in a `<summary>` ("1 city alert") is not the card. The siege card sits outside "Turn details", visible at scroll 0 with the race panel showing.
- **A faction rule that makes barbarians harmless rewrites every mark and sentence beside them** (2026-11-04): icons, swatches, strength keys, lens empty states and tile cards, in the viewer's second person ("Barbarians never attack you"), never "neutral".

## From the ui-1105 review (2026-11-05, aircraft card on the unit card, Swiss sheet)
- **A hint never names a button that isn't on the card or is disabled.** No "Hold it" when the button says "Wait for target", no "or rebase it" under a disabled Rebase (copy lint bans "Hold it or rebase it").
- **"Won't resolve" follows the refused order's own description** ("Rebasing to Emain Macha · won't resolve"), never a status computed without the order ("Ready… · won't resolve").
- **Two buttons on one card never do the same thing.** If F and Space have the same effect in a state, show one.
- **Same card, same stats:** every military unit card shows Strength, aircraft included.
- **A needs-orders status says so in words** ("Awaiting orders · on guard over 4 tiles"), so red never sits on a line that reads as settled.
- **Every unit-kind branch handles the same target states** (hostile, at Peace, barbarians that spare you, none): the Fighter skipped the at-Peace branch the Bomber had, and its card said "Nothing to strike" over a Strike primary.
- **While a picker is open, it is the decision:** no other button on the card stays primary.
- Open, for a later pass: upright text for anything that drives a decision, italic only for flavour (today `.hint` is italic on every unit card).
- **Original capitals are marked for the whole game, even after capture (user, 2026-11-05; the point became held, not kept, in rules 50, 2026-11-22: the hover now reads "a rival scores 1 VP while it holds it", the star is outlined while you hold it, and a capital's VP moves with the city like a wonder's; user quote: "since capitals are worth a victory point, shouldn't we have a clear visual indicator for which cities are capitals for the whole game? even when they're captured").** The banner and seal star marked `is_capital`, which clears on capture and moves to a replacement capital that scores nothing. The star marks `original_capital` instead, in the founder's ink, kept after capture; its hover says whose capital it was and that a rival scores 1 VP while it holds it (the banner tooltip: "Original capital of X · a rival scores 1 VP while it holds it"), and a star is outlined while the viewer holds the point (`capitalMark.ts`). A mark that stands for a way to score follows the scoring rule, not a related flag.
- **A mark that stands for VP reads at a glance at the default zoom on 1000x600, without hovering (capital review, 2026-11-05).** It changes the banner's silhouette (an end-cap seal of at least 16 px), never a glyph inside the banner's text run, where small glyphs read as punctuation; judge it in a tour shot with no tile card open. The house four-point star needs about 12 px and a waist of 0.3 or more wherever it carries meaning, or it reads as "+".
- **When a glyph gains a meaning, retire its decorative uses in the same context** (the city panel kicker's star ornament, once the star means "original capital").
- **A mark for a per-empire rule follows the viewer, not the team** (capital VP was per captor until rules 50; it is now held by one team at a time, and the mark follows the viewer's own hold).
- **City-panel head tooltips go beside the panel** (`placement="right"`), as unit-panel ones do.
- **A line telling the player what to do must be possible from the current state** (capital review, 2026-11-05): never "capture it" for a city a teammate holds; say what must happen first (rules 50: "Hold it to score 1 VP", "Your teammate scores 1 VP while holding it").
- **A status badge on a far-zoom city seal never covers the seal's glyph or population:** at most 0.75 of the seal radius, outside the disc.

## From the Germany rework review (2026-11-06)
- **When a faction piece moves to a new slot, grep every prose table for the replaced building's name,** not only the old slot's surfaces: `RESOURCE_USERS`, `resourceUsersThisEra`, `FAMILY_NAMES`, `FACTION_RESOURCE_RULE`, `BUILDING_STATS.*.summary`, `EFFECT_WHY`. Each goes through `itemName(…, faction)` (Germany's Factory card said "upgrades your Forge" after the Foundry moved to the Forge slot).
- **A comparison with the replaced item gives the replaced item's own value** ("+6 Production (a Forge gives +3)"), never a bare delta in the same form: two "+3"s meaning different things on two surfaces is a fail. Upgrading past a faction piece says whether the city keeps it and its goal count.
- **A sentence that says "you" or "your" uses the viewer's faction for its numbers,** never the tile's or unit's owner (a rival hovering a German deposit read Germany's doubled supply as "inside your borders").
- **A faction multiplier shows base and bonus as separate breakdown rows** ("Iron · 2", "Germany: deposits supply double · +2"), pillar 5.
- **When a faction gains or loses a piece, re-read its motto, gallery subtitle and lore, not only its rules rows (UI review, 2026-11-06):** the Scriptorium cut left Byzantium's motto "Preserve the record." Grep for the theme as well as the name.
- **A treaty names everyone it binds (user, 2026-11-06, Mongol game T86):** he accepted Byzantium's Peace to avoid a two-front war and it also ended his war with Sparta ("which is not what I wanted to do. [what?]"); a Peace used to cover both whole sides (`diplomacy::peace_parties`). The interim UI listed every empire on both sides and explained the ally ("Sparta is Byzantium's ally"). **Decided (user, 2026-11-06): Peace is two-party only** ("otherwise its too confusing"); a Peace with one member of an alliance leaves the ally's war running, and the UI says which wars continue.

## From the 2026-11-07 landing reviews (audit fixes, copy, art)
- **Show what's reserved.** A displayed amount that already subtracts queued orders says so where it appears: the tooltip gives the full amount, the queued amount and what's left. Never "you have N" for a net figure; say "N left after your queued orders".
- **A "can't" message names the real constraint** (focus, resource, tech) and never contradicts an option visible on the same screen ("nothing it can build" beside a Build button was a blocker).
- **Advice names a lever the player has.** Never cite an internal state, such as a focus the build sets, as if it were a control.
- **One warning per list.** A warning that applies to every row goes in the list header, not on each row.
- **A longer top-bar label re-checks the 1440x900 research cell** with its turn count; a truncated "T7" is a regression. Keep chip labels short ("Units 0/3") and the full wording in the aria label and tip.
- **A faction's signature payoff card uses that faction's own painting**, never the generic moment picture.
- **Map-art tour shots** frame the piece at close zoom with no banner, tooltip or region label over it, and move the mouse away first. A landmark's name label stays clear of unit counters.
- Player text bans "work radius" (say "within 3 tiles"), "that part", "army points" and "build focus".
- **A reward granted through a shared prompt names its real source.** Greece Captive reused the free-tech prompt and told the captor "Athens shared its learning"; a new source of a grant updates every prompt and report line that names the old one.
- **Ledger and score fixtures come from engine output, never a hand-filled view**: turns and totals must be possible for the game shown (the objectives tour showed turn 112 in a turn-2 game).
- **A permanently impossible action has no control.** When a rule removes an action for everyone (buying world wonders), delete the button or cell and keep one sentence of rule text; hatched disabled controls are for temporary blocks that have a fix.
- **A rule cut gets a stale-text sweep.** Grep UI strings, the Guide, loading tips, tooltips, faction sheet "Countered by" and "How you lose" for the removed term, and rewrite any counter that relied on the cut mechanic instead of deleting it. Run `tsc -b` (the app build) as part of the check: a leftover import passes vitest and Playwright.
- **A faction sheet states a fact once.** "Strength" and "Goal" must not both restate the same rule (the Celts and barbarians).
- **Say a treaty's or order's scope once, in the headline (UI review of two-party Peace, 2026-11-07).** Don't repeat it in the title, the scope line and the button: the button carries the verb and object, the scope line only what isn't obvious (which wars continue). The result moment (the "Peace signed" interstitial) repeats what stays unchanged when the decision card said it would.
- **Every new Guide rule paragraph gets a tour shot scrolled to it**, so the copy can be reviewed as pixels. Guide rules use plain words: not "binds", "permanent teammates" or stacked qualifiers.
- **A wrapped button label at 1000x600 is a layout finding.** Buttons keep one line, or the label shortens.
- **Report lines read right from every viewer's side:** "their war" is wrong for a party to that war; check your own, a rival's and an observer's form.
- **Slow AI thinking is shown, never silent (user, 2026-11-07, Training mode): "have some kind of loading indicator UI... so I know what's going on since the turns will likely go from almost instant to potentially several seconds".** Any AI wait over about a second gets a calm progress card that names what it is doing and where ("studying 3 battles · Lugano (2 of 3) · 6 s"), with a bar. While he plans, a quiet chip. Afterwards, a report line of what it found. Never block his own planning.
- **A status chip never displaces navigation (UI review, 2026-11-07, Training chip).** A status that runs while he plans gets no top-bar cell of its own (a folded cell still cost the Technologies button and the era ring): it rides as a second line inside an existing cell (Training: "• Training · 1" under the turn number, explained in the turn tooltip). Its tour shot must show the same bar as the shot without it, at both sizes.
- **Counts in display type use lining figures** (`font-variant-numeric: lining-nums`): Cormorant's old-style "1" reads as "ı" in chips, card titles and report lines.
- **Red and pulsing are for alarms only.** A "working" indicator uses ink, never the accent red.
- **A wait card names the action and the place, with time against its cap** ("Attack on Lugano (2 of 3) · 6 of 15 s"), sits clear of the unit panel and the map's subject, and its report line names the place with Show on map even when nothing changed. Say "the AI on Training", never "Training AIs".
- **A treaty card states its cost, not the arithmetic after it (user, 2026-11-07, Alliance offer in 8.9.26): "i dont think we need the net income line here. people should just see the cost."** Show the upkeep as the red cost line, and put no "Net income +7 → +5" line beside it. The full breakdown stays in the top-bar Gold tooltip (pillar 5).
- **Never put two bare numbers side by side on a banner (user, 2026-11-07, Lugano's banner in 8.9.26: "What do the numbers mean? i think one is population (1), what is the other?").** The circled seat number next to the population square read as nonsense. Every number on the map needs its meaning visible (an icon or a label) or a hover tooltip, and owner identity comes from the faction glyph, not a seat number. Built (`land/banner-clarity`, 2026-11-07): the seat-number circle is gone from banners; the population square carries a small three-heads mark; hovering a banner shows a tooltip of its parts in order (`app/src/map/bannerTooltip.ts`: "Population 3 · Byzantium · Lugano", plus the original-capital line).
- **A map banner always carries the owner's faction glyph, never a seat number (UI review, 2026-11-07, banner clarity).** Every named banner keeps the glyph at every zoom, including the smallest named hexes and the far-zoom seal (`render.ts::cityBanners`).
