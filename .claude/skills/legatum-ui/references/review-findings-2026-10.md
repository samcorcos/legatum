# UI: findings from UI reviews, 2026-10-19 to 2026-11-01

Rules proposed by UI reviews (8.9.6, strategic supply, 8.9.7, Mercenary Companies, 8.9.14, 8.9.16, density, 8.9.19, train-1031, train-1101, ui-fix-1103).

Moved verbatim from SKILL.md on 2026-11-22 (original order kept). The current rules are in SKILL.md; where this file disagrees with SKILL.md, SKILL.md wins.

## From the 8.9.6 UI review (2026-10-19)
- **A card's headline, list and button agree** about whether there is something to do: never "No targets" over a non-empty target list. Test such cards in every state (at Peace, no sight, nothing in range).
- **A forecast that cancels a penalty names what is cancelled and what still applies.** "No Walls penalty" never sits beside a red "Walls: …" line; the city's shots are "City fire", so "Walls" only means the building.
- **A "Coming soon" or "Waiting for X" card is re-checked whenever X ships.**
- **A scrolling grid shows whole rows at 1000x600,** or a cue for how many more; the newest item must not sit below the fold on the user's laptop.
- **A new unit's rule appears once, in the same words,** on the build card, the unit panel and the tech card.
- **Hint lines over the map get a parchment background,** so they never print over region labels.
- **A city attack forecast shows the whole fight, city and garrison together (user, 2026-10-19, 8.9.6, T187 at Lugano):** "it looked like i clearly won... but now im realizing that it was only showing me taking down the city/walls not the unit under it. when in combat, it should really show the whole situation combined not just the walls and then I have to guess / infer on the stuff underneath." The forecast for a city with a garrison must show, in one card: the city HP after the hit, the overflow onto the garrison (the combined-assault rule), the garrison's HP after, whether the city is captured this turn, and your unit's HP after the counterattack. Include the other attacks you have queued on the same city this turn, since overflow combines them. Never a "Decisive" headline that only describes the walls.
  Built 2026-10-20: `forecast/assault.rs` (core, pooled by stage, queued attacks passed alongside), the `AssaultBlock` in `ForecastCard.tsx`, shown when a garrison stands under the city or attacks are pooled. The card's worst case (a walled city, a garrison and two queued attacks) must still fit 1000x600: `e2e/city-assault-forecast.spec.ts` checks it, and the card sheds its explanations first (expected-damage line, "How city fire works", the plain Walls line), never the rows or the capture sentence.
- **One primary button per card, always (8.9.6 review):** an aircraft card with nothing to strike makes Hold (Stay on guard) the primary; one with an at-Peace target makes Strike the primary. Copy-lint now bans "cancels Walls", "Walls: … fires first" and "median" (`test/copyRules.ts`); the city's shots are "City fire", the Engineer's verb is "covers", its rule is one sentence (`data.ts::ENGINEER_RULE`).

## From the strategic-supply UI review (2026-10-20)
- **Strategic resources keep their word.** Icon-only is for yield amounts; Iron and Coal appear as "1 Iron" with the icon beside the word, in cards, tile cards, keys and refusals. An icon never stands in for a sentence's subject.
- **An "N of M" label stays true when N exceeds M.** Never call demand "in use": say what works and what is idle ("2 of 2 Iron at work, 2 idle"; "Needs 4, supplies 2"; "Short by 2").
- **A state that changes a stat changes the shown stat everywhere.** An idle unit shows its effective strength with its breakdown, the same number the forecast uses, and its token carries the alert mark.
- **Every "short" or "idle" state has a locator and a fix:** a map mark, "Show on map" on the report line, and one fix clause.
- **A refusal is one shared string** on the build card, the upgrade card and the tooltip; the tooltip only adds detail.
- **Remote tours rebuild `app/dist` first** (the fixtures serve it statically, so a stale build shows old text).

## From the 8.9.7 UI review (2026-10-21)
- **A locked-by-faction unlock keeps its truth everywhere.** When a faction can't use a tech's unlock until later (Mongols and Walls), the tech card, hovercard, unlock moment and build list all say so ("Walls for the Mongols from the Medieval era"), with no price.
- **A decision card's first screen says whether accepting is enough.** A Peace offer names its two teams and the single war it ends on the first screen (the scope line says it once, "Ends your war with Byzantium.", then names the viewer's wars that continue). Only the two teams must agree. The outgoing offer shows the same scope beside its action. A war notice caused by a newly signed Alliance says who allied with whom and that the new ally joined the existing war, rather than presenting it as a new declaration (designer's 8.9.21, turns 24–25).
- **A delayed civilian capture names visible competitors.** When a capture order takes more than one movement step to reach a hostile civilian, its forecast names military units already adjacent and hostile to that civilian, including barbarians, in one line (designer's 8.9.21, turn 22).
- **A relation row states each rule once,** and the consequence line (the wars you join) sits directly under the button it belongs to.
- **Scenario text is player-facing:** never "the user's game" or a build number.
- **Generated sentences use an a/an helper** ("an Infantry unit", never "a Infantry").
- **Aircraft use the same unit UI as every other unit (user, 2026-10-22):** "it feels like it's hacked together and it doesn't feel like how other units feel being all in the left bar of the city... not sure why the actions can't just be on the bottom like other units and the picture of the unit shows up on the city when it's active (and it can hide when it's not selected) like in civ?" Selecting an aircraft (from the rotation, the city or a hotkey) shows the normal bottom unit card with its actions (Strike, Rebase, Stay on guard, Skip) and keys; the aircraft's art appears on its city tile while it's selected or acting and hides otherwise; the city panel keeps only a compact hangar line ("Hangar 2 / 2", click to select one). **Same card, not a look-alike (user, 2026-11-05, Bomber card: "this ui feels a little different than the other units... i see no reason for it to be so different"):** the aircraft card had moved to the side but kept its own art box, prose stat run, italic hint and tall uppercase buttons. It uses the unit card's head, status line, stat grid and `act` buttons (Hold | Skip side by side like Fortify | Skip); a unit kind never gets its own card styling.
- **Aircraft have hotkeys and a picker that knows hangar space (user, 2026-10-22, T217):** two Bombers were both sent to a hangar with one free slot; the second was refused but its card still read "Rebasing this turn". A picker greys out a full destination before you choose, counting this turn's queued moves, and a refused order never reads as accepted. Aircraft keys: Space skip, S strike, R rebase, F stay on guard ("I'd love to use hotkeys for the bombers, like space for skip turn").
- **Tour assertions move with rule changes:** a faction rule change updates the tour's faction assertions in the same branch.

## From the Mercenary Companies UI review (2026-10-23)
- **Show a purchase's per-turn income change and its broke consequence before commit.** A Company costs 2 Gold a turn and turns barbarian when unpaid: the chosen row says "-3 to -5 a turn" and what happens at 0 (not yet built; open item).
- **A section that adds more than about 4 rows folds to a best-3 preview** with "+N more" (the hire shop does the fold per seller; folding the whole shop is open).
- **Rows blocked only by a shared "one at a time" rule keep their dimmed price, and the selected row shows an inline Cancel.**
- **When 3 or more disabled rows share one reason, say it once in the section head.**
- **A far-zoom plaque never shows a bare number** (the contract countdown reads "7 turns" or "Last turn").
- **"Countered by" lists only actions the rival controls** (outlast a public countdown, drain the treasury), never "sell to them" or a war that does not stop the faction hiring elsewhere.
- **Glossary:** "tribute" is gone; the words are "Company" (a hired unit with a contract), "contract", "thin levies" (never "levy cap"), "hired from <empire>" (never "(Spartan)").
- **A hired unit carries its contract in the open:** seller seal plus "N turns" on the map tag and unit panel, for every viewer who sees the unit.
- **The Heir is announced and public (user, 2026-10-22):** one moment card to every empire when he is born, naming the city; his marker shows in fog on every map, current each turn, and says who holds him.
- **A public-position marker keeps its label at every zoom, with a solid halo** (the Heir in fog); a tile that holds the notable thing leads with it even when unexplored.
- **A "capture" announcement states the "at War" precondition,** and when a fact becomes public, grep the stale "in view" hints (the race panel said so).
- **A held-goal tour covers every holder state** (its owner, a rival, barbarians, unborn); the open items from the Heir review are the rival and barbarian shots, an unexplored-tile locator and one name for the Heir (decided: "Heir to the Throne").
- **Era resources review (2026-10-22, legatum-ui-reviewer):** when the top bar sheds labels to fit, each value keeps an icon, so a bare number never stands alone; an alert chip keeps its word at every size (names may drop only for resources with painted art); a missing-resource alert offers Show on map to the nearest rival-held or unclaimed deposit; a resource's sentences use its own improvement's name from data (Mine, Saltpetre Works, Oil Well, Lithium Mine), never a hard-coded "Mine"; save-migration status is not a warning: mark only what the rule affects, in grey, in plain words ("Built before the era rule: needs no Iron until it upgrades"); one source for the current era (`research.era`) in every widget. Glossary: "supply" covers all six strategic resources; the "era resource" is the current era's.
- **The top bar names the next reveal (2026-11-08, `SupplyChips.tsx::chipSupplies`):** besides every revealed strategic resource, a muted grey chip shows the current era's resource until its technology is learned, reading "Niter: revealed by Gunpowder" (Coal by Industrialization, Oil by Combustion, Silicon by Autonomous Systems), with a tooltip "Used for this era: <units and key building>" (`resources.ts::resourceUsersThisEra`). It is a research target, never an alert: grey, no red mark, no idle count. A tech card lists a "Reveals X on the map" row before the bonuses that use that resource (`techCardUnlocks.ts::rank`: first find Oil, then Oil Wells gain Gold).

- **8.9.8 release review (2026-10-23, legatum-ui-reviewer):**
  - A feature's tour domain covers its disabled reason, forecast line and hotkey states. Siege and the aircraft hotkeys shipped untested.
  - A grandfather or migration note never pushes a live reason below the card's fold; put it after the reasons, on one line.
  - When an order is renamed (Sleep → Wait), grep all player text, including the Guide and unlock strings, for the old name.
  - New strategic resources and improvements ship painted art in the Guide row and the top bar, never flat glyphs.
  - Titles wrap at word boundaries (a historical unit name broke mid-word); check the width before reaching for `overflow-wrap: break-word`.
  - A tour spec's text assertions read from `data.ts`, not a pasted copy of the sentence. Specs park the mouse before a shot, so no hover tooltip leaks in.
  - Player text avoids internal names: say "Built before units needed Iron", not "the era rule".
- **The Heir is "Heir to the Throne" (user, 2026-10-23):** one player-facing name, "the Heir" for short. Never "Purple-Born" or "Porphyrogennetos".
- **"Every Way to Score" (the victory points panel) needs a redesign (user, 2026-10-24):** "this page is confusing af... it also doesn't feel as sophisticated and elegant as the rest of the application. fix that in the actual game first." This is the third iteration, and the user OKed Fable or a stronger model to get it right.
- **The victory points panel is the score ledger (user, 2026-10-24: "so much better. yeah this is the right path").** It's one table, `docs/design/score-panel/README.md`:
  - **Columns:** empires in rank order, the viewer's column gilt wherever it falls.
  - **Rows:** every way to score, in three sections: Kept for good, Held, Eras.
  - **Cells:** three marks: a filled token in the owner's ink means held, a dashed socket means open, a faint socket means not reached.
  - **Detail:** rows expand in place for the rule and the public facts.
  - **Cut:** legends, the decadence paragraph, per-era tables and suggestions.
  - **Defaults** taken from the mockup, since the user didn't pick: rank-order columns, and short windows open with all rows closed. Prefer this ledger pattern (columns = rivals, rows = ways to win) wherever a screen compares empires.
- **8.9.9 release review (2026-10-24):**
  - Unit and city "why not" lines (Upgrade, Siege, Needs, grandfathered) wrap in both the 1000x600 side panel and the 1440x900 dock: never one clipped centred line, never an ellipsis. The tour asserts no reason element has `scrollWidth > clientWidth`.
  - A seat label ("Japan · Japan") never appears twice in one sentence. Use faction names unless two empires share a faction.
  - An offer shows its cost once: on the button or in the lines, not both.
- **Every screen asks "should this have an animation?" (user, 2026-10-24).** "All payoff / investment / building / conquering etc moments should have a rewarding animation." That covers founding a city, a building or wonder completing, a tech or era unlocking, a crown taken, a capture or sack, a victory point scored, a war declared, an Alliance signed, and units moving and fighting. A static state change at a payoff moment is a defect. Animations respect the combat and animation settings toggle and `prefers-reduced-motion`, never block input, and stay in the atlas style: ink, sepia, one accent red. They feed the trailer, so ask also whether the moment would look good on camera.
- **Animations can be turned off (user, 2026-10-24):** Settings has "Map animations: On or Off" (one control for combat playback and the other map moments; user, 2026-10-24: On/Off only, Fast removed). Off is the game without animations: instant state changes, units stand where they ended, damage numbers still show. The system's reduced-motion setting forces Off. Playback never blocks input or the next turn.
- **Payoff moments, built (moments-fx, 2026-10-24; `map/momentFx.ts`, `momentFxDraw.ts`, `idleLife.ts`, clips in `docs/design/moments-fx/`):** on the combat animation's layer (one plan per resolved turn, a pure function of time, the "Map animations" On/Off setting and reduced motion). Rules learned building it: a moment holds back the state it is animating (the terrain cache's `setHold`) rather than painting over it; moments follow the rules' phase order (moves and fights, then founding, then production, growth and borders, then the score); a medallion or seal rises above the city banner, never over it, and moments on one city take slots side by side (VP seals merge per empire per turn); gilt is a fine rule or ring for VP, crowns and Great Works only, never a burst; idle life (town smoke, water glints) redraws at 15 fps, never every frame.
- **Construction shows the real building in stages (user, 2026-10-24; round 1's SimCity scaffolds and cranes were rejected as "cheesy" and "arbitrary scaffolding"):** Great Works and Walls were built up from their own art in increments (painted construction stages in `assets/construction/` when present; since 2026-11-22 Walls draw only their stage-1 footings for the whole build, because stages 2 and 3 read as finished Walls; otherwise the finished art rising in four increments); ordinary buildings and new towns get a short ink-drawn reveal of the real art. Payoffs are restrained: "+1 VP" in the house serif over a fine gilt rule, small quiet wax seals for treaties, a fine gilt ring for a Great Work; no sunbursts, bounces, ripples or sparkles anywhere. **Round 2 rejected too (user, 2026-10-24):** the increments read as "a cheap reveal animation", the founding as "outlined, then filled in", the building as "bolted on and disjointed". Construction animation is switched off (`CONSTRUCTION_ANIMATION` in `map/momentFx.ts`) until a researched design is approved; the restrained payoffs stay.
- **Great Works are landmarks on the map (user, 2026-10-24: "have things like the Great Library more visible on the map, extending the borders of the city"):** A finished Great Work stands on a chosen owned tile beside its city. It replaces that tile's yield and improvement, and its holder scores +2 VP; capture can transfer the points. The tile hover states both the VP benefit and the lost yield. The landmark stays legible at every zoom (a gilt-ringed badge with the work's icon at the far tier). Rivals' Great Works use the public city view to show without exposing private detail. See legatum-rules for the transfer rules.
- **Map animations setting is On / Off only (user, 2026-10-24):** "I suggest that the only settings are 'on' and 'off'." On plays the animations; Off is the game as it was before animations, with instant state changes. There's no Fast speed. Reduced motion forces Off.
- **8.9.10 release review (2026-10-25):**
  - A section heading's promise ("never lost") must hold for every row in it; held goals never sit under a "kept" head.
  - Prose that names an action ("or rebase it") needs a button for it, or a disabled button with its reason.
  - Every interstitial with nothing else to do uses one primary, **Continue**.
  - A moment's tour shot dismisses any simultaneous card first, and an Off-state shot asserts no rays or rings.
- **The unit panel lives on the side, not along the bottom (user, 2026-10-25):** "actions on the left (basically the same view as building construction and actions) is probably the right call.. but the main thing is that it can't take up so much space on the bottom... the lack of visible map here makes it really hard to see the whole map and see where units should go. given that computer screens are horizontally oriented rectangles, using the left and right spaces are probably the way to go." The selected unit's panel is a narrow vertical side panel, matching the city panel's placement and style. Keep the bottom of the map clear. This applies to any persistent panel: use the sides of the screen, not the bottom.
- **Show every revealed strategic resource in the top bar (user, 2026-10-25):** "i only see coal at the top of the screen and this upgrade requires iron... it was hard to see how much iron i have... strategic resources will be increasingly important so we should see them more clearly." Only the current era's resource showed; the others were grey print at the bottom of its popover. Every revealed strategic resource gets a compact chip (medallion plus spare and total), the current era's emphasised, and a shortage turns its chip red. Clicking any chip opens that resource's own breakdown: suppliers, users and spare. Never leave a resource reachable only through another resource's popover.
- **No unexplained map marks (user, 2026-10-25):** "i don't know what this striped tile means." That was the hatching for tiles claimed beyond a city's 3-hex work radius (owned, not worked). Stacked alerts (coalition warning, your hold countdown, desertion), How You Win, and the crowns overflowed with no way to scroll. Persistent panels cap their height. They show the most important item first (the hold countdown), fold secondary alerts into one compact line that expands, and scroll visibly (wheel and scrollbar) when they still overflow. Test this at late-game states at both sizes.
- **The right sidebar is being redesigned as one system (user, 2026-10-25: "this whole side bar here needs a fresh look... super cluttered").** Race panel, This Turn and minimap together: the headline first, the rest folded, no duplication with the top bar or the ledger.
- **Max rank stays in the rank family (user, 2026-10-25):** Sparta's level-6 mark replaces the six stacked promotion lines, and its Λ is the centre device inside three chevrons and three rockers. The unit panel on the left side and the redrawn landmarks were approved the same day (unit panel: "use whichever one you think is best"; landmarks: "yes").

- **Unit action panel refinement (designer, 2026-10-25):** Upgrade and Siege form one compact **Improve** ledger: action, concrete benefit, and an aligned red cost column. A resource is used supply, never a consumed cost. Attach each information control to its row. Shared blockers appear once, left-aligned and wrapping next to the affected rows; disabled rows stay legible without hatching or heavy dashed frames. Disband belongs in a quiet, ruled footer. This supersedes the older Fortify-primary and Siege-never-primary guidance: Scouts and Settlers keep their role actions; other units emphasize an available upgrade, then available Siege, otherwise map movement/attack targeting. ## From the 8.9.14 UI review (2026-10-28)
- **Assert that the footer is in view; don't assume it.** A decision or unlock card's primary button is measured in view at 1000x600 (`getBoundingClientRect().bottom <= innerHeight`); Playwright's "visible" isn't enough. The body scrolls and the footer stays pinned (`flex: none`). The layout audit missed a fully clipped primary inside an `overflow: hidden` card (ledger unlock cards, Education and Industrialization).
- **The slim 196 px sidebar rail is its own layout** (1000x600 with the unit panel open, the commonest state there). Every row (goal count, "All empires", crowns, race rows) gets an overflow assertion in that mode. Counts never wrap onto the next row: stack them under the name, or leave them to the tooltip.
- **A dock primary's sub-label wraps, and the key badge never clips.** Keep sub-labels to about two short words ("Reply by T5", "Game report").
- **After a rename, grep every surface for the old name:** components, `SeatBar` sub-labels, alarm copy. "Signal" survived the rename to "Share the Learning".
- **A "needs" line separates requirement from stock:** "Iron supply: needs 1 spare, you have none", never "(1 to spare) ✗".
- **Counts in labels use a plural helper** ("1 order"), enforced by the copy lint.
- **Archive every release's tour,** so the next review diffs against the previous release (8.9.12 and 8.9.13 were never archived, so the 8.9.14 review diffed against 8.9.11 and flagged everything).

## From the 8.9.16 UI review (2026-10-29)
- **A badge in a card's name column gets a clip check at 1000x600 whenever a column width changes.** The Buy column grew to 92 px and left the name column 88 px: "RECOMMENDED" was cut on the first idle-city card. The badge's box never outgrows its column (`max-width: 100%`, tight tracking, wraps whole), and `e2e/tour/turn-one.spec.ts` asserts it is whole inside its name and its card at both sizes.
- **An era-dependent change gets one shot per era band.** Siege gear and built-in Siege shipped with only Ancient units on the map. `e2e/siege-units-shots.spec.ts` now frames one unit per band (Ancient, Renaissance with Siege, Industrial with none) at both sizes, with a unit-panel shot and a forecast shot of the Industrial case.
- **A locked card's reason sits in one place.** The grey "Build Granary here first" line and the red refusal notice after a click share the same slot, under the price row and above the yield line, and the gap between it and the yield line stays tight (`renderUnavailable` in `CityPanel.tsx`).
- **Never a negative number in player text.** "you have -2" for spare supply is a bug: use "you have none" for zero or less. Copy lint (`test/copyRules.ts`) bans a negative amount after "you have" and a distance with no unit ("strikes within 5").
- **An icon that sits before punctuation must not leave a visible gap.** A narrow glyph in its square box reads as a stray space before the semicolon ("+3 [icon] ; upgrades"): `Amount` takes `tight` and `Amounts` sets it when ; , . : or ) follows.
- **Wrapped effect and upkeep lines use `text-wrap: pretty`,** so a single word or number never sits alone on a line, and they still never truncate.

## From the UI-density review (2026-10-31)
- **Density switches go by height, never by count alone.** A layout that gives up art or decoration to fit 1000x600 applies only below the height that needs it (for example `@media (max-height: 700px)`). At 1440x900 an unlock card keeps its large painting.
- **Moving information out of a sticky head must not hide it at open.** Status that explains a problem (not healing, under threat, can't fire) stays in view when the panel opens, whatever the auto-scroll does. A tour shot named for a state must show that state.
- **No scroll box inside a scrolling panel.** The queue grows with the panel; a fixed cap that slices a row is a defect. The current item appears once, in Now producing.
- **Measure density in the common case too.** Report choices visible at open for a healthy city with 0–1 queued items. Don't lose pixels there to win them in rarer states.
- **A compacted celebration keeps its emblem.** Under height pressure, drop the quote and rays first, then shrink the seal, but never remove the seal and its stamp.
- **Generated articles handle vowel sounds** ("a University", "a European", "a one-…"), enforced by the copy lint.

## From the 8.9.19 review (2026-10-31, mounted rebalance)
- **Check new labels in the narrowest column:** a new forecast breakdown label is checked in the card's narrowest column. The forecast card is narrower at 1440x900 (about 320 px) than at 1000x600. A label that wraps past two lines, or that leaves its amount on a different line from its last word, gets a full-width row.
- **A unit's one-line build summary names its drawback as well as its strength**, e.g. "Fast riders: strong against ranged units, poor at taking cities". A rebalance that adds a penalty puts it on the picker card, not only in the hover.
- **Use the true minus (U+2212)** in every percent and amount, so a label and its amount match ("−33%" and "−8"). Never an ASCII "(-33%)".
- **Each number once per tooltip:** don't print "4 moves" twice in one card.
- **Tour fixtures that depend on turn resolution pin their outcome** (garrison HP, seeds) and assert the payoff line (e.g. "captured"), so AI or rule changes fail loudly instead of quietly losing the payoff shots.
