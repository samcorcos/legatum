# UI: recurring feedback as rules, part 2 (full text)

Tech unlock cards, disabled reasons, menus, Walls wording, rewards, production cards, held cards, scrolling, the screen tour, the end of the game, pickers, meters, glyphs, net and gross numbers, overlays, the Settle lens, premium tiers, portraits, upkeep, yield hovers, celebrations, eliminated empires, docked panels, lists, AI levels, lobby, clicks and lists, numbers, sticky stacks, badges, scoring visibility, VP surfaces, locked items, one-time actions, alarms, figures, moment cards, background failures, tour hygiene, map bonuses, war and peace moments, calls to arms, diplomacy view, aircraft, era objectives, owners, the end of a game.

Moved verbatim from SKILL.md on 2026-11-22 (original order kept). The current rules are in SKILL.md; where this file disagrees with SKILL.md, SKILL.md wins.

- **Finishing a tech must feel like an unlock, Civ 6-style** (user, 2026-10-05; revised 2026-10-31): every finished technology gets a card with its unlocked buildings, units and improvements. The card is the announcement. The city's production list keeps its ordinary section and item order; no "Just unlocked" group or persistent new marks remain (S14).
- **A disabled action's reason sits next to it:** an inline one-line note within one control-height of the button, not only a tooltip. In crowded docks `Tip` placement can fall back about 900 px away (7b review: Sparta Heal).
- **Title and menu messages are in the normal flow** and tested at 1000x600 against the footer. The layout audit doesn't catch text overprinting text (7b: the old-save message under the network-server footer).
- **"Walls" means only the Walls building;** the city's shield is "city HP". A card must never say "break the walls" and "no Walls" together.
- **Rewards show their win:** distinct art and the gain list expanded with icons, not hidden behind a chevron row (7b camp celebration). Done in art pass 2: `Moment.gains` lists each gain with its icon, camps and ruins wear their own emblem, and no card has a chevron row any more.
- **Production cards lead with the effect** and never truncate it. When Gold/turn after building goes negative, the card says so in red.
- **A card held until the player acts is closed only by its own controls** (primary, Later, Esc), never by a stray click or map drag. It can be reopened from its turn-report line. Its primary closes only itself, never the moments queued behind it (tech-unlock review, 2026-10-05).
- **A card that announces a finished mechanic offers the next idle decision as a button,** not a sentence (e.g. "Choose research" when nothing is queued).
- **One live Return primary per screen:** while a card owns the keyboard, dim or hide every other Return badge.
- **Auto-scrolling a panel keeps the panel's identity header in view** (a sticky head), or the revealed section must fit without scrolling.
- **Every feature screen joins the screen tour in the same change,** not only its own shot spec (8.9.4 review: Cities overview, where-to-build, overlays, the Keshig forecast and build card, the phalanx forecast, the Byzantine Library card (ordinary Library), the air wing and ready cards, supply chip tooltips and the decadence tooltip had shots only in scattered folders, `.progress/art/air-wing` outside test-results). Cities overview, overlays and the air wing ready card are in the screen tour (`e2e/tour/post-game.spec.ts`, 97a-c); the supply chip, idle Factory and blocked Forge card are in `e2e/tour/turn-one.spec.ts` (98a-c); the rest are still to add. Separately gated features break each other's layout, so the tour on the combined master is the gate.
- **Chart shots park the mouse away from legends:** a hovered legend row picks out one empire and fades every other line.
- **After the game ends the HUD stops being live:** no hold strip, coalition banner or era "Next:" line in the race, no "Awaiting orders" or action buttons on a unit, no goal measure past its target (`cappedGoalLabel`), and a coalition names the empire by faction (`empireTitle`), never a seat label ("AI 6").
- **Fixtures that stage a report set the same state the HUD reads,** so no screenshot shows two sources disagreeing. Shoot the common real case, not only the staged one.
- **Improvements are a picker group, not a screen** (city-built improvements, 2026-10-06): the city production list has an "Improvements" section after Units, one row per eligible tile, titled "Mine, Iron · 2 tiles NE" (resource, distance, 8-point bearing, never coordinates), with its forecast under the title ("+1 Production when built · Deep Mine with Machine Tools (+1 Production)"), cost, turns and Buy. Hovering or focusing a row rings its tile on the map; every eligible tile is dash-ringed while the city is selected; a click on one of those tiles picks it and lights its row. The rows carry no tooltip (a tooltip would cover the map tile the hover is pointing at). The section header says how many resources lie dormant. The tile card of your own unimproved resource says "Dormant: Farm it from a city's production list".
- **Pickers start on the map:** any "choose a tile or target" control highlights its candidates on the map, marks the chosen one and accepts a hex click. A dropdown is only the keyboard path. Why an out-of-range hex is unavailable is said on the map itself (territory review, 2026-10-05).
- **A meter's label is one line,** "Grows to N · claims X". Yields go under the bar as icons, with stored/turns at the right under the bar. It never wraps to three lines at 1000x600.
- **A disabled action shows its reason where its price would be,** never a price on a button that can't be pressed.
- **Rare states need a common-case shot:** a mechanic that appears only at an extreme gets a fixture at the common case too.
- **An idle city's first build card is visible at scroll 0 at 1000x600** (Playtest 8 review): budget the panel's top furniture (sticky head, territory bar, notes) against it, or auto-scroll an idle city to its build list.
- **One glyph, one meaning:** the red diamond means NEW. "Needs a decision" and "pending until Ready" get their own marks, with a visible word on desktop.
- **Label gross versus net numbers:** never show bare tile totals beside per-turn income; say which is which, in neutral ink unless it's actually bad.
- **Keep jargon out of visible labels** (e.g. "ring 2"); put it in the tooltip.
- **A status word comes from one shared check** (e.g. "dormant"). Its text, map treatment and count must agree on every surface (improvements review, 2026-10-06).
- **Per-tile action lists go after the main groups,** or are capped at 3 plus "+N more", so Buildings and Units are never pushed below the fold. A row's distance and bearing stay together.
- **The map-first hover ring is drawn above unit counters.** "Upgrades by tech" lines read "Later: X, +N Yield".
- **A forecast and the tile card it describes use one shared gain function,** so their numbers match once an upgrade tech is known (8.1 review).
- **The Borders overlay (a stackable toggle, no longer a lens) is a wash of at most 0.3 with a hairline.** Per-tile numerals appear only at normal zoom, and the far tier shows only the tint.
- **The Settle lens must read at a glance and never depend on a Settler** (playtest 8.4: "i press the settle button and it doesn't do anything"). Its wash covers 26% (Poor) to 95% (Exceptional), deepening with quality (Borders stays 0.3), it tints every legal site on the whole explored map by absolute band (below) and marks the top three, and with no Settler it scores from the capital and says so in the legend. A lens that is empty in a normal game state is a bug, not an empty state.
- **Polish rules from the 8.1 review (built in `claude/p82-polish`):** one shared `improvementGain` (`improvements.ts`) feeds the tile card and the picker, so "+2 Production" never disagrees with "+1"; the picker row reads "+2 Production when built (Deep Mine, known)" and drops the "Later:" clause once the upgrade tech is known. A dormant resource's tile card states each fact once and ends "Dormant: build the Mine from <city>'s production list". The production meter's label uses the short form "Quarry, Stone NE" (one line, bearing last). The per-tile white diamond that marked worked tiles while a city was selected is gone: every owned tile inside the work radius is worked, and the hatch already marks the claimed-only ones, so it said nothing new and added noise to every owned hex. A mark that is true of every tile in a set is noise; mark the exceptions.
- **Measure controls that show a level or option name with the longest name** (e.g. "Training (slower turns)") at 1000x600 and 1440x900, including closed native `<select>`s, which clip silently. A closed select shows a short form; the open list shows the full name (8.2 review).
- **Forecast clauses carry no internal state words** like "known". Explanatory lines are at least body-small. Tour fixtures park the mouse before shots so hover tooltips don't leak into baselines.
- **Premium means imagery and iconography, not only clear boxes** (user, 2026-10-06, on the faction sheet: "clear and communicate the information, but it still feels flat. not like a premium 4x game with a lot of flavor"). Big moments (faction choice, victory, reveals) use the art we have (leader portraits, seals) and an icon on every listed trait. Colour-coded boxes alone read as a form. This bar applies to the whole game: the UI reviewer flags flat screens as major, and a premium pass brings every big screen up to the reference: the faction sheet as of 6cadd79c (portrait hero in an engraved frame, icon medallions with up/down arrows on every trait, the unique piece as an icon card with stat pairs, star dividers, header icons, parchment grain).
- **Premium tiers by how big the moment is** (premium audit, 2026-10-06):
  - **Tier A,** with a portrait or a big medallion: faction choice, victory, defeat, war declared on you, a city lost, the first city, a capture.
  - **Tier B,** a medium card with icon pairs: tech unlocks, treaties, titles, Great Works.
  - **Tier C,** a small card with an emblem: growth, claims, hints.
  - A Tier B event shown as Tier C counts as a flat finding.
- **Leader portraits appear wherever a leader is the subject:** victory, defeat, alarms, diplomacy, the declare-war dialog, the foreign city card, lobby seats and the Guide. Panel heads carry a crest or icon plus the house star. Headline numbers (strength, health, population, walls) are icon medallions, not label-and-number text.
- **Reuse the faction sheet's building blocks** (portrait frame, medallion row, piece card, star divider, grain) as shared components. Re-shoot the tour baseline after each art merge.
  - **Built (premium pass 1, branch `claude/premium-1`):** `app/src/ui/premium.tsx`/`premium.css` export `PortraitFrame` (engraved frame, star studs, crest pin, `fallen` greys it; size from `--pw`/`--ph`), `PortraitRoundel` (round thumbnail with the seal pinned, optional player-colour ring), `Ribbon` (ink, red or gilt swallowtail banner), `Medallion`/`MedallionRow`, `PieceCard`, `StarDivider` and the `paper-grain` class. Compose these; don't restyle.
  - **Victory and Defeat** (`GameOver.tsx`): the hero is the winner's leader (a team shows two or three frames) under an ink ribbon with the empire's name ("Victor: The Celts" when a rival won); your own elimination shows your leader greyed under a red "Fallen" ribbon; a draw shows your own leader under a gilt "No victor" ribbon. Every empire's row has a roundel in its player colour, a VP bar and the score; the winner's row has a star, an eliminated row is dimmed and says so. It must fit eight empires at 1000x600 without scrolling (`e2e/premium-shots.spec.ts` asserts it).
  - **Alarms and Declare War** (premium pass 1): the declaring leader's `PortraitFrame` (crest pinned in their colour, seat badge bottom-left) sits on the alarm banner for one or two members (crests stay for bigger teams); the Declare War dialog puts the target's leader beside the decision over a red `Ribbon`. The one primary and its Return key are unchanged.
  - **Lobby seat rows**: the faction cell is a 48 px `PortraitRoundel` (the seat's colour rings it, the seal is pinned) plus the name and the faction's one-line identity (it wraps, never truncates). The faction column is 196 px and the rows are about 65 px, so a 1000x600 window shows about four seats and scrolls under the Start dock; `e2e/premium-shots.spec.ts` sets all eight factions and asserts nothing clips or overflows the panel.
- **Premium pass 2 (2026-10-06, `claude/premium-2`; `ui/ringMedallion.tsx`, `reportIcons.ts`):**
  - **Turn report:** every category header carries a neutral ring medallion with its icon (`CATEGORY_ICON`) and each line a ring medallion with its own icon (`ReportLine.icon`, from `eventIcon()` in `reportIcons.ts`; fallback the category's), ringed green with a rising arrow, red with a falling one, plain ink when neutral. A capture is `emblem-captured`, a founding `emblem-founded`, a tech `emblem-tech`. Star dividers between sections; the filter chips carry the category icon. The old left rule is a tinted hairline under each line. Reopening, "Show on map", "Open inbox" and "Show card" are unchanged.
  - **Diplomacy:** an incoming offer opens with the proposer's leader portrait (`PortraitFrame`, 64x80), the treaty emblem (`TreatyEmblem`: the house star in a ring, two interlocked rings for an Alliance, a red ring and cross for War) pinned on the portrait's corner, and the leader's motto as the quote under the row. Fee, upkeep, net income and the betrayal cost are red-ringed gold medallions with a falling arrow (`TermLine`: `yield-gold` in `--y-gold`), gains are green rings; notes stay plain muted lines. The same rows serve an empire row's action lines and a sent offer, which also gets the recipient's `PortraitRoundel`. An open empire row starts with its leader roundel, motto and the relation's emblem. The portrait goes **beside** the headline only when the panel is wide; at 1000x600 the story column is ~295 px, so the quote spans the full width under the row. Accept stays the only primary. The Declare War dialog is unchanged.
  - **Race:** titles and milestones are ringed medallions (`title-*`, `milestone-*` icons at 32-36 px): gilt with the holder's crest pinned once held or earned (a milestone also gets a check when you earned it), a hollow ring while open; reached-by lists show crests with their seat badge. Star dividers separate Titles, Great Works and Milestones. The expanded panel's head is sticky, so the auto-scroll to the titles can't slice it off (a soft fade above the pinned footer says the rest scrolls). Eliminated empires stay out (`livingPlayers`).
- **Map overlays stay translucent** (user, 2026-10-04: the Japan roots were "a little too dark"; "you can see more of the map under it"). Faction ornaments must let the terrain read through, at around 0.5–0.6 opacity, with only a thin accent line stronger.
- **Every build card shows its ongoing cost** (user, 2026-10-04: "from this view I don't see the cost... is upkeep different on some units?"). Show upkeep ("free, 1 of 2 free slots left" or "−1 Gold/turn") and Gold/turn after building, not just the one-time Production and buy price.
- **Yield hovers show where the number comes from** (user, 2026-10-04: "it would be great to see in the hover-over for science where the science is coming from"). A top-bar yield hover lists its main sources with amounts; "Click for the full breakdown" is for the rest.
- **A number shown is the number in force, and a limit that applies is named in the same line** (user, 2026-10-17, on "Coal: 60 stored, 0 per turn from 2 sources", which read as a bug because a hidden cap had zeroed the income; the stockpile is gone since 2026-10-19). Strategic resources are supply: the chip reads "Iron 5 / 6" (in use / supplied) and its tooltip lists every deposit, every user, what builds in progress hold, what is idle and what is spare (`SupplyTip` and `supplySummary` in `ui/SupplyChips.tsx`, pinned by `SupplyChips.test.tsx`). A shortage is never colour alone: the alert mark, the red fraction and "N idle". A build card that needs supply says "Uses 1 Iron" where its price is, and when none is spare it is greyed out with the core's own sentence (`resource_blocker`) and can still be queued behind other work. An idle building says "Idle: no Coal" in the city's building list and a note above it; an unsupplied unit says "No Iron: fights 25% weaker until supply returns".
- **Rewards get a celebration** (user, 2026-10-04: clearing a barbarian camp for +50 Gold "didn't feel like enough of a win"). Any discrete reward a player earns by acting (ruins, cleared camps, captures) gets the celebration popup listing every gain, not just a report line.
- **Eliminated empires leave active lists; they remain only in history and final standings** (user, 2026-10-06, playtest: "they're taking up space and they are no longer relevant to the game"). "Eliminated" is core's `alive: false` on the player (no cities and no Settlers), exposed in the view; never infer it from city count. Active lists are the race, its title compare and tooltip, Great Work and milestone rows, the diplomacy empire list and betrayal records, coalition members and alarms, so no orphan "War"/"Peace" badge outlives the empire. History keeps them: turn reports, the event log, the game-over standings (`buildTracks(view)` in `vp.ts` defaults to every team; the live race passes `{ aliveOnly: true }`). Your own row stays even if your team has fallen. Use `livingPlayers`/`isTeamAlive` in `empires.ts` for any new empire list.
- **Docked panels scroll as one card, with real slack** (8.9.15.1, WebKit): a fixed cap inside a dock panel (the Fighter card's `max-height: 60dvh` head) slices its last line while the dock still has room. Let the dock scroll the whole card. A panel with a "Scroll for more" hint needs about 15 px of slack in WebKit at 1000x600: fitting by 1 px in Chromium latches the hint on, because the hint itself takes 28 px. Specs print scroll and client heights.
- **Lists that don't fit:** a list that can't show every row (e.g. the race panel) either fits all rows or shows whole rows plus a "+N more" control that is never itself clipped. A row sliced by the panel edge reads as a bug, even when scrolling reaches it (6.3 review).
- **One rule, one wording:** the same rule (e.g. when a war starts) must read the same on every surface.
- **A disabled action's reason sits right under it, in one line, in view** (7b review: the Heal tooltip landed far away and the in-panel note was below the fold); the tooltip adds the detail. Build-card effect lines wrap whole, never end in an ellipsis; a "why not" is stated once per card (the clicked red notice replaces the grey line). Messages on the title screen go in the menu's flow above the buttons, never over the footer. "Walls" means only the building; say "bring the city to 0 HP" for city HP.

- **One glyph, one meaning** (Playtest 8 review): the red diamond is NEW only. "Needs a decision" is a round "!" badge; "pending until Ready" is a hollow ring with a gap and the word "pending". Never reuse a mark for a second meaning.
- **Gross totals are labelled as such, in neutral ink:** tile yields read "Tiles: 13 Food, 10 Production (2 Food eaten)", never a bare red "Food 13" that looks like income.
- **The first city panel the player sees (turn 1, idle, population 1) shows a whole build card at scroll 0 at 1000x600.** Long rules go in a tooltip on the phrase they explain, not a paragraph; an idle city scrolls its choice under the sticky head. A territory-bar label stays one line even with "unlocks X" (visible label has no ring; ring is in the tooltip).
- **A relation badge is never cut:** in race rows the name ellipsizes, the badge doesn't; a badge's transparent padding border must not be repainted by a state colour.
- **After a failed load, the control that failed is retitled and disabled** ("Saved game (older version)"), not left to repeat the failure.

- **The AI level controls offer Easy, Normal and Hard only (launch readiness, 2026-11-21; not the user's words).** Training reads the true state in its battle study, so it is a research tool behind a developer switch (a development build with `?training=1`; `lobby.ts::offeredLevels`), never a level in a production picker. A stored Training or `HardSearch` choice opens as Hard. Where the switch is on, its sub-label is "Research tool" and its description says it uses the true positions of nearby units. Never describe a feature in player text without the fact that would change the player's choice. The entry below is history for the dev-only control.
- **(Superseded for players 2026-11-21) The AI level control offered "Training"** (user, 2026-11-07; replaced "Hard + Search (experimental)", user 2026-10-06, `claude/difficulty-search`): a fourth level beside Easy, Normal and Hard, in the all-AI Segmented control (sub-label "Studies each battle") and in each seat's list ("Training (slower turns)"). Every battle is saved so future AIs can learn from it." (`DIFFICULTY_INFO.Training`) under the control, in the flow above the seed (never a tooltip only), and it is saved in the game's setup. - **One improvement, one wording** (8.2 ship fixes): the family is "Quarry, Stone" (improvement, resource), then the place: the queue and plan read "Quarry, Stone · 2 tiles N", the meter "Quarry, Stone N", the picker two lines "Quarry, Stone" over "2 tiles N" (`improvementName` in `improvements.ts`). A report line has no resource: "Mine, 2 tiles NE". Drop "known" jargon from forecasts: the number already includes a known upgrade. A forecast line is at least 12px.
- **A native `<select>` whose longest label is clipped shows a short closed form** and spells the label out only while open (`AiLevelSelect` in `LobbyScreen.tsx`: "Hard + Search" closed, "Hard + Search (experimental)" in the list). The grid column must fit the closed text; check the row total at 1440x900 too (the header rule running past the panel edge means the grid overflowed).
- **No game-type preset bar in the lobby** (user, 2026-10-27: the Free-for-all / Teams / Co-op vs AI / Custom toggles "are not relevant. they just change who is on what team, which is redundant to just clicking and selecting those"). Teams are set only with each seat's Team picker; solo still starts free-for-all and online hosting starts co-op through `newLobby` defaults. Don't add a control that only bulk-rewrites settings each row already exposes (branch `claude/lobby-no-presets`).
- **A new Single Player lobby starts at the map size's default empire count: 6 on Standard, you and 5 AIs (user, 2026-11-03):** "Can we make the default for a normal map 6 players? Not sure why it defaults to 8, then you click on another one and come back and it's 5." Each size has a default in `MAP_SIZES` (Small 4, Standard 6, Large 8); a size change that must raise the count goes to that default, never the minimum. The Co-op (2 humans and 6 AIs) and Teams presets keep 8 seats because their structure needs them, and the add-seat and × controls still reach any count from 2 to 8.
- **Choosing a map size snaps the empire count** (user, 2026-10-06 playtest: clicking Small left 8 seats): Small trims to 4, a count below Standard's minimum of 5 goes to Standard's default of 6, Large sets 8, and a count already in range is left alone (`setMapSize` in `lobby.ts`). Only default AI seats are trimmed, from the bottom; humans, named or faction-chosen AIs are never removed. The "maps suit" warning appears only when the player then goes outside the range by hand.

- **Clicks never shift content under the pointer:** confirmations appear in place or outside the list (user, 2026-10-06, Playtest 8.2: "+ Queue" inserted a line and the second click landed in the wrong place). The Queue button itself says "Queued #2" for 1.5 s, the order badge is pinned to the card's corner out of the flow, and a list growing above the pointer scrolls the panel by the same amount (`keepInPlace` in `CityPanel.tsx`); the panel sets `overflow-anchor: none` so the browser's own scroll anchoring cannot add a second jump, and the idle-picker scroll runs in a layout effect so the first paint is already scrolled (8.9.29 release failures). Test it by clicking twice in a row and comparing bounding boxes (`e2e/queue-click.spec.ts`).
- **Editable lists look editable:** visible reorder and remove controls on every row, never only on hover. The city's production queue is its own section under "Now producing" (icon, name, turns, up, down, ×), with a one-line "Empty · add from the list below" when empty. Say "queue" everywhere (not "plan").
- **Each number once per card** (8.3 review): a piece card shows stats as icon-number pairs, and its text carries only what the pairs can't (requirements, starts promoted, replaces X). Never repeat "40 Production, 2 moves" in prose under the pairs.
- **A clause never ends in a bare verb or state word** ("Later: Irrigated Farm, +1 Food · unlocks"). End at the number, or name what it unlocks.
- **A level's label matches everywhere, spacing included:** the all-AI segment and the seat select both say "Hard + Search"; a segment label keeps at least 8 px of gutter at 1440x900 (wrap or shrink it rather than cramp it).

- **A list opened inside a card names what it acts on** in its own sticky head (8.9.4 review: the where-to-build list scrolled its item's card title away, leaving Build buttons with no subject). `.wtb[open] > summary` sticks under the city head (`--cp-head-h`) and leads with the item's name. One-per-empire items (Great Works, the Yam Relay Station) being built elsewhere read "Building in Khanbaliq · 14 turns left" (`oncePerEmpire`), never "Fastest: Khanbaliq ... already being built".
- **Sticky stacks:** a panel's auto-scroll subtracts every sticky element above its target (head, warnings, chips), not just the head (8.3b: the Sparta desertion warning covered the first title).
- **Pinned badges and emblems never sit on a text line:** a corner badge (queue order, treaty emblem) gets reserved space, or it overprints text at one of the two sizes. Check both.
- **Emblem colour follows tone:** red marks (cross, ring) are for war or loss only; Alliance and Peace emblems use ink or gilt.
- **A goal's progress is a meter with its number,** never italic prose, including faction goals in the race. City HP healing gets a meter under Health, with the heal line as its label.

- **Yield amounts carry their icon everywhere** (user, 2026-10-06, on a Laboratory card's italic "+3 Science"): any amount of a yield or resource (Food, Production, Gold, Science, VP, HP, population) shows as signed number plus icon, the way costs already show "146 ⚙". This covers effect lines, unlock cards, tooltips, report lines and forecasts. One shared component renders it, so no screen hand-writes "+3 Science". The word stays in the accessible name and tooltip. Implementation: `app/src/ui/Amounts.tsx` (`Amount`, `Quantity`, `YieldAmounts`, and `Amounts` for prose, which finds "+3 Science"/"146 Production"/"+2 VP" in a string; the parsing rules and their tests live in `ui/amountParts.ts`). `Tip` runs its string content and summary through `Amounts` itself. Wrap any string a screen prints (effect lines, report lines, warnings) in `<Amounts text=…/>`; native `<option>`s and aria-labels stay plain words. HP and population have no icon in the set, so they stay words. Guide paragraphs stay prose; its definition lists, faction cards and headings use icons.

- **Every way to score is visible, with ownership unmistakable, and no suggestions** (user, 2026-10-06). At 9/10 VP: "I have no idea how to get one more point." On rejecting all three round-1 mocks: "all paths to get victory points should be easily visible, and it should be clear which ones I have, which ones other people have, and which ones nobody has. I don't need the suggested way to get more VPs, I should be able to figure that out myself."

  - Never show a recommended route, a "fastest way" or a near-victory nudge. Rejected 2026-10-06: the round-1 variants A, B and C (the next-points strip, the paths drawer, the near-victory prompt).
