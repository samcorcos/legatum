# 3D map rock surface

`map3d/rock-strata.webp` is a 512 px resize and WebP encode of the [Rock 06](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_06) diffuse map. Author: Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven. Licence: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Original 1K JPEG: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rock_06/rock_06_diff_1k.jpg. The renderer samples it on three world axes and supplies its own neutral rock palette and horizontal strata. Shipped size: about 104 KB.
