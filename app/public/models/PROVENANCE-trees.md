# 3D map trees

The seven GLB files in `trees/` come from **Kenney, Nature Kit**. Author: Kenney. Licence: **Creative Commons CC0 1.0**. Original pack and licence: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit and https://kenney.nl/support.

Files: `tree-oak.glb`, `tree-detailed.glb`, `tree-default.glb`, `tree-plateau.glb`, `tree-pinedefaulta.glb`, `tree-pinedefaultb.glb`, `tree-pinerounda.glb`.

The exact GLB downloads were retrieved from a public mirror of that pack: https://github.com/Hidencod/tge-assets/tree/main/packs/nature-kit. The renderer flattens their meshes to instanced vertex-coloured geometry and applies Legatum's own foliage palette. Source models total about 132 KB. No textures are included.
