Renaissance era film: shots from the Gemini API (model gemini-omni-1.2-flash, Interactions API, 16:9, 720p, 8 s, with generated sound), 2026-11-27. The film uses shot 1 v2 (trimmed and cropped) and shot 2.

Shot 1 v1 had rooftop satellite dishes and a made-up cluster of domes. v2 fixed the cathedral, but its first 4.5 s still had dishes, TV aerials and skylights on the rooftops, so it was trimmed and cropped:
  ffmpeg -ss 4.5 -i shot1-city.mp4 -vf "crop=1120:630:160:0,scale=1280:720:flags=lanczos,setsar=1" -c:v libx264 -crf 16 -c:a aac -b:a 192k s1.mp4
Cut (s1.mp4 is 3.54 s long):
  ffmpeg -i s1.mp4 -i shot2-piazza.mp4 -filter_complex "[0:v]fade=t=in:st=0:d=0.6,settb=AVTB[v0];[1:v]settb=AVTB[v1];[v0][v1]xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.6:offset=2.942,fade=t=out:st=10.042:d=0.9,format=yuv420p[v];[0:a][1:a]acrossfade=d=0.6,afade=t=in:d=0.6,afade=t=out:st=9.842:d=1.1[a]" -map "[v]" -map "[a]" -c:v libx264 -crf 25 -movflags +faststart -c:a aac -b:a 96k cut.mp4
  ffmpeg -i cut.mp4 -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 27 -movflags +faststart -c:a copy renaissance.mp4
The 720p shot masters are archived outside git.
