# Painted resource medallions

Chosen by the designer on 2026-10-10: use the painted-medallion set for Wheat, Cattle, Stone, Copper, Iron, and Coal. Niter, Oil, and Silicon joined the set on 2026-10-24. Silicon depicts high-purity quartz for chips. These images replace the abstract resource glyphs in the map, Resources lens context, tile and city UI, production needs, supply chips, and Guide.

Each resource has prepared 256 px, 64 px, and 32 px PNGs. The map uses the appropriate source for the device-pixel output size and caches a high-quality downscale. UI surfaces use the 64 px image at their requested display size. The strategic red hairline remains over the painted frame; at far map zoom the existing high-contrast bonus/strategic category marks remain in place. Their palette and the Resources lens key are shared through `map/style.ts`.

The original six were adopted from the painted-medallions folder of docs/design/resource-art (its images are archived outside the repository) on branch `astra/resource-art-variants`. That exploration README contains their generation prompt, preparation notes, and contact-sheet comparison. The shipped raster files live here so app builds do not depend on design-document paths.

## Niter, Oil, and Silicon provenance

The three additions were generated with the Codex OpenAI image-generation tool on 2026-10-24, using the shipped Stone, Iron, Copper, Coal, and Wheat PNGs as style, frame, and scale references. Three variants were generated for each; the third set was chosen after the art-distinctness check found the earlier versions too close to other resources at small sizes. Silicon's third variant received one targeted brightness edit. An antialiased circular matte removed stray pixels outside the frame, then the selected originals were downscaled with Pillow LANCZOS to 256, 64, and 32 px. The alternate originals remain outside the repository in the Codex generated-images directory. The exact selected prompts are in `niter.prompt.txt`, `oil.prompt.txt`, and `silicon.prompt.txt`. The images are AI-generated art covered by the repository's `LICENSE-ART` (CC BY 4.0 to the extent rights exist).

## Prompt

> Transparent-background resource medallions, no text. Sophisticated historical strategy game atlas aesthetic: thin restrained antique brass plus dark ink circular ring, painterly oil and watercolor, elegant old-master naturalism, nuanced visible brushwork, parchment-compatible muted earth pigments. Subjects: golden tied sheaf of wheat; pair of tawny cattle; stacked pale limestone blocks; rough copper ore nugget with subtle verdigris; **Iron:** low, wide horizontal short pyramid of three forged trapezoid iron ingots with sloped ends, cool steel blue-grey with bright edge highlights, in front of oxblood and burnt-sienna hematite with ochre rust, on a warm mid-light rust/terracotta field; **Coal:** tall round mound of six rounded glossy faceted near-black lumps with cool blue sheen and blue-white facets, on a very dark indigo/blue-black slate field with subtle blue rim light. Bold recognizable subjects, legible at 32 px; iron's lighter field, brighter bars and flat silhouette remain distinct from coal's darker field and round mound in grayscale. Transparent everywhere outside rings with antialiased edges. No anvil, no ingots in Coal, no brown or rust in Coal, no round lumps instead of bars in Iron, no glows, gradients, bevels, stickers, cartoon style, external shadows, borders between cells, words, letters, symbols.
